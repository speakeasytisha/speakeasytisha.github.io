SPEAKING FOUNDATION

Build a clear professional introduction.

A strong introduction is not a long speech. It is a short sequence of clear ideas that you can reuse with a colleague, partner, visitor or during a professional event.

GRAMMAR FOUNDATIONS

Three structures that immediately make your English clearer.

We are not learning grammar for grammar’s sake. These three structures help you talk about who you are, what you do, what you did, and what you want next.

01

BE = identity / situation

I am interested in heritage.
We are ready for the meeting.
The exhibition is open.

Subject + am / is / are
02

Present simple = regular / general

I work with partners.
I organise projects.
The museum opens at 10.

I/you/we/they + verb · he/she/it + verb-s
03

Past simple = finished past

I worked in another sector.
I studied modern literature.
I completed a master’s degree.

Finished past → verb-ed / irregular form
Do not translate word by word. English often needs a subject + a verb where French may use a noun-heavy structure.
BE = identité/situation. Present simple = habitudes, faits, activité générale. Past simple = période ou action terminée.

REAL PROFESSIONAL SITUATION

Meet a new cultural partner.

The goal is not to impress. The goal is to open the conversation, explain your direction, ask a simple question and keep the exchange moving.

Open

Hello, nice to meet you. Thank you for taking the time to meet me.

Introduce

I am moving into the cultural sector and I am especially interested in heritage and cultural projects.

Connect

I would like to learn more about how you work with local partners and visitors.

Ask

What kinds of projects are you working on at the moment?

Clarify

Sorry, could you explain that in a different way?

Close

Thank you. That was very useful for me.

Une interaction professionnelle peut rester simple : saluer → se présenter → créer un lien → poser une question → demander une précision → conclure.

CREATE · BUILD · SPEAK

Curate a mini cultural project.

You will create something of your own, then use it to practise English. This turns vocabulary into language you can actually remember and reuse.

WHAT?

The project, exhibition, event or heritage visit.

WHO?

WHY?

FIRST PROFESSIONAL TOOLKIT

Join a meeting without needing advanced English.

For now, focus on four useful functions: understand, give an opinion, ask a question, and negotiate one simple point.

Understand

Could you repeat that, please?

Could you speak a little more slowly?

Do you mean…?

Give an opinion

I think this could work.

For me, the priority is…

I prefer the second option.

Ask

What is the deadline?

Who is responsible for this part?

What budget is available?

Negotiate

Could we change the date?

Could we look at another option?

Is there any flexibility on the budget?

Dans une réunion, vous n’avez pas besoin de tout comprendre ou de faire de longues phrases. Ces quatre fonctions donnent déjà beaucoup d’autonomie.

LILATE IA · SMALL STEP

One professional mission — not a full mock exam.

The LILATE IA evaluates how you use English in professional situations. Today, we only practise one small interaction so the format becomes familiar without taking over the lesson.

IA

What matters today

The test includes professional situations that require you to understand information and respond in writing or orally. A useful first skill is welcoming or responding to an interlocutor clearly.

On ne fait pas un examen blanc aujourd’hui. On commence par une seule compétence : comprendre une demande professionnelle et répondre clairement.
