11 · QUICK GRAMMAR WIN

Keeping your story in the past: was / were

Once a story starts in the past, every verb stays there — this small habit makes a big difference.

5 min
one person / thing

___ + was

Singular subjects.

The hotel was lovely.
It was a great trip.
plural / you / we / they

___ + were

Plural subjects, or "you".

There were a lot of tourists.
We were really happy.

Make it personal
