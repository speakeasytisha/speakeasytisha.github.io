Cardinals, ordinals, dates, times and prices

Learn the rules before listening.

CORE — learn these.The blue “Recognition only” box later is optional.

The ordinal rule

1 → first → 1stpremier / première
2 → second → 2nddeuxième
3 → third → 3rdtroisième
4 → fourth → 4thquatrième
5 → fifth → 5thcinquième
8 → eighth → 8thhuitième
9 → ninth → 9thneuvième
12 → twelfth → 12thdouzième
Most numbers use -th. 4th, 6th, 7th, 10th, 11th, 12th…
But: numbers ending in 1, 2, 3 normally use st / nd / rd: 21st, 22nd, 23rd.
Important exception: 11th, 12th, 13th.
20th / 30th / 40th: twentieth / thirtieth / fortieth. Then 21st = twenty-first.

04 · GRAMMAR FOR REAL QUESTIONS

Ask, check and confirm

One grammar focus only: direct questions and polite information questions.

15 min

DIRECT + NATURAL

What time does it start?

Rule: question word + auxiliary + subject + base verb.

À quelle heure est-ce que ça commence ?

POLITE EVERYDAY

Could you tell me what time it starts?

Rule: after Could you tell me…, use normal statement word order.

Pourriez-vous me dire à quelle heure ça commence ?

CHECK WHAT YOU HEARD

Did you say 7:30?

Use this instead of pretending you understood.

Vous avez dit 19 h 30 ?

CONFIRM

So that's the 12th floor, right?

Repeat the key information and check it.

Donc c’est bien le 12e étage, c’est ça ?

07 · SHORT REAL-LIFE WRITING

One clear message, not an essay

10 min

HOTEL

Confirm your late arrival

Your reservation is for May 21st. You expect to arrive at 10:45 p.m. Ask whether reception will still be open.

Reservation date → expected arrival → question → thanks.
Model · Everyday natural

Hi, I have a reservation for May 21st. I expect to arrive around 10:45 p.m. Will reception still be open at that time? Thanks very much.

Model · Polished but usable

Hello, I have a reservation for May 21st and I expect to arrive at approximately 10:45 p.m. Could you please confirm whether reception will still be open when I arrive? Thank you.

RESTAURANT

Move your reservation

Your table is at 7:15 p.m. Ask whether it can be moved to 7:45 p.m.

Current time → requested time → polite question.
Model · Everyday natural

Hi, we have a reservation for 7:15. Would it be possible to move it to 7:45 instead? Thanks!

Model · Polished but usable

Hello, we have a reservation for 7:15 this evening. Would it be possible to move the reservation to 7:45? Thank you very much for your help.

STEP 1 · LISTEN

“Hi, just to confirm your reservation: your table is booked for May twenty-third at seven-forty-five. We’re at 128 West Forty-second Street. Please arrive about ten minutes early.”

STEP 2 · RESPOND

Confirm the information and ask one practical follow-up question.

Model · Everyday natural

Great, thank you. So that’s May 23rd at 7:45, at 128 West 42nd Street. Do we need to bring the reservation confirmation with us?

Model · Polished but usable

Thank you. Just to confirm, the reservation is for May 23rd at 7:45 p.m., at 128 West 42nd Street. Could you tell me whether we need to bring a copy of the reservation confirmation?
