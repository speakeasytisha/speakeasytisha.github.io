Quick grammar reminder: AT / ON / IN for time

AT

at 3:00 p.m. · at noon · at night

heure précise
ON

jour / date
IN

in May · in spring · in 2027 · in the afternoon

mois / saison / année / partie de la journée

May in New York = spring. U.S. dates are often written May 12, 2027. In speech: May twelfth.

03 · GRAMMAR WIN #1

Describe a hotel: HAVE / INCLUDE / BE INCLUDED

Three structures you can reuse when comparing hotels.

Full forms

HAVE / HAS

The hotel has a gym.

Affirmative: It has a gym.

Negative: It doesn't have a pool.

Question: Does it have room service?

INCLUDE

The rate includes breakfast.

Affirmative: The rate includes Wi-Fi.

Negative: The rate doesn't include breakfast.

Question: What does the rate include?

BE INCLUDED

Breakfast is included.

Affirmative: Wi-Fi is included.

Negative: Breakfast isn't included.

Question: Is breakfast included?

Meaning: The rate includes breakfast and Breakfast is included in the rate describe the same fact from two different angles.

06 · ARRIVAL & CHECK-IN

From the lobby to the room

Understand the credit-card language before you stand at the front desk.

Dialogue + payment
depositcaution / somme versée ou bloquée

Money used to guarantee the booking or cover possible charges.

security depositcaution de garantie

A refundable amount for possible extras or damage.

authorization holdempreinte / préautorisation bancaire

The hotel temporarily blocks an amount on your card; it is not necessarily a final charge.

incidentalsfrais supplémentaires

Extras such as food, minibar, services or other room charges.

prepaymentpaiement à l’avance

Money actually paid before arrival.

balancesolde restant

The amount still due.

Important: “down payment” exists in English, but for a hotel you will more often hear deposit, prepayment, security deposit, incidental hold or card authorization.
Reception

Hi! Welcome. Could I see your photo ID and the card you’d like to use for the room?

You

Of course. Is the room already paid for, or is there a balance due?

Reception

The room is prepaid, but we do need a card for an incidental hold.

You

Could you tell me how much the hold is and when it will be released?

Your check-in

Confirm your name, room type, breakfast, check-out time and the card hold.

Model · Everyday natural

Hi, I have a reservation for two people. I booked a king room with a city view. Could you confirm whether breakfast is included? And what time is check-out? Also, will there be a security hold on my card?

Model · Extra polite / luxury style

Good afternoon. I have a reservation for two guests and I believe I booked a king room with a city view. Could you please confirm whether breakfast is included in my rate and what the check-out time is? I’d also be grateful if you could explain any security deposit or card authorization required at check-in.

07 · GRAMMAR WIN #2

Phrasal verbs: what do IN, OUT, UP, DOWN, BACK and THROUGH suggest?

Particles often give a direction or completion idea — but they are patterns, not perfect mathematical rules.

Verb + small word
IN

enter / begin

check in · go in
OUT

exit / finish / complete

check out · go out · fill out
UP

higher / prepare / complete

go up · send up · set up · wake up
DOWN

lower / toward a lower floor

go down · call down
BACK

return

come back · give back
THROUGH

from beginning to end / through a process

go through the details
Do not translate the small word alone. Learn the whole verb as a unit: check out = leave a hotel and finish the account; run out of = have no more of something.

10 · CHECK-OUT GRAMMAR MASTERCLASS

Past Simple vs Present Perfect

You need both to talk about a hotel stay and write a convincing review.

60 questions · staged
A Past Simple rules → 20 exercisesB Present Perfect rules → 20 exercisesC Contrast → 20 mixed exercisesD Write your review

11 · PAST SIMPLE

Finished actions in finished past time

20 exercises

AFFIRMATIVE

subject + past form

We checked in at 3 p.m.
The room was quiet.

NEGATIVE

subject + didn't + base verb

We didn't use room service.
The room wasn't noisy.

QUESTION

Did + subject + base verb?

Did you enjoy breakfast?
Was the room clean?

Use Past Simple when the time is finished or stated: yesterday · last night · on Tuesday · in 2025 · when we arrived · during our stay last May.
Useful irregulars: go → went · have → had · get → got · find → found · bring → brought · leave → left · pay → paid · take → took · be → was/were

12 · PRESENT PERFECT

Past + connection to now

20 exercises

AFFIRMATIVE

have / has + past participle

We have stayed here twice.
The hotel has confirmed the booking.

NEGATIVE

haven't / hasn't + participle

We haven't had breakfast yet.

QUESTION

Have / Has + subject + participle?

Have you ever stayed here?
Has the hotel replied?

Use Present Perfect for: experience with no finished time · a recent/current result · an unfinished period · since / for · already / just / yet / ever / never / so far.
American English note — recognition only: in everyday U.S. speech you may also hear Past Simple with already / just / yet in situations where a British textbook strongly prefers Present Perfect. Learn the core distinction first.

13 · PAST SIMPLE OR PRESENT PERFECT?

Find the time signal first

20 mixed exercises
Finished time?last year · yesterday · at 8:00 · in 2025→ PAST SIMPLE
No finished time / result now?ever · never · already · yet · so far · since · for→ PRESENT PERFECT

We stayed there last year. → finished time.

We have stayed there three times. → experience, no finished time.

I lost my key last night. → tells the past event.

I have lost my key. → the key is missing now.
