10 · WRITING TRANSFER

Short, useful writing

Real life + certification

Task A · Travel

Message to a hotel

You arrive before check-in. Ask whether you can leave your luggage, confirm the check-in time, and ask about transport from the hotel to Midtown.

Model · Level 1

Hello, I have a reservation for next week. I will arrive before check-in. Could I leave my luggage at the hotel? What time can I check in? Also, what is the easiest way to get to Midtown? Thank you for your help.

Model · Level 2

Hello, I’m staying at your hotel next week and I expect to arrive around 11 a.m. As this is before the normal check-in time, would it be possible to leave my luggage at reception? Could you also confirm the check-in time and recommend the easiest way to travel from the hotel to Midtown? Many thanks in advance.

Task B · Organising

Confirm an appointment

Write a short message confirming an appointment on Tuesday at 2:30 p.m. Ask the person to arrive 10 minutes early and bring the necessary document.

Model · Level 1

Hello, your appointment is confirmed for Tuesday at 2:30 p.m. Please arrive 10 minutes early and remember to bring the document with you. If you have a problem, please let me know. See you on Tuesday.

Model · Level 2

Hello, I’m writing to confirm your appointment for Tuesday at 2:30 p.m. Could you please arrive about 10 minutes early and bring the requested document with you? If you need to change the time or have any questions, please let me know as soon as possible.

11 · CERTIFICATION BRIDGE

Turn everyday English into exam performance

No panic · one strategy at a time
1 · Understand the task

Who? Where? What do they want from you?

2 · Give the direct answer

Answer first. Do not hide your answer inside a long sentence.

3 · Add one useful detail

Reason, example, time, place, preference or solution.

4 · Keep speaking

Use a connector: because, so, but, however, for example, actually.

Timed speaking

You arrive at a hotel and your room is not ready.

Explain the problem, ask for one solution, then ask one follow-up question.

01:00
Model · Level 1

Hi, I have a reservation, but my room isn’t ready yet. Could I leave my luggage here while I wait? I’d like to go for lunch nearby. What time do you think the room will be ready?

Model · Level 2

Hi, I’ve just arrived and I understand that my room isn’t ready yet. Would it be possible to leave my luggage at reception while I go out for lunch? I’m happy to come back later. Do you have an approximate time for when the room should be available?
