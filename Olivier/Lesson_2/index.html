01 · POSITIONING BEFORE WRITING

Your profile needs one clear strategic message.

You are not presenting a chronology. You are helping a founder, recruiter or healthcare partner understand your level, evidence and transfer value in seconds.

Ne racontez pas tout dans l'ordre. Faites comprendre immédiatement votre niveau, vos preuves et la valeur transférable vers votre prochain environnement.

Positioning framework

Use: I bring + experience/expertise + to + business value. Present simple describes your current strengths; past simple describes completed achievements. Avoid claiming future outcomes as accomplished facts.

02 · LINKEDIN & RÉSUMÉ POWER VERBS

Learn the verb before you are asked to use it.

Every strong verb below has a specific meaning. Choose it only when it accurately matches the evidence.

LEARN → NOTICE → PRACTISE → APPLY

Past-simple action verbs

Résumé bullets usually start with a past-tense action verb for completed work: led, built, improved, launched, coordinated. Use present tense only for current work. Strong verbs do not justify unverified achievements.

Clear model: I led [verified initiative] across [verified scope], resulting in [verified outcome].

Check understanding: Choose the correct résumé opening.

LED

lead

Meaning: direct people, activity or change.

Use when: you owned decisions and results.

“Led two healthcare sites through integration.”

LAUNCHED

launch

Meaning: start a new service or activity.

Use when: something genuinely new became operational.

“Launched an emergency unit and MRI service.”

SECURED

secure

Meaning: successfully obtain something important.

Use when: accreditation, authorization or agreement was achieved.

“Secured high-level quality accreditation.”

INTEGRATED

integrate

Meaning: bring structures, teams or operations together.

Use when: you combined organizations or systems.

“Integrated two sites into a new group structure.”

REOPENED

reopen

Meaning: restore a service after closure or disruption.

Use when: activity returned to operation.

“Reopened services after the COVID period.”

BUILT

build

Meaning: create a structure, capability or facility.

Use when: you developed something substantial.

“Built a new surgical center.”

EXPANDED

expand

Meaning: increase scope, capacity or services.

Use when: the organization became broader or stronger.

“Expanded the service offering and specialist network.”

ALIGNED

align

Meaning: bring stakeholders around one objective.

Use when: different priorities needed coordination.

“Aligned clinical and operational stakeholders.”

05 · LINKEDIN ABOUT SECTION

Tell a leadership story — not a chronology.

The About section needs four moves: identity → evidence → transfer value → next chapter.

LEARN → NOTICE → PRACTISE → APPLY

Four-paragraph narrative

Paragraph 1: present identity (I bring / I specialize in). Paragraph 2: documented experience (I led / I managed). Paragraph 3: transferable skills (This experience has taught me…). Paragraph 4: future aim (I am interested in / I aim to). Present perfect connects accumulated experience to now; past simple names completed examples.

Clear model: I have developed operational expertise over my career. In a previous role, I led a transformation initiative. I am now interested in applying that experience in MedTech.

Check understanding: Choose the strongest accurate tense pairing.

Achievement formula

Verb + action + scope + substantiated result. Use past simple for completed projects. Numbers are optional; never fabricate percentages, budget sizes or patient volumes. When an outcome is unmeasured, explain the legitimate operational purpose without making up success.

08 · ISRAEL STARTUP TRANSFER

Explain why an operator matters inside innovation.

You do not need to present yourself as a software engineer or Israeli regulatory expert. Your differentiated value is the operational layer between product ambition and real healthcare adoption.

Votre valeur différenciante : le passage entre une bonne idée, la réalité hospitalière et une mise en œuvre crédible.

LEARN → NOTICE → PRACTISE → APPLY

Responsible transition language

Use transferable rather than identical experience. Prefer could, may and would when proposing a future contribution. Your hospital leadership gives practical insights, not automatic proof that a product will succeed in Israel.

Clear model: My experience could help the team understand clinical workflows and implementation risks.
