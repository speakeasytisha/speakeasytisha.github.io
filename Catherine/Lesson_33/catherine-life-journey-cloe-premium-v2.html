1

Where does the time word go?

Usually goes before the main verb: I usually run twice a week.

With be, place it after am/is/are: I am currently planning my holiday.

2

Where does the reason go?

Use because + subject + verb near the end: …because I want the children to enjoy their visit.

Use so for the result: The house was cold, so we improved the insulation.

03

GRAMMAR RUNWAY

Choose the tense from the meaning

Review only the grammar you need to tell your real story clearly.

Present simple and present continuous

Routine: I usually run twice a week.

Around now / arrangement: My grandchildren are coming to visit me.

Past simple and past continuous

Finished event: We went to a corn maze last weekend.

Background: While we were walking, the children were laughing.

Present perfect

Result now: I have had the roof renovated.

Unfinished decision: I have not chosen a destination yet.

Future forms

Plan: I am going to take my holiday in September.

Possibility: I might go somewhere warm.

My daughter and I / my daughter and me

Before the verb: My daughter and I went together.

After a verb or preposition: The guide spoke to my daughter and me.

MODEL ANSWERS

Choose the level that helps you most

A2 → B1+

A2 model — short and safe

My grandchildren are coming to visit me, and I am very happy. They are three and five years old. I am going to organise simple activities for them. I am also going on holiday in September, but I have not decided where to go yet. I might choose somewhere warm and relaxing. These plans are important because I want to spend time with my family and enjoy my holiday.

B1 model — connected and detailed

My grandchildren are coming to visit me soon, and I am really looking forward to spending time with them. Because they are three and five years old, I am going to prepare simple activities that they can enjoy. I am also planning to take my holiday in September. However, I have not chosen a destination or booked accommodation yet. I might go somewhere warm and peaceful, but I am still comparing different options. These plans are important to me because I want to make the most of my family time and enjoy a relaxing break.

B1+ model — nuanced and personal

Over the next few months, I have two enjoyable projects to look forward to. First, my young grandchildren are coming to stay, so I am preparing age-appropriate activities that will keep them entertained without becoming too tiring. Later, I intend to take a holiday in September. Although the dates are decided, the destination remains open, and I may choose a quiet coastal location or somewhere warmer. These plans represent an important balance for me: meaningful time with my family, followed by an opportunity to relax, discover a new place and enjoy this stage of my life.

CLOE COMMUNICATION MISSIONS

React, explain, ask and reformulate

Complete four realistic tasks using the language from the whole lesson.

MISSION 01

Family phone call

Your relative asks what you are planning for the children’s visit. Explain the plan and ask one follow-up question.

They are coming on Friday, so I am planning a quiet activity and an outdoor activity. What time do you expect to arrive?

MISSION 02

Contractor update

Reformulate the contractor’s message about the roof and skylights.

The contractor explained that the roof work was complete and that the insulation around the skylights had been improved. I need to contact them if I notice moisture.

MISSION 03

Holiday enquiry

Call a travel agency. Explain what you want and ask about price, accommodation and cancellation.

I am looking for a relaxing holiday in September. Could you tell me what is included, how much it costs and whether I can cancel?

MISSION 04

Personal story

Answer: “What recent experience has been important to you?” Use past, present and future.

Recently, I spent time with my grandchildren at a corn maze. Family time is very important to me now, and I am already planning more activities for their next visit.
