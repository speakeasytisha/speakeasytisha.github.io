Step 3

Pronouns: keep your story clear

Pronouns replace names and nouns. The most important question is: Does the person do the action, receive the action, or show possession?

The rule Catherine specifically needs

Before the verb: my daughter and I

My daughter and I visited Denmark.

Remove “my daughter and”: I visited is correct.
After a verb/preposition: my daughter and me

The guide spoke to my daughter and me.

Remove “my daughter and”: the guide spoke to me.

Mission 1 · Who are you now?

Choose one option in every dropdown. Click Build. Then change at least one detail so the paragraph becomes yours.

A2 model

I am retired now. I work as an assessor in my free time. I usually run twice a week. My family is very important to me.

B1 model

I am retired now, but I still have an active life. I work as an assessor in my free time, and I usually run twice a week. I am currently preparing another trip to Denmark because spending time with my family is important to me.

B1+ model

Although I am retired, I have chosen to keep an active and meaningful routine. I work as an assessor in my free time and regularly go running. At the moment, I am also planning another visit to Denmark, which will allow me to spend quality time with my family.

Mission 2 · A person who matters to you

Choose the person, relationship, quality, shared activity and influence. The builder will select the correct pronouns automatically.

A2 model

My son is very important to me. He lives in Denmark. I visit him when I can. His family is also important to me.

B1 model

One person who is very important to me is my son. He lives in Denmark with his family, and I enjoy spending time with them. I admire him because he is independent, and our relationship has taught me that family connections can remain strong across distance.

B1+ model

My son has played an important role in my life. Although he lives in Denmark, we have maintained a close relationship, and I value the time I spend with him and his family. His independence has made me proud, while our relationship has shown me that distance does not have to weaken a strong family bond.

Mission 3 · What made you who you are?

Choose a past experience, what it taught you, how you have changed and who you are now.

A2 model

My family made me who I am. I learned to be patient. I am stronger now. Today, I try to help other people.

B1 model

Raising my family has had a strong influence on me. It taught me to be patient, adaptable and responsible. Over the years, I have become more confident and thoughtful. Today, I try to use these qualities when I support young people as an assessor.

B1+ model

The experience of raising a family while managing professional responsibilities has profoundly shaped me. It taught me to adapt, listen and remain calm when situations became difficult. Over time, I have developed greater confidence and a stronger sense of fairness, qualities that now guide me in my role as an assessor.

Final writing check

Speaking

Turn guided writing into confident speech

30 seconds

Read your Mission 1 text once. Hide it. Say the opening, one routine and one reason.

I am retired now, but I still have an active life. I work as an assessor in my free time, and I usually run twice a week because staying active is important to me.

45 seconds

Use Mission 2. Mention the person, pronouns, one shared activity and one influence.

My son is very important to me. He lives in Denmark, and I visit him when I can. I enjoy spending time with him and his family, and our relationship has taught me that family bonds can remain strong across distance.

60 seconds

Use Mission 3. Explain the past, what changed and who you are now.

Managing family and professional responsibilities has shaped me. It taught me to be patient and adaptable. Since then, I have become more confident and thoughtful. Today, I use these qualities in my role as an assessor and try to support young people fairly.
