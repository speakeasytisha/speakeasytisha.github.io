01 · A NEW CHALLENGE · 5 MIN

You know so much. Now let's use it.

You have already worked on storytelling, grammar, travel, family, youth justice, nature, opinions and connectors. Today we will not repeat those courses. We'll practise turning what you know into a conversation that keeps moving.

↗

Give the conversation back

React to what you hear and ask a real follow-up question.

THE GOAL IS COMMUNICATION, NOT A PERFECT RECITED PARAGRAPH

Less natural: I like running. Nature is good. Families are busy.

More natural: Well, I enjoy running because it helps me clear my head. For example, I often feel better after being outdoors. What about you?

06 · THE UNREHEARSED MINUTE · 5 MIN

Now make it your conversation.

No need to start with a perfect sentence. Choose a new prompt. Try 30–60 seconds with no full script, then ask your partner a question. Use the support only if necessary.
