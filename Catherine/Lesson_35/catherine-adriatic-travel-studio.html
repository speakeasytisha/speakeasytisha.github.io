2 · DESTINATION KNOWLEDGE

Travel facts you can actually say

Use the facts as speaking material, not as a geography test.

🏛️ Dubrovnik

🧱
The Old City is surrounded by famous stone walls that are almost two kilometres long.
La vieille ville est entourée de remparts de presque deux kilomètres.
👣
You can explore the historic centre on foot, walk along the walls and look down over the red roofs and the Adriatic Sea.
On peut découvrir le centre historique à pied et marcher sur les remparts.
🌊
Dubrovnik combines culture and sea views, which makes it easy to describe using relative clauses.
Dubrovnik combine culture et vues sur la mer.

🌲 Mljet

🏞️
Mljet National Park is in the western part of the island and is known for two saltwater lakes: Veliko and Malo Jezero.
Le parc national est connu pour ses deux lacs salés.
⛪
On St Mary’s islet, in the Great Lake, there is a Benedictine monastery that you can reach by boat.
Un monastère bénédictin se trouve sur un îlot du Grand Lac.
🚲
It is a place where you can walk, cycle, kayak or simply enjoy the forest and the water.
C'est un endroit où l'on peut marcher, faire du vélo ou du kayak.

🌊 Bay of Kotor

⛰️
The Bay of Kotor is surrounded by steep mountains and historic coastal towns.
La baie est entourée de montagnes abruptes et de villes historiques.
⛵
It is an area where you can take boat trips, kayak, walk and explore places such as Kotor and Perast.
On peut y faire du bateau, du kayak, marcher et visiter Kotor ou Perast.
🏘️
Kotor has a walled old town, which may appeal to you if you enjoy history as well as scenery.
La vieille ville fortifiée peut vous plaire si vous aimez l'histoire et les paysages.

⛰️ Durmitor National Park

🥾
Durmitor is a mountain national park where you can find hiking routes for different levels.
Durmitor est un parc de montagne avec des randonnées de niveaux variés.
🖤
Black Lake, near Žabljak, is one of the best-known places for an accessible walk and mountain views.
Le Lac Noir est un lieu connu pour marcher et profiter des montagnes.
🏞️
The Tara River runs through a deep canyon where rafting and other outdoor activities are possible.
La rivière Tara traverse un canyon profond où l'on peut notamment faire du rafting.
