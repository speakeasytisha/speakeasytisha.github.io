2 · DESTINATION KNOWLEDGE

Travel facts you can actually use in conversation

Learn enough factual English to speak freely without turning the lesson into a geography test.

🇵🇹 The Azores / Açores

9️⃣
Nine islands: São Miguel, Terceira, Pico, Faial, São Jorge, Graciosa, Santa Maria, Flores and Corvo.
Neuf îles portugaises dans l’Atlantique.
🌋
The archipelago is volcanic. São Miguel is known for crater lakes such as Sete Cidades and Lagoa do Fogo, while Furnas is associated with geothermal activity.
Archipel volcanique, lacs de cratère et activité géothermique.
🌿
The climate is generally mild and humid, which helps create the famously green landscapes.
Climat généralement doux et humide, paysages très verts.
🥾
Walking routes cross crater rims, forests, coastal areas and viewpoints. Weather can change quickly, so layers and waterproof clothing are useful.
Randonnées variées; météo changeante, vêtements adaptés utiles.

🇫🇷 Corsica / Corse

☀️
Corsica has a Mediterranean climate, with warm, dry summers on the coast and cooler conditions at altitude.
Climat méditerranéen, plus frais en altitude.
🌿
The maquis is a fragrant Mediterranean scrubland with plants such as myrtle and other aromatic shrubs.
Le maquis: végétation méditerranéenne dense et parfumée.
⛰️
The island combines coastline and high mountains. There are easy walks, village paths and demanding routes such as the GR20.
Mer + montagne, randonnées faciles ou très exigeantes.
🪨
The Calanques de Piana are famous for sculpted pink-red granite cliffs above the sea. Corsica is not a destination known for active volcanoes.
Falaises granitiques spectaculaires; pas une destination de volcans actifs.
