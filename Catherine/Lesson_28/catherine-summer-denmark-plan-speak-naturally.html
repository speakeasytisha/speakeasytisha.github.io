3) Grammar toolkit — what you need for this lesson

Would like to

Use it for wishes and plans.

You would like to go to Denmark this summer.

You would like to spend time with your family.

Be going to

Use it for a plan or intention.

You are going to visit your family.

You are going to organise the trip soon.

Would rather

Use it for preference.

You would rather stay near the coast.

You would rather travel in July.

Comparatives

The coast is more relaxing than a busy town.

A small place is calmer than a crowded city.

This plan is better than the other one.

Superlatives

For you, the coast is the best place to relax.

This is the most practical option.

It would be the most relaxing choice.

5) Real-life dialogues — natural and useful

Dialogue 1 — talking to your son about the trip

Useful phrases

  • You would like to come this summer.
  • You are thinking about July or August.
  • You would prefer a quiet period.
  • You would like to spend time together.

Model dialogue

You: You would like to come to Denmark this summer. You are thinking about July or August. You would prefer a quiet period so you can spend more time together.

Son: That sounds lovely. July might be easier for us.

You: July sounds better for you too, so that could be the best option.

Dialogue 2 — comparing two activities

Useful phrases

  • You would rather go for a walk by the coast.
  • That would be more relaxing.
  • A town visit would also be interesting.
  • For you, the coast is the best option.

Model dialogue

Question: Would you prefer to visit a town or spend time by the coast?

Answer: You would rather spend time by the coast because it would be more relaxing. A town visit would also be interesting, but for you, the coast is the best option.

6) Guided speaking — build your answer step by step

Speaking task A — your summer plan

Use this frame

This summer, you would like to __________.
You would like to __________.
You would prefer __________ because __________.
For you, the best part would be __________.

Model

This summer, you would like to go to Denmark. You would like to spend time with your family and enjoy the coast. You would prefer a quiet and relaxing stay because it would be more meaningful for you. For you, the best part would be the time spent together.

Speaking task B — compare two options

Use this frame

For you, __________ is better than __________ because __________.
It is more __________ and more __________.
That is why it is the best option for you.

Model

For you, staying near the coast is better than staying in a busy town because it is more peaceful and more relaxing. It is also more beautiful for you. That is why it is the best option for you.

7) Writing studio — from ideas to a short paragraph

Writing recipe

Sentence 1: say what you would like to do

Sentence 2: add where / with whom

Sentence 3: compare or explain a preference

Sentence 4: finish with a reason or conclusion

Model paragraph

You would like to go to Denmark this summer. You would like to visit your son and daughter-in-law and spend time near the coast. For you, this would be better than a busy holiday because it would be calmer and more meaningful. That is why it would be a lovely summer trip for you.

Guided writing frame

You would like to __________ this summer.
You would like to __________.
For you, __________ is better than __________ because __________.
That is why __________ would be the best option for you.

8) Final CLOE challenge — plan, compare, and justify

Use the vocabulary, the future forms, and the comparison language from the lesson.

Oral prompts

  • What would you like to do in Denmark this summer?
  • What would you prefer: the coast or a town? Why?
  • How are you going to organise the trip?
  • Why is this summer visit important for you?

Checklist

  • Use would like to
  • Use be going to
  • Use at least one preference phrase
  • Use at least one comparative or superlative
  • Finish with a reason or conclusion
