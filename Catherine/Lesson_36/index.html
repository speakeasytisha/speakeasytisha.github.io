MISSION 03 · GRAMMAR & SYNTAX

Relative clauses + tense clues + natural word order

Your goal is not to recite rules. Your goal is to spot the clue and choose the structure quickly.

whopeople

The guide who helped us was friendly.

whereplaces

Mljet is an island where you can enjoy quiet nature.

which / thatthings

We chose a trail which goes around the lake.

whyreason

That is the reason why I prefer slower travel.

Astuce : where remplace « dans lequel / où » pour un lieu ; which/that donne une information sur une chose.

be going tointention / plan

We are going to visit Mljet.

present continuousarranged plan

We are staying in Dubrovnik for two nights.

willdecision / prediction

I think the views will be beautiful.

usually / every → present simplenow / at the moment → present continuousyesterday / last / ago → past simplesince / for / already / ever → present perfecttomorrow / next / in September → future language
SUBJECT+VERB+OBJECT / DETAIL+PLACE+TIME

We are visiting the old town in Dubrovnik on Tuesday.

MISSION 05 · READING

Read for proof — not for every word

First identify the situation, then find the exact sentence that proves your answer.

1. Read the question2. Scan for names / dates / contrast3. Find evidence4. Eliminate distractors
Travel message

A change of plan

We had originally planned to spend three nights in Dubrovnik, but we changed the itinerary because we wanted more time in nature. We are now staying in the city for two nights before taking a ferry to Mljet, where we hope to walk near the lakes and enjoy a quieter atmosphere. After that, we are travelling south toward Montenegro. If the weather is good, we would like to spend a day in the Durmitor area, which is known for mountain scenery and outdoor activities. We have not booked every excursion yet because we want to keep part of the trip flexible.

Technique : cherchez surtout but, because, before, after that, if et yet : ces mots organisent le sens.

MISSION 08 · WRITTEN TRANSFER

Optional syntax transfer — a short travel problem message

This is not presented as a separate official CLOE writing task; it is here to strengthen the grammar and organisation you need across the certification.

80–110 words

Your ferry is cancelled.

Write to your accommodation in Mljet. Explain the problem, give your new arrival time, ask whether late check-in is possible, and thank them.

Plan : situation → conséquence → nouvelle heure → demande polie → remerciement.

5-line structure

  1. Hello / Dear…
  2. I’m writing because…
  3. Unfortunately… so…
  4. Would it be possible to…?
  5. Thank you for your help.
Your word count and language signals will appear here.
A2 · safe

Hello, I have a problem with my ferry. It is cancelled, so I will arrive later. I think I will arrive at about 8 p.m. Is late check-in possible, please? Thank you for your help. Kind regards, Catherine

A2+ · fuller

Dear Sir or Madam, I’m writing because our morning ferry to Mljet has been cancelled due to strong wind. We are taking another boat in the afternoon, so we expect to arrive at about 8 p.m. Could you please tell me if late check-in is possible? Thank you for your understanding and your help. Kind regards, Catherine

B1 · target

Dear Sir or Madam, I’m writing to let you know that the ferry which we were supposed to take this morning has been cancelled because of strong wind. We have booked the afternoon departure instead, which means that we should arrive at around 8 p.m. Would it be possible to arrange a late check-in? Please let me know if you need any additional information. Thank you in advance for your help. Kind regards, Catherine
