STOP TRANSLATING

French idea → natural English reflex

These are exactly the small patterns that make fluent English sound less natural. Choose an answer: you get immediate feedback and a hint every time.

New habit: learn complete chunks — handle a situation · make sure · explain something to someone · be used to doing.
Oral transfer model

Use the chunks in one answer: “I’m used to working under pressure, so when a passenger is upset, I handle the situation calmly. I first check the facts, then I explain the procedure and make sure the passenger understands the next step.”
