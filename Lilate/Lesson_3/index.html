STEP 1 · KNOW WHAT PART 2 IS ASKING

You are not expected to copy everything. You are expected to identify and transfer the useful information.

EXAMINER MODALITY

Two situations + QCU · about 15 minutes

Mission ASummarise key information from a document or audio message.
Mission BAnalyse and restate information from a table or document.
What mattersAccurate facts, useful reformulation, clear transcription.

L’objectif est de comprendre les informations pertinentes puis de les restituer clairement, sans ajouter ni modifier les faits.

IA MODALITY

Same competency · different delivery

You can meet QCU, QCM and an open response based on a written document, audio message, table or other professional support.

Core skill: collect → reformulate → transcribe under another format.

Vous pouvez rencontrer des QCU, QCM et une réponse ouverte à partir d’un document écrit, d’un message audio ou d’un tableau.

STEP 2 · BUILD YOUR INFORMATION MAP

Before you write, sort the message into six boxes.

WHOPeople / departmentName, role, team, contact
WHATMain requestPurpose, problem, product, service
WHENDate / time / deadlineDay, hour, due date
WHEREPlace / channelRoom, office, link, platform
NUMBERPrecise detailsPrice, quantity, reference, phone
ACTIONNext stepWhat must happen next?
Technique: write keywords, not full sentences, while you listen. Build the full sentence only after you know the important facts.

Pendant l’écoute, notez des mots-clés plutôt que des phrases complètes. Vous construirez ensuite une réponse claire.

STEP 3 · GRAMMAR BEFORE PRACTICE

Ask for missing information and confirm details correctly.

RULE A · BE

Use be for identity, location, time and status.

AffirmativeThe meeting is in Room 4.
NegativeThe meeting isn't in Room 4.
QuestionIs the meeting in Room 4?
WH-questionWhere is the meeting?

Avec be, on inverse simplement le verbe et le sujet dans la question.

RULE B · PRESENT SIMPLE

Use do / does to ask about routines, services and factual actions.

AffirmativeThe office opens at 9.
NegativeThe office doesn't open at 8.
QuestionDoes the office open at 9?
WH-questionWhen does the office open?

Après does, le verbe revient à sa base : does open, jamais does opens.

RULE C · CAN / COULD

Use modal + base verb for clarification and professional requests.

AffirmativeYou can send the form by email.
NegativeYou can't send it after Friday.
QuestionCan you repeat the reference?
PoliteCould you spell the surname, please?

Après can/could, utilisez toujours la base verbale : can repeat, could spell.

WHOWho is the contact person?
WHATWhat do you need?
WHENWhen does the session start?
WHEREWhere is the meeting?
HOW MUCHHow much is the service?
HOW MANYHow many people are attending?

PART 2 SUCCESS BANK

Use these habits when the information feels dense.

Do this

  • Read the required output before the source.
  • Listen for names, numbers, dates, places and actions.
  • Ask for repetition or clarification when the format allows interaction.
  • Use keywords while listening.
  • Reformulate in simpler English.
  • Check precise details before validating.
  • Keep the next action visible in your final response.

Avoid these habits

  • Trying to transcribe every word.
  • Adding a detail because it “sounds logical.”
  • Ignoring a number or reference because the general idea is clear.
  • Copying a long sentence without understanding it.
  • Changing the meaning while paraphrasing.
  • Writing before you know the task format.
