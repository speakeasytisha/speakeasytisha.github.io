STEP 1 · IDENTIFY YOUR FORMAT

There are two LILATE modalities.

THE DIFFERENCE THAT MATTERS

Same professional reference framework. Different delivery.

The two modalities evaluate your ability to use the language in professional situations. The official result is expressed on the CEFR scale.

Les deux modalités évaluent votre capacité à utiliser la langue dans des situations professionnelles. Le résultat officiel est exprimé selon le CECRL.

Your candidate space tells you which modality you have been assigned. Always check it before training specifically for the interface.

STEP 2 · UNDERSTAND THE 4 PARTS

Every part asks you to do a professional action.

1

Accueil & interaction

Welcome someone, identify who they are and what they need, respond appropriately and keep the exchange moving.

Accueillir, identifier la personne et sa demande, répondre de façon adaptée et poursuivre l’échange.

You need to: interact
2

Recueil & transcription

Understand information from a written or audio source, select the key information, reformulate it and transfer it into another format.

Comprendre un document ou un audio, repérer l’essentiel, reformuler et retranscrire les informations.

You need to: collect + reformulate
3

Recueil & transmission

Receive information, instructions or a third person’s message, then pass it on clearly and use it to complete a professional mission.

Recevoir une information ou consigne puis la transmettre clairement pour accomplir une mission professionnelle.

You need to: relay + act
4

Recueil & exploitation

Consult documents or multimedia information, identify what matters, answer questions, contextualise, give a viewpoint and propose a solution.

Consulter des supports, identifier l’utile, répondre, contextualiser, argumenter et proposer une solution.

You need to: analyse + exploit
Memory code: WELCOME → COLLECT → RELAY → USE

STEP 3 · HOW YOU ARE GRADED

It is not simply “points out of 100”.

The official result is a CEFR level.

LILATE evaluates language ability and professional performance using competency grids. The result is expressed from A1 to C2, with the professional certificate issued from B1 for spoken languages.

A1level attestation
A2level attestation
B1certificate
B2certificate
C1certificate
C2certificate

Le LILATE ne repose pas sur une simple note. Il mesure des compétences linguistiques et professionnelles selon le CECRL.

STEP 4 · KNOW THE TASK TYPES

Recognise the screen before you need the English.

Guided practice
◉

Single choice · QCU

Choose one answer only.

Read the instruction first. Eliminate answers that contradict the source. Choose the option supported by evidence, not the one that merely uses familiar words.
☑

Multiple choice · QCM

More than one answer may be correct.

Treat each option as true/false. Do not stop after finding one correct idea. Check every option against the source.
✎

Open written answer

Write the requested information clearly and professionally.

Answer the task first, then add the essential detail. Use short, controlled sentences rather than ambitious sentences you cannot finish.
●

Open oral answer

Record your voice when the interface asks you to respond orally.

Start speaking with a complete sentence. If you need thinking time, use a professional bridge: “Let me check that.” / “From what I understand…”
▶

Audio / video support

Listen or watch, identify key facts, then answer or use the information.

Before playing, identify what you need to find: who, what, when, where, problem, action, next step.
▤

Document / message

Scan written information, select what matters and reformulate it.

Do not translate every word. Locate names, dates, numbers, action verbs, restrictions and the required next step.

STEP 5 · MASTER THE TIMER

The timer should guide you, not frighten you.

What is official?

IA

Each question has a limited response time shown on screen. When the time is over, the system moves forward. LILATE does not publish one universal fixed number of seconds for every question.

EXAMINER

The examiner manages the pace. The test lasts up to 60 minutes, with four parts of about 15 minutes each. You do not need to manage a per-question countdown.

En modalité IA, le temps restant est affiché à l’écran pour chaque question. Avec examinateur, c’est l’examinateur qui gère le rythme.

TRAINING TIMER · NOT AN OFFICIAL DURATION

01:00
0–25%: understand the task · 25–80%: answer · last 20%: finish and check.

STEP 7 · RESPONSE FORMULA

Never answer with one lonely word if the task needs interaction.

1

Answer

Respond directly to the request.

“Yes, the meeting has changed.”
2

Add the key detail

Give the information that makes your answer useful.

“It is now on Thursday at 10:30.”
3

Clarify / reformulate

Show that you understood and remove ambiguity.

“So, you need the updated slides today.”
4

Close / next step

Complete the professional action.

“I’ll send them before 5 p.m.”

STEP 10 · THE PASS STRATEGY

Do the professional job first. Perfect English comes second.

DO

  • Answer the task immediately.
  • Use clear complete sentences.
  • Reformulate key information.
  • Ask for clarification when needed.
  • Use linking words: first, then, because, however, so.
  • Correct yourself naturally and continue.
  • Use professional politeness.
  • Finish with the next step.

AVOID

  • Long silence while searching for a “perfect” sentence.
  • One-word answers when interaction is required.
  • Translating every word of a source.
  • Inventing details not present in the document/audio.
  • Stopping after a small mistake.
  • Speaking very fast to sound fluent.
  • Ignoring numbers, dates, times and conditions.
  • Forgetting what the professional mission was.
If you freeze:“Let me check that.”“From what I understand…”“Could you repeat the last part, please?”“The main point is…”“The next step is…”
