STEP 1 · KNOW WHAT PART 1 IS ASKING

The examiner or system is looking for professional interaction — not a memorised speech.

EXAMINER MODALITY

Two situations · about 15 minutes

Situation 1Present yourself and your professional context; welcome the person.
Situation 2Identify the request, analyse the need and propose an adapted service or response.
PacingThe examiner guides the interaction.

Vous êtes évalué sur votre capacité à entrer dans l’échange, comprendre le besoin et agir de façon professionnelle.

IA MODALITY

Same competency · different delivery

You can meet single-choice questions, multiple-choice questions and an open response connected to a professional interaction.

Important: there is no need to guess the “perfect sentence.” Show that you understand the situation and can respond usefully.

Vous pouvez rencontrer des QCU, QCM et une réponse ouverte. L’objectif reste de démontrer que vous savez comprendre et gérer l’interaction.

STEP 2 · YOUR RESPONSE ROUTE

Use the 5-step interaction route until it becomes automatic.

1

Welcome

Open the interaction naturally and professionally.

“Good morning. How can I help you?”
2

Identify

Find out who, what and why.

“Could you tell me what you need today?”
3

Clarify

Ask for the missing detail.

“Just to clarify, do you mean…?”
4

Adapt

Offer a response that fits the need.

“In that case, I can…”
5

Check

Make sure the solution is clear.

“Would that work for you?”

One flexible structure you can reuse

Good morning. How can I help you today? → Just to make sure I understand… → In that case, I can… → Would that work for you?

Ce squelette fonctionne dans de nombreux secteurs. Vous adaptez simplement les détails au contexte.

STEP 8 · REFORMULATION

Prove that you understood before you solve the problem.

THE SAFE FORMULA

“So, if I understand correctly…”

STEP 10 · TRAINING TIMER

Practise thinking under pressure without rushing your English.

TRAINING TIMER · NOT AN OFFICIAL ITEM TIME

Respond before the training clock finishes.

01:30

Plan: 10 sec understand → 10 sec organise → speak clearly → finish with a check.

PRESSURE-PROOF TECHNIQUE

Do not start speaking before you know the mission.

  1. Catch the task: Who is speaking? What do they need?
  2. Choose your route: welcome → question → clarification → solution.
  3. Use safe language: short, accurate sentences beat complicated unfinished ones.
  4. Close the loop: “Does that work for you?”
The official IA interface controls its own timing. These 60/90/120-second clocks are practice tools only.

PART 1 SUCCESS BANK

Phrases to rescue the interaction.

Use these

  • How can I help you today?
  • Could you tell me a little more about that?
  • Just to clarify…
  • So, if I understand correctly…
  • In that case, I can…
  • Let me check that for you.
  • Would that work for you?
  • Is there anything else I can help you with?

Avoid these habits

  • Answering before you understand the request.
  • Giving a long self-introduction that does not answer the task.
  • Using one-word answers when interaction is expected.
  • Pretending you understood when one detail is unclear.
  • Offering a solution without checking whether it fits the need.
  • Stopping completely after one small grammar error.
When you freeze:“Let me think for a moment.”“Could you repeat that, please?”“Do you mean…?”“If I understand correctly…”“What I can do is…”
