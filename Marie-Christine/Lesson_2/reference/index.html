02

YOUR TENSE COMPASS

Choose the right tense without guessing

Choisissez le bon temps sans deviner.

NOW / USUALLY

Present simple

Use it for: facts, jobs, homes and routines.

Utilisez-le pour : les faits, métiers, lieux de vie et habitudes.

I/you/we/they + verb
he/she/it + verb + s
  • My daughter works in IT.
  • My sons live in France.
  • We usually call at the weekend.
PAST + NOW / EXPERIENCE

Present perfect

Use it for: life experience or a situation that continues now.

Utilisez-le pour : une expérience de vie ou une situation qui continue.

have/has + past participle
  • She has lived in Montreal for eight years.
  • I have visited Ireland and Scotland.
  • We have kept in touch.
FINISHED PAST

Past simple

Use it for: a finished action with a finished time.

Utilisez-le pour : une action terminée à un moment terminé.

subject + past form
  • I travelled to Iceland last month.
  • We saw volcanoes and waterfalls.
  • I spoke to my daughter yesterday.

YOUR THREE-QUESTION DECISION

Ask yourself:

1

Is it a fact, job, home or routine?

→ Present simple
2

Is it an experience, or does it continue now?

→ Present perfect
3

Is the action finished with a finished time?

→ Past simple
TenseUseful cluesYour example
Present simpleusually, often, every week, lives, worksWe usually speak at the weekend.
Present perfectfor, since, ever, never, already, recentlyShe has lived there for eight years.
Past simpleyesterday, last month, in 2024, two years agoI visited Iceland last month.

FOR OR SINCE?

Duration or starting point?

fora durationfor eight years
sincea starting pointsince 2018

She has lived in Montreal for about eight years.

She has lived in Montreal since 2018.

My two sons live in France: one lives in Lyon, while my youngest son, who is 27, lives in Dunkirk. YOUR TRAVEL MODEL

Present perfect + past simple

I have visited Ireland and Scotland, and one of my most memorable experiences was a family trip to Iceland. We travelled there last month. During our stay, we saw volcanoes, waterfalls and icebergs. Although the weather was cool, it was sunny, and I was amazed by the scenery. Above all, the trip gave us the opportunity to create wonderful memories together.

YOUR CALL MODEL

A warm, practical family call

Hello, is now a good time to talk? How have you been? How is work going? That sounds interesting—tell me more about it. Sorry, I didn’t catch the last part. Could you repeat it more slowly, please? It was lovely catching up with you. Take care, and give everyone my love.

YOUR CORRECTION CHECKLIST

Small changes that make your English clearer

De petites corrections qui rendent votre anglais plus clair.

✗ my sons lives✓ my sons live

Use the verb without -s with a plural subject.

✗ she live in Boston✓ she lives in Boston

Add -s with he, she or one person.

✗ for 2018✓ since 2018

Use since with a starting point.

✗ since eight years✓ for eight years

Use for with a duration.

✗ I have visited Iceland last month.✓ I visited Iceland last month.

Use the past simple with a finished date.

✗ I went to Island.✓ I went to Iceland.

Iceland is the country; an island is land surrounded by water.

✗ hot clothes✓ warm clothes

Use warm clothes for clothes that protect you from cold weather.

✗ Although it was sunny but cool.✓ Although it was sunny, it was cool.

Use although or but, not both together.
