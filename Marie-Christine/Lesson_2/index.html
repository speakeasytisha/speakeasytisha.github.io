GRAMMAR FOR REAL LIFE

Present simple: where people live and what they do

Le présent simple : où les personnes vivent et ce qu’elles font.

USE

Facts, jobs and routines

Use the present simple for information that is generally true now.

I / You / We / Theylive · work · teachHe / Shelives · works · teaches

AFFIRMATIVE

Your daughter teaches.

My eldest daughter teaches at an international school.

My sons live in France.

NEGATIVE

Use doesn’t + base verb

She doesn’t live in France.

They don’t live in the same city.

QUESTION

Use does / do

Where does your daughter live?

What do your sons do?

Most verbswork → workslive → lives
-ch / -sh / -s / -x / -oteach → teachesgo → goes
Consonant + ystudy → studiestry → tries
Irregularhave → hasdo → does

PRONUNCIATION TIP

CONNECT THE PAST TO NOW

Present perfect with “for” and “since”

Le present perfect avec « for » et « since ».

FACT NOW

She lives in Montreal.

This tells us her current home.

Cela indique où elle habite actuellement.

→

PAST + NOW

She has lived there for about eight years.

This tells us the duration from the past until now.

Cela indique une durée commencée dans le passé et toujours vraie aujourd’hui.

FORM

have / has + past participle

I have lived · She has lived · We have visited

TIME WORD

for = duration · since = starting point

for eight years · since 2018

FOR

A duration

for eight years

for a long time

for three months

for several years

SINCE

A starting point

since 2018

since January

since she moved

since our last lesson

LISTENING 1 · MONTREAL

A quick family call

Daughter: Hello Mum. How are you?

Marie-Christine: Hi! I’m well, thank you. How have you been?

Daughter: I’ve been busy with work, but everything is going well.

Marie-Christine: That’s good to hear. Are you still working on the same computer project?

Daughter: Yes, I am. We’ve worked on it for three months.

Marie-Christine: Sorry, I didn’t catch the last part. Could you repeat it more slowly?

Daughter: Of course. We’ve worked on the project for three months.

2. How long have they worked on the project?

Hi Mum,

I hope you are well. Work has been quite busy recently because our team has started a new computer project. I have worked on it for three months, and we are making good progress.
