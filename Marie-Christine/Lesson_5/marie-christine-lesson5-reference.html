02

GRAMMAR 1 · COMPARATIVES

Say how two things are different

Comparer deux destinations, deux expériences ou deux options.

SHORT ADJECTIVE

+ -er than

cold → colder than
quiet → quieter than

Adjectif court + -er + than.

SPELLING CHANGES

big / hot / easy

big → bigger
hot → hotter
easy → easier

On double parfois la consonne ; y devient i.

LONG ADJECTIVE

more / less + adj + than

more interesting than
less expensive than

Pour les adjectifs longs.

IRREGULAR

good / bad / far

good → better
bad → worse
far → farther/further

À mémoriser par cœur.

Formula: subject + verb + comparative + than + comparison. Example: Italy may be warmer than Ireland in autumn. Not "more warmer" — never combine more and -er together.
03

GRAMMAR 2 · RICHER COMPARISONS

Sound more natural and more precise

Aller plus loin que « bigger than ».

≈ EQUAL

as … as

Italy can be as interesting as Ireland for different reasons.

≠ LESS

not as … as

A short break is not as long as a week abroad.

↑ DEGREE

much / far / a little

This route is much easier. That hotel is a little cheaper.

★ 3+

the …-est / the most

December may be the coldest of your four travel months.

🏆 B1

one of the most

It could be one of the most memorable trips of the year.

↔ CONTRAST

whereas / while

Ireland may feel greener, whereas Italy may feel warmer.

Remember: not as + adjective + as = a smaller degree, not a negative opposite. "Saint-Malo is not as busy as Rome" ≠ "Saint-Malo is quiet" — it just means Rome is busier.

05

VERB POWER · TALK ABOUT YOUR FUTURE TRIPS

going to · present continuous · will · schedules

Quatre façons utiles de parler du futur.

PLAN

be going to + verb

I'm going to visit Ireland in September.

ARRANGEMENT

present continuous

I'm travelling to Italy in October.

PREDICTION

will + verb

I think the trip will be memorable.

TIMETABLE

present simple

The train leaves at 8:15.
