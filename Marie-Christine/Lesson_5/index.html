GRAMMAR 1 · COMPARATIVES

Say how two things are different

Comparer deux destinations, deux expériences ou deux options.

1

Short adjective + -er than

cold → colder than
quiet → quieter than

Pour de nombreux adjectifs courts : adjectif + -er + than.

Saint-Malo may be quieter than a large Italian city.
2

Spelling changes

big → bigger
hot → hotter
easy → easier

On double parfois la consonne finale ; y devient i.

A direct journey is often easier than a journey with several changes.
3

more / less + long adjective + than

more interesting than
less expensive than

Pour les adjectifs longs : more / less + adjectif + than.

One itinerary can be more convenient than another.
4

Irregular forms

good → better
bad → worse
far → farther/further

Ces comparatifs doivent être mémorisés.

A morning flight may be better than a late-night flight.
Formula: subject + verb + comparative + than + comparison.

GRAMMAR 2 · RICHER COMPARISONS

Sound more natural and more precise

Aller plus loin que « bigger than ».

≈

as … as

Two things are similar.

Italy can be as interesting as Ireland for different reasons.
≠

not as … as

One thing has less of a quality.

A short break is not as long as a week abroad.
↑

much / far / a little

Modify the difference.

This route is much easier. That hotel is a little cheaper.
★

the …-est / the most

Compare three or more things.

December may be the coldest of your four travel months.
🏆

one of the most

Useful B1 structure.

It could be one of the most memorable trips of the year.
↔

whereas / while

Contrast two ideas.

Ireland may feel greener, whereas Italy may feel warmer.

VERB POWER 2 · TALK ABOUT YOUR FUTURE TRIPS

going to · present continuous · will · schedules

Quatre façons utiles de parler du futur.

PLAN

be going to + verb

Use for an intention or plan.

I’m going to visit Ireland in September.

Projet ou intention.

ARRANGEMENT

present continuous

Use for an arranged future event.

I’m travelling to Italy in October.

Arrangement déjà prévu.

PREDICTION / DECISION

will + verb

Use for a prediction or decision made now.

I think the trip will be memorable.

Prédiction ou décision spontanée.

TIMETABLE

present simple

Use for official schedules.

The train leaves at 8:15.

Horaire officiel.

LISTENING · COMPARE & UNDERSTAND

Hear the key difference, then respond

Écoutez d'abord sans transcript, puis vérifiez.

LISTENING 1

Ireland or Italy?

I’m really looking forward to both trips, but they will be quite different. Ireland may be cooler and greener, whereas Italy may feel warmer and more urban depending on where we go. I think Ireland will be better for long walks, while Italy may offer more opportunities for museums, cafés and city visits. I don’t think one trip will be better than the other. They will simply be different experiences.

LISTENING 2

Saint-Malo or Alsace?

The November and December trips are both in France, but the atmosphere should be very different. Saint-Malo is a coastal destination, so the sea will be an important part of the experience. Alsace in December may feel more festive and wintry. The Saint-Malo trip will probably be shorter, whereas the Alsace visit may include more towns and seasonal activities.

SPEAKING BUILDER

Compare two trips without getting stuck

Construisez d'abord votre réponse, puis essayez sans regarder.

Start with: Compared with…, … seems… because… Then add: However… / whereas… / I’m looking forward to both because…
Add at least one comparative and one reason. Your feedback will update as you write.
A2

Italy is warmer than Ireland. I like both trips, but I am very interested in Italy because I enjoy food and culture.

A2+

Compared with Ireland, Italy may be warmer and more cultural for me. Ireland may be better for walking, whereas Italy may offer more city visits. I’m looking forward to both.

B1

Although Ireland and Italy are both exciting destinations, I expect them to offer very different experiences. Ireland may be cooler and greener, which could make it better for walking, whereas Italy may feel warmer and more focused on cities, food and culture. I don’t think one will necessarily be better; I’m looking forward to the contrast.

WRITING · COMPARE & PLAN

Write a clear message about your travel year

Objectif : 100–130 mots, phrases claires, comparatifs et verbes corrects.

TASK

Write to an English-speaking friend

Explain your four upcoming trips. Compare at least two destinations, say what you are looking forward to, and mention one practical plan.

  • Use at least 2 comparatives.
  • Use at least 3 different verbs.
  • Use one future form.
  • Use because / whereas / although / however.
1. Introduce your travel calendar. 2. Compare Ireland and Italy. 3. Mention Saint-Malo and Alsace. 4. Add what you are most looking forward to. 5. Finish with a question.
B1 model

This autumn and winter are going to be quite busy for me. I’m travelling to Ireland in September and Italy in October. I think Ireland may be cooler and greener, whereas Italy may feel warmer and more cultural. In November, I’m going to Saint-Malo for a shorter break, and in December I’ll visit Alsace. The Alsace trip may be the most festive of the four because it will be in winter. I’m looking forward to every trip for a different reason, but Ireland is probably the one I’m most curious about at the moment. Which destination would you choose?
