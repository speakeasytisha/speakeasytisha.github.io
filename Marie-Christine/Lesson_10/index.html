MANUAL AUDIO · AMERICAN ENGLISH

“Where should we meet?”

Audio plays only when you press Play.

A: Hey, where are you now?

B: I’m near Back Bay Station. I’m trying to figure out the easiest way to get to Boston Common.

A: You can take the T, but honestly, if the weather’s nice, it’s not too far to walk.

B: That sounds good. Is it pretty straightforward from here?

A: Yes. Head toward the Public Garden. Once you get there, Boston Common is right next to it.

B: Perfect. I’m on my way. Where should we meet?

A: Let’s meet near the entrance by the Common. Text me when you’re close.

B: Will do. I should be there in about fifteen minutes.

7 MIN · EVERYDAY STRUCTURES

Make plans without sounding stiff

Ces structures sont courantes, polies et naturelles. Elles ne sont pas de l’argot.

SUGGEST

How about + -ing…?

How about walking through the Public Garden?

SUGGEST

Do you want to…?

Do you want to get lunch before we go?

DECIDE

Let’s + verb

Let’s meet outside the station.

CHECK

Should we…?

Should we take the T or walk?

ARRANGEMENT

We’re + -ing

We’re meeting everyone at six.

PLAN

We’re going to…

We’re going to spend the afternoon downtown.

Natural movement verbs: go to a place · get to a destination · head to a place · take the T/bus · get on · get off · walk to.

8 MIN · FAMILY CONVERSATION LAB

Three everyday Boston moments

Parlez avec vos propres mots. Utilisez l’indice si nécessaire, puis comparez avec les deux modèles.

SPEAKING TIMER01:30
SCENE 1

Meet-up change

Situation: Your family changes the meeting point. Confirm where to go, say when you will arrive and ask one useful question.

So, we’re meeting…I’ll be there…Should I…?
Confirm the new place first. Then give your arrival time. Finish with one practical question.

A2+: Okay, we’re meeting near the station now. I’ll be there in fifteen minutes. Should I wait outside?

B1: Okay, so we’re meeting outside the station instead. That works for me. I’m on my way and I should be there in about fifteen minutes. Should I wait by the main entrance?

○ not practised
SCENE 2

Lunch plan

Situation: Suggest lunch, ask what your family feels like eating and agree on a simple plan.

How about…?What do you feel like…?Sounds good
Suggestion → preference question → agreement.

A2+: How about getting lunch now? What do you want to eat? We can find somewhere near here.

B1: How about getting lunch before we keep walking? What do you feel like eating? There are plenty of places around here, so we can choose when we get there.

○ not practised
SCENE 3

You are briefly lost

Situation: Ask someone for help, check that you understood and thank them naturally.

Excuse me…So I… right?Thanks, I appreciate it
Ask one clear question. Repeat the key direction in your own words to check.

A2+: Excuse me, how do I get to the station? So I turn left at the next street, right? Thank you.

B1: Excuse me, could you tell me the easiest way to get to the station? Okay, so I go straight for two blocks and turn left at the light, right? Great, thank you. I really appreciate it.
