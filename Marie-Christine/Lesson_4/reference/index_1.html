03

GRAMMAR · TELL WHAT HAPPENED

Past simple vs past continuous

Distinguer l'action principale et l'action en cours.

PAST SIMPLE

Finished action

Subject + past form

I missed my connection. The train arrived late.

PAST CONTINUOUS

Action in progress

was/were + verb-ing

I was waiting when they announced the delay.

TOGETHER

Background + event

I was waiting for my luggage when an employee called my name. While I was checking in, the computer stopped working.

Remember: not "I was waited" — say I was waiting (in progress) or I waited (finished).
04

GRAMMAR · PAST EVENT OR CURRENT PROBLEM?

Past simple vs present perfect

Choisir selon le lien avec le présent.

PAST SIMPLE

Finished time

With yesterday, last night, at 3 p.m.

I lost my phone yesterday.

PRESENT PERFECT

Result matters now

have/has + past participle

My suitcase hasn't arrived yet.

KEY WORDS

yet · already · just

It hasn't arrived yet. I've already spoken to reception. The train has just been cancelled.

05

GRAMMAR · ASK, ADVISE, OBLIGE

Can, could, would, should, have to & must

Demandes polies, conseils et obligations.

CAN

Ability / simple request

Can you help me?

COULD

Politer request

Could you repeat that?

WOULD

Polite offer

Would you like me to call a taxi?

SHOULD

Advice

You should speak to the information desk.

HAVE TO

External rule

You have to complete this form.

MUST

Strong obligation

You must keep your receipt.

Important: after a modal, use the base verb — could repeat, should call, must keep. Not "could repeated" or "must to keep".
06

GRAMMAR · PLAN THE SOLUTION

First conditional

Parler d'une situation possible et de sa conséquence.

IF + PRESENT → WILL + BASE VERB

If the train is late, I will take the next one.

If my suitcase doesn't arrive tonight, I will call the airline tomorrow.

Not:

❌ If the train will be late…

✅ If the train is late…

if = conditionwhen = expected eventunless = if not (B1 bonus)
