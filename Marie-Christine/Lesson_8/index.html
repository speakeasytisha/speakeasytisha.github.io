IMPORTANT · CURRENT IA UPDATE

Your centre PDF and the current IA modality are not describing the same test experience

The September 2025 centre guide presents LILATE mainly as a 60-minute videoconference with a human evaluator, organised around four 15-minute parts. Current official LILATE guidance now clearly separates that examiner modality from LILATE IA. The IA modality is automated, usually lasts about 30–45 minutes, requires no live appointment, and currently presents four parts of roughly seven minutes each with QCU, QCM and open written/oral responses.

This page therefore prepares you for the current LILATE IA modality, while keeping the same four underlying professional competency areas described in the centre guide.

The IA version is taken independently online. You do not book a live examiner appointment. Once the test is available in your candidate space, you launch it before the deadline shown there. The session is adaptive: the difficulty and overall length can change according to your responses.

02 · THE 4 PARTS

Same competency logic throughout

  1. Welcome & interactionIdentify a professional contact, understand the request, respond appropriately.
  2. Collect & transcribeExtract information from a document or audio and reproduce it in another format.
  3. Collect & transmitPass on instructions or third-party information clearly and accurately.
  4. Collect & exploitAnalyse a document/video-style source, select useful information and justify a professional response.

04 · ORAL RESPONSES

The IA does not converse with you

You respond to a succession of instructions. Your answer is recorded. Public LILATE guidance says you can listen back and redo your recording before sending it. Your accent itself is not the target; intelligibility, relevance and professional communication are what matter.

05 · WRITING

Concrete professional writing

Expect short, functional writing: an email, message, reformulation or set of instructions. Copy/paste is blocked in the actual IA test. Clear, structured language is more useful than over-complicated sentences.

06 · TECHNICAL SETUP & PROCTORING

Prepare the environment before pressing “Start”

Identity check: the current IA access flow can ask you to scan a QR code on your computer and use your phone for identity verification (ID photos + selfie). Once the exam begins, external tools, other windows, online assistance and phone use are prohibited. Webcam and microphone stay active and browser interactions can be monitored.

07 · YOUR PROFESSION MATTERS

Yes — the IA version can be profession-specific

Current LILATE guidance states that the IA modality is adapted to your profession. The system can ask for your job, sector, company name and company website, then adapt the test context. The professional “decor” changes; the underlying language competencies remain the same.

Trainer note for future learners: collect those details before building the final mock. This simulation uses a broad professional-services context so it remains useful without assuming a specific job.

08 · RESULTS

What the result means

Official results are currently announced within 24 hours. A LILATE certificate attesting aptitude to work in the language is issued from B1. Lower validated levels receive a level attestation rather than the professional aptitude certificate.
