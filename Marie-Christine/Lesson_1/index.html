NAMES & TITLES

Address people appropriately

S’adresser aux personnes de manière appropriée.

Mr

Mr Brown

For a man. Marital status is not relevant.

Pour un homme, quelle que soit sa situation matrimoniale.

Ms

Ms Taylor

The safest neutral title for a woman when you do not know her preference or marital status.

Le titre neutre le plus sûr pour une femme lorsque sa préférence ou sa situation est inconnue.

Mrs

Mrs Wilson

Traditionally used for a married woman who chooses this title.

Traditionnellement utilisé pour une femme mariée qui choisit ce titre.

Miss

Miss Green

Traditionally used for an unmarried woman; also common for female teachers in Britain.

Traditionnellement pour une femme non mariée ; également courant pour les enseignantes au Royaume-Uni.

Dr

Dr Adams

For a medical doctor or someone with a doctorate.

Pour un médecin ou une personne titulaire d’un doctorat.

First name

“Please call me Anna.”

Use a first name when the person introduces themselves that way or invites you to.

Utilisez le prénom si la personne se présente ainsi ou vous y invite.

About “folks”

Folks is a friendly, informal way to address a group, especially in American English: “Hi, folks!” It can also mean “parents” or “family”: “My folks live in Boston.” Avoid it in very formal situations.

Folks est une manière amicale et informelle de s’adresser à un groupe, surtout en anglais américain. Le mot peut aussi signifier « mes parents » ou « ma famille ». À éviter dans les situations très formelles.

Three useful prepositions

IN

ON

NEAR

près de, sans distance précise

TRAVEL STORY BUILDER

Tell the story of your Iceland trip

A2

Clear and simple

Last month, I went to Iceland with my family. We saw volcanoes, waterfalls and icebergs. The weather was sunny but cool. We wore warm clothes. It was a beautiful trip.

A2+

Connected and personal

Last month, I travelled to Iceland with my family. First, we explored the extraordinary landscapes and saw volcanoes, waterfalls and icebergs. Luckily, the weather was sunny, although the temperature was only about fifteen degrees. We brought warm clothes and enjoyed every moment together.

B1

Detailed and natural

One of my most memorable trips was a family holiday in Iceland. My daughter, who lives in Montreal, joined us, as did my other daughter from Boston and my two sons who live in France. What made the experience exceptional was the dramatic landscape, with its volcanoes, waterfalls and icebergs. Although the temperature was around fifteen degrees, we were lucky because the weather remained sunny. Above all, I loved sharing such a special experience with my family.
