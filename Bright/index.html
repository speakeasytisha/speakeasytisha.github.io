01 · THE EXAM EXPLAINED

What happens on test day?

Your pathway assesses five skills. The online multiple-choice part is the same for everyone; writing and speaking follow your centre's instructions.

Votre parcours évalue cinq compétences. La partie QCM en ligne est commune ; l'écrit et l'oral suivent les consignes de votre centre.

✍️

Written expression

Grammar · vocabulary · sentence structure
  • 60 questions
  • Complete the sentence: 4 options (A–D)
  • 45 seconds per question
🎧

Listening

Short sentences or mini-dialogues
  • 60 questions
  • Choose the meaning: usually 3 options (A–C)
  • 60 seconds per question
  • You may replay the audio, but the clock keeps running
📧

Writing

According to your centre's guide
  • A professional email
  • A short developed text (about 140 words)
  • About 30 minutes
🗣️

Speaking

Live, with an examiner by video
  • Introduce yourself, talk about your work
  • Answer questions, give opinions and examples
  • About 20 minutes

The 5 golden rules

1

The clock decides. When time runs out, the test moves to the next question automatically.

Quand le temps est écoulé, on passe automatiquement à la question suivante.

2

Never leave a blank. An unanswered question counts as wrong. A guess gives you a chance.

Une question sans réponse compte comme fausse : répondez toujours.

3

Keep moving forward. You normally cannot go back to an earlier question.

On ne peut normalement pas revenir en arrière.

4

Listening has no pictures. Read the 3 short options first, then listen for the general meaning.

Pas d'image ni de contexte : lisez les options, puis écoutez le sens global.

5

Grammar matters a lot. Many written questions test verb forms, prepositions and word types.

Beaucoup de questions portent sur les formes verbales, les prépositions et la nature des mots.

04 · GRAMMAR · VERB PATTERNS

“to do” or “doing”?

This is one of the most frequent traps in the written test. The first verb decides the form of the second verb.

C'est le premier verbe qui décide de la forme du second : « to + verbe » ou « verbe + -ing ».

Verb + to + verb

want · decide · agree · plan · hope · need · offer · refuse · manage · afford · would like

We decided to change our supplier.

Verb + -ing

enjoy · avoid · finish · suggest · consider · mind · keep · recommend

She suggested meeting online.

Verb + person + to

ask · remind · tell · allow · advise · encourage someone to…

Could you remind me to call the client?

Preposition + -ing

interested in · good at · before / after · look forward to · thank you for

I look forward to hearing from you.

💡 Trap: in look forward to, “to” is a preposition, so it is followed by -ing: I look forward to meeting you, never to meet. Dans « look forward to », « to » est une préposition : on met donc -ing.

05 · GRAMMAR · TENSES

Present perfect or past simple?

Look for the time word. It almost always tells you which tense to choose.

Cherchez le marqueur de temps : il indique presque toujours le temps à utiliser.

Past simple finished time

I worked · I went · Did you go?

The action is finished and we know when.

yesterday · last week · in 2020 · two days ago · when I was…

I sent the report yesterday.

Action terminée, moment précis → prétérit.

Present perfect link with now

I have worked · She has gone · Have you been?

Experience, a result now, or a situation that is still true.

since · for · already · yet · just · ever · never · so far

We have worked here since 2019.

Lien avec le présent → present perfect. « since 2019 » = depuis 2019.

💡 French speakers: « Je travaille ici depuis 5 ans » = I have worked here for 5 years, never I work here since 5 years. since + point de départ (since 2019) · for + durée (for 5 years).

07 · GRAMMAR · WORD FAMILIES

Noun, verb, adjective or adverb?

Often the four options are the same word in different forms. Look at the position of the gap to find the type of word you need.

Souvent, les 4 options sont le même mot sous des formes différentes. La place du blanc indique la nature du mot.

08 · LISTENING TOOLKIT

Hear the meaning, not every word.

In the listening test you hear one short sentence with no picture and no context. The correct answer says the same thing with different words.

La bonne réponse reformule la phrase entendue avec d'autres mots.

1

Read the 3 options first

They are short. They tell you what to listen for.

Lisez d'abord les 3 options.

2

Listen for the key idea

Who? What? When? Positive or negative?

Qui ? Quoi ? Quand ? Positif ou négatif ?

3

Beware of the “same word” trap

An option that repeats a word you heard is often wrong.

Une option qui reprend un mot entendu est souvent un piège.

4

Replay if you need to

You can replay within the 60 seconds. Decide before time runs out.

Réécoutez si besoin, mais décidez avant la fin des 60 secondes.
