08

Live conversation · Let’s meet

ROUND 1 · WITH SUPPORT

You meet an English-speaking person for the first time.

Say hello → introduce yourself → say where you live → talk about one interest → ask one question.

Useful questions
Where are you from? · What do you like doing? · Do you like travelling? · What kind of films/books do you like?
Use 4 short sentences. You do not need a long speech.

ROUND 2 · MORE NATURAL

Keep the conversation going.

Add because, ask a follow-up question, and react to what the other person says.

Really? · Me too! · That sounds interesting. · Why do you like it? · What about you?
