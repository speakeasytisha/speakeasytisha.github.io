08

USE IT FOR REAL

Three first conversations

SOCIAL

An English-speaking acquaintance

You meet again at a dinner or social event. Introduce yourself, talk about an interest and ask a follow-up question.

How have you been? · What have you been up to? · What kind of books do you like?
TRAVEL

Meeting someone while travelling

You start a friendly conversation at a hotel, café, tour or on a journey.

Where are you from? · Is this your first time here? · What do you recommend?
WORK

A simple professional introduction

You meet an English-speaking contact. Explain what you do or your professional background in simple, clear English.

I work in… · My role is… · I mainly… · And what do you do?
