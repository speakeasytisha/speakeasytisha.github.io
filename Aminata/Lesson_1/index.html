04 · Grammar runway

Present simple or present continuous?

Choose according to meaning — not only according to one keyword.

T
01

Present simple

Use it for facts, routines, permanent situations, professional responsibilities and states.

I / You / We / They + base verb
He / She / It + verb + s
  • I welcome passengers.
  • I work well under pressure.
  • Safety comes first.

Faits, habitudes, responsabilités régulières et situations stables.

02

Present continuous

Use it for actions happening now, temporary situations and current changes or projects.

am / is / are + verb-ing
  • I am preparing for the LILATE.
  • I am applying to Air France.
  • My English is improving.

Actions en cours, situations temporaires et projets actuels.

Important: some verbs are usually not continuous

Use the present simple with many state verbs: know, understand, need, want, prefer, believe, remember, belong.

✗ I am understanding the procedure.✓ I understand the procedure.

Your 4-second decision

1Is it happening now or temporary?Yes → continuous
2Is it a routine, fact, duty or state?Yes → simple
3Is the verb a state verb?Usually → simple

Task 1 · 100–130 words

Present your professional profile for a cabin-crew recruiter.

Include your experience, responsibilities, strengths, current preparation and objective.

Reveal the model after writing

I am an experienced flight attendant with more than ten years in the aviation industry. I have worked in Economy and Business Class, airport services and corporate aviation. My main responsibilities have included welcoming passengers, delivering high-quality service, applying safety procedures and responding calmly to unexpected situations. I particularly enjoy creating a personalized experience for international customers. I am attentive, adaptable and comfortable working as part of a multicultural team. I am currently preparing for the LILATE and strengthening my English because I would like to join Air France and contribute my experience to its high standards of safety and customer service.
