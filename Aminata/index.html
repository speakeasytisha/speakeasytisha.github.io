Writing task 1

Professional introduction

80–120 words

Write a short professional introduction for an Air France recruiter. Present your experience, your strengths and your professional objective.

Afficher les idées pour vous guider
  • Who are you and where do you live?
  • What professional experience do you have?
  • What are your strengths?
  • Why do you want to work in the airline industry?
  • What is your current objective?

Writing task 2

Professional email

70–110 words

You are applying for a cabin crew position. Write an email to introduce yourself and ask for information about the recruitment process.

Afficher les idées pour vous guider
  • Use a professional greeting.
  • Explain why you are writing.
  • Give one or two details about your experience.
  • Ask for information about the next steps.
  • Finish politely.
