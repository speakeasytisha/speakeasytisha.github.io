02 · SENTENCE METHOD · 10 MIN

Use the S.E.N.T. method under pressure.

Do not start by translating. First identify the structure the sentence needs.

S

Signal

Find the time word or grammar clue: since, yesterday, if, by Friday, already, currently.

“since 2024” → a tense that connects past and present
E

Engine

Locate the subject and auxiliary. Ask: who does what, and which helper verb is needed?

she + has + worked · did + you + send?
N

Natural order

Check English word order, especially in questions, negatives and adverbs.

How often do you meet? · We usually send…
T

Trap check

Check the small words: articles, prepositions, agreement, infinitive/gerund and irregular forms.

responsible for · interested in · avoid doing
COACH THINKING · MODEL

“We ___ this supplier since 2022.”

1Signal: “since 2022” starts in the past and continues now.
2Engine: subject = “we”; present perfect needs have.
3Form: have + past participle → have used.
4Trap check: not “use” and not “used” alone.
TRAP 01

French tense transfer

“Depuis” often needs the present perfect: I have worked here for three years.

TRAP 02

Question order

Where do you work? not “Where you work?” Auxiliary before subject.

TRAP 03

Third-person -s

She manages the team, but Does she manage the team?

TRAP 04

Modal + base verb

must send, can attend, should check — never “must to send”.

GRAMMAR SUCCESS STRATEGY

How to answer without freezing

  1. Read the whole sentence.The clue may come after the gap, not before it.
  2. Circle the invisible signal.Mentally notice words such as yesterday, since, already, if, by, currently and usually.
  3. Find the subject and auxiliary.Many mistakes disappear when you identify who acts and which helper verb controls the sentence.
  4. Check form after meaning.Once the tense or structure is clear, verify irregular forms, -s, -ing, past participle and prepositions.
  5. Choose and move.CLOE is adaptive. One difficult item is not a verdict on your level.
