00 / ORIENTATION

What might you need to do?

You may need to state a preference, explain a choice, compare solutions, justify a recommendation, or respond to a workplace situation. This is transferable CLOE preparation, not a prediction of an official essay prompt.

A2: 2–4 clear sentences · B1: 6–8 connected sentences · B2: a balanced argument with a counterpoint. A formal essay is not required for every learner.

Recognise the instruction

01 / THE METHOD

Five steps that work for every subject

1 · Understand the question

Underline the topic and task: Which do you prefer? Do you agree? What would you recommend?

Model: The question asks me to compare two options.

2 · State your view

A2: I think … / I prefer … B1: In my opinion, … B2: From my perspective, …

3 · Explain why

Give a reason with because, then one real example with for example.

4 · Compare or recognise another view

Use more … than, whereas, however or although when appropriate.

5 · Finish clearly

Use Overall, … / For these reasons, … / I would recommend …

Transferable B1 model

In my opinion, [option A] is a good choice because [reason]. For example, [specific situation]. [Option B] also has an advantage: [benefit]. However, [contrast]. Overall, I would recommend [option A] because [final reason].
