02 · WINNING TECHNIQUE · 10 MIN

Use the C.L.O.E. vocabulary method.

Do not translate every word. Read for the clue the question is giving you.

C

Context

Who is speaking? What is happening? Is this an email, meeting, delivery, complaint or request?

“The customer is unhappy…” → service/problem vocabulary
L

Link

Look for a word that naturally goes with another word: a collocation.

meet a deadline · place an order · raise an issue
O

Options

Eliminate answers that are grammatically possible but unnatural in this situation.

make / do / meet a deadline → only “meet” fits
E

Exact meaning

Check register, nuance and false friends before you validate.

actually ≠ actuellement · attend ≠ attendre
COACH THINKING · MODEL

“Please ___ the attached document before tomorrow’s meeting.”

1Context: professional request about a document.
2Link: verbs that naturally go with “document”.
3Options: “review” means examine/check; “watch” and “look” do not fit here.
4Exact answer: review.
TRAP 01

False friends

Eventually = finalement, not éventuellement.
Currently = actuellement, not “actually”.

TRAP 02

Near synonyms

Job = a position; work = activity. Customer buys; client often receives professional services.

TRAP 03

Prepositions

responsible for · interested in · available on Monday · apply for a job.

TRAP 04

Register

“Could you send…” is more professional than “Send me…”. Meaning and situation matter.

Fast memory rule

Say the English word → say its partner/collocation → make one sentence about your own work. Example: deadline → meet a deadline → “I need to meet a deadline on Friday.”

SUCCESS STRATEGY

What successful CLOE practice looks like

  1. Understand the task before the language.Know whether you are selecting, ordering, typing or listening.
  2. Search for combinations.Professional English is full of predictable partnerships: make a decision, meet a deadline, take responsibility.
  3. Use elimination.You do not need to “know” instantly. Remove impossible or unnatural answers first.
  4. Do not freeze.The adaptive test needs your answer to estimate your level. Choose the best option you can and keep moving.
  5. Review explanations after practice.The explanation is where improvement happens.
