02 · COACH RULES

Use help strategically—not automatically.

You have only five Coach Tokens for the entire written mock. A token reveals one targeted hint before you answer. Correct answers are not shown immediately; you review them at each 10-question checkpoint.

🎟 5 Coach Tokens

Use one only when you are genuinely blocked. The goal is to practise recovery, not dependence.

🔒 Delayed correction

Your answer is recorded immediately, but the explanation waits until the next checkpoint so you keep an exam rhythm.

🧠 Complete model thinking

At checkpoints, every question includes the answer, explanation and the reasoning technique that should have led you there.

WHEN TO USE A TOKEN

You are unsure between two answers.

First: eliminate anything grammatically impossible.

Then: test both remaining options in the whole context.

Only then: spend a Coach Token if the distinction is still unclear.

04 · PROFESSIONAL WRITING TRANSFER

Choose one writing type and produce the message yourself.

Do not memorise the models. Plan the purpose, required information, structure and tone first. Model answers stay hidden until you choose to review them.

05 · ORAL INTERVIEW TRANSFER

Finish with the three stages of the CLOE oral interview.

Speak aloud before opening the models. Aim for complete answers, natural interaction and clear professional communication rather than perfect memorised English.
