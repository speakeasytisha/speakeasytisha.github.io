01 · THE EXAM

The facts you need before test day

Use this section to understand what is assessed, how the test behaves and what the examiner is listening for.

01

Certification

CLOE Anglais – Communiquer dans un contexte professionnel, certified by CCI France and registered as RS7667.

02

Written core

The public candidate guide describes a 50-question adaptive test, with 10 questions in each of five skill areas.

03

Adaptive difficulty

Question difficulty changes according to previous answers. The goal is to locate the candidate's real CEFR level efficiently.

04

Question timing

There is no single fixed global countdown in the documented core. Each question has its own time limit with a green → yellow → red progress bar.

05

Listening

Listening extracts may be announcements, messages, dialogues or monologues. Each recording can be played up to twice.

06

Oral interview

Individual video interview with a CLOE assessor, usually 15–20 minutes, adapted to the candidate's professional profile.

What happens in the oral interview?

1

Introductory questions
Work, responsibilities, experience, projects, routines, goals.

2

Professional role-play
Explain, request, negotiate, solve a problem, arrange, reassure, clarify.

3

Professional discussion
Give an opinion, justify it, compare options, develop examples and react naturally.

What is scored in the oral?

  • Vocabulary range and appropriacy
  • Grammar and sentence control
  • Fluency and ease of speech
  • Pronunciation and intonation
  • Quality of interaction
High-impact habit: answer the question, add a reason, then add a concrete example. Short but developed answers show far more ability than one-line replies.
2026 UPDATE

Professional writing is now explicitly part of the registered competency framework.

The renewed RS7667 framework states that candidates must be able to write professional emails, technical notes and short reports clearly and appropriately. Public CLOE candidate material still documents the 50-question adaptive core, but it does not publicly specify the exact number or timing of writing-production tasks. This pack therefore trains writing seriously without inventing an “official” task count.

05 · COMPLETE SKILL REVIEW

What to know and how to perform

These strategy cards turn knowledge into exam behaviour.

A

Vocabulary

Choose words that fit both meaning and professional register. CLOE can test precise collocations, workplace nouns, phrasal verbs and context-sensitive choices.

Think in chunks: meet a deadline, raise a concern, issue a refund, reach an agreement.
B

Grammar & syntax

Look at the whole sentence before choosing. Time markers, auxiliaries, prepositions and clause structure often reveal the answer.

Scan for signals: since/for, by the time, if, already/yet, despite, unless, whereas.
C

Expressions

Expect fixed professional phrases and idiomatic combinations. Literal translation from French can produce a grammatically possible but unnatural answer.

Example: “I look forward to hearing from you,” not “to hear”.
D

Reading

Read the question first. Then identify purpose, sender/recipient, dates, conditions, exceptions and action required.

Do not over-read: find the evidence that proves the answer.
E

Listening

Use play 1 for the general message and play 2 for details. Do not mentally translate every word; listen for names, numbers, decisions, reasons and next steps.

Before play: read the question and predict what kind of information you need.
F

Writing

Use a clear purpose, logical paragraphs, useful connectors and an appropriate professional tone. Avoid overly complex sentences that create errors.

Reliable structure: purpose → context → action/request → deadline/next step → close.
G

Oral interaction

Interaction matters. Ask questions, react to the examiner, clarify and negotiate instead of giving a memorised monologue.

Useful repair: “If I understood correctly, you’d like me to…”
H

Pronunciation

Clarity matters more than sounding native. Stress content words, finish endings, use pauses and avoid rushing when nervous.

Priority: understandable rhythm + clear key words + controlled pace.

30-MINUTE REVISION PLAN

Fast final review

  1. 5 min · Vocabulary: review meetings, emails, customer service and problem-solving.
  2. 7 min · Grammar: tenses, modals, conditionals, prepositions and gerund/infinitive.
  3. 5 min · Reading: scan two short workplace messages and identify action + deadline.
  4. 5 min · Listening: practise one message twice: gist first, details second.
  5. 4 min · Writing: plan a professional email in five steps.
  6. 4 min · Oral: answer one intro question + one role-play + one opinion question aloud.
