02 · L.I.S.T.E.N. METHOD · 9 MIN

Six moves. Two listens. One clear answer.

Do not press play immediately. Prepare your ears first.

L

Look

Read the question and options first.

What exact information are you listening for?
I

Identify

Predict the situation and likely vocabulary.

Call? Announcement? Meeting? Problem? Request?
S

Sense

First listen = overall meaning.

Who? Why? What is happening?
T

Target

Mark the missing detail.

Date? number? reason? action? attitude?
E

Evidence

Second listen = confirm the exact proof.

Listen for paraphrases, negatives and corrections.
N

Nail it

Answer from what was said—not from assumptions.

Choose the option that matches the message precisely.
COACH THINKING · MODEL

Question: Why will the delivery arrive on Friday instead of Thursday?

LLook: I need a reason, not the new date.
IIdentify: likely logistics vocabulary: delay, driver, warehouse, traffic.
SSense: first listen tells me the delivery is late because of transport.
TTarget: I still need the precise cause.
EEvidence: second listen confirms “the driver’s vehicle broke down.”
NNail it: choose vehicle breakdown—not “warehouse problem.”
TRAP 01

Same word, wrong idea

A distractor may repeat a word from the audio while changing the meaning. Follow the message, not the keyword.

TRAP 02

Corrections

Speakers often correct themselves: “Tuesday—sorry, Wednesday.” The final information is the answer.

TRAP 03

Negatives

Not, no longer, except, unless, instead can reverse what you thought you heard.

TRAP 04

Numbers

Distinguish 13/30, 14/40, £15/£50, and times such as 2:15/2:50.

TRAP 05

Accent panic

You do not need every sound. Keep listening for meaning, stress words and context.

LISTENING COACH

Before every audio: L.I.S.T.E.N.

  1. Look at the question and options.
  2. Identify the likely situation and vocabulary.
  3. Sense the overall meaning on listen one.
  4. Target the missing detail.
  5. Evidence: confirm it on listen two.
  6. Nail it: answer only what the audio supports.
