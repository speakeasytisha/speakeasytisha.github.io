01 · READING DNA · 7 MIN

CLOE reading is not “translate everything.” It is “find what matters.”

The current certification describes written comprehension through professional documents such as emails, notes, instructions and short articles. The goal is to identify, select and use the essential information needed for a workplace task.

02 · S.C.A.N. METHOD · 9 MIN

Four moves. One goal: prove your answer.

Use the question to guide your eyes. You rarely need to understand every word.

S

Situation

Identify the document and the relationship.

Email? Notice? Procedure? Who wrote it, and for whom?
C

Command

Read the question before hunting for the answer.

Purpose? Detail? Reason? Next action? Meaning?
A

Anchors

Scan for names, dates, numbers, negatives, linkers and synonyms.

before · unless · instead · due to · however · by Friday
N

Nail the evidence

Choose the answer the text proves—not the one that merely sounds plausible.

Point to the exact phrase that supports your choice.
COACH THINKING · MODEL

“The workshop has moved from Room 4 to Room 7. The start time remains 10:30.” What changed?

SSituation: short workplace notice.
CCommand: identify the changed detail.
AAnchors: “moved from…to…” and “remains”.
NEvidence: only the room changed; 10:30 did not.
TRAP 01

Keyword match

An option may repeat words from the text but change the meaning. Match ideas, not just vocabulary.

TRAP 02

World knowledge

Do not answer from what is usually true. Answer from what this document says.

TRAP 03

Negatives

Not, except, unless, no longer can completely reverse the meaning.

TRAP 04

Timeline

Separate what already happened, what is happening now and what will happen next.

TRAP 05

Pronouns

Check what it, they, this, these refer to before choosing an answer.

READING STRATEGY

S.C.A.N. before you answer.

  1. S · SituationIdentify the document, writer, reader and workplace context.
  2. C · CommandRead exactly what the question asks: purpose, detail, reason, inference, next action or meaning.
  3. A · AnchorsScan for dates, numbers, names, negatives, linkers and paraphrases connected to the question.
  4. N · Nail the evidenceDo not choose until you can point to the phrase that proves the answer.
Final check

If two options seem possible, ask: “Which one is directly supported by the text?”
