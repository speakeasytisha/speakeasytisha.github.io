Everyday storyA2+ → B1−

A lunch that lasted longer than planned

Last Sunday, we invited two friends for lunch. We had planned a simple meal because everyone had had a busy week and wanted a quiet afternoon. I prepared a vegetable tart and a salad, while one of our friends brought dessert.

We thought they would leave at about three, but the weather was unexpectedly warm. After lunch, we made coffee and sat outside. The conversation moved from family news to films, travel and old memories, so nobody noticed the time. At half past four, one friend suddenly looked at her watch and laughed.

I had planned to finish some jobs in the house, but I decided they could wait until Monday. The day was not exciting or carefully organised. However, it was one of those simple afternoons that feels special because nobody is in a hurry.

In the evening, I realised that the best part had not been the food itself. It had been the time we had spent together.

🌿Do not translate every word.

“Unexpectedly” is not essential to understand the story.

🔎Look for paraphrases.

“They stayed longer” may be expressed as “nobody noticed the time.”

🧩Connectors show logic.

but = contrast · so = result · because = reason.

03 · NATURAL ENGLISH CLINIC

Small changes that make a big difference

This section targets the kinds of tiny choices that often separate understandable English from more natural B1 English.

🍽️

meal / dish

meal = the whole eating occasion
dish = one prepared food item

We had a lovely meal.
My favourite dish was the tart.

👥

our weekend

Use the possessive before the noun.

our weekend · their house · your plans

Avoid: the weekend of us

🕰️

currently / actually

currently / at the moment = actuellement
actually = en fait / en réalité

I’m currently reading a novel.
Actually, I changed my mind.

📍

to / in / at

Movement → to
city/country → arrive in
specific place → arrive at

travel to London · arrive in England · arrive at the hotel

📚

this kind of story

After this kind of, use a singular noun.

I like this kind of story.
I like these kinds of stories.

🌍

other / others

other + noun
others stands alone

other countries · Some are busy; others are quiet.

⚖️

comparisons

Use one complete comparison structure.

quieter than
more relaxing than
at the same level as

🔗

connectors

Do not rely only on and.

because = reason
so = result
however = contrast

💗
Your objective is not “perfect grammar”

Your objective is to recognise these patterns faster and use them more automatically. Bright rewards accurate choices; conversation rewards clear, natural patterns. The same practice helps both.

The rule

06 · LISTENING WORKSHOP

Listen for the change, the number, the place or the reason

You already understand the general idea well. The next step is precision: learning to catch the one detail that changes the answer.

🎧
Bright listening strategy

Before you listen, identify what the answers are testing. Is it a time? a place? a reason? a change? This tells your ear what to wait for.

Avant l’audio, regarde les réponses. Demande-toi : « Quelle information change entre les trois réponses ? » Ton oreille saura quoi chercher.
⏰Time

Listen for instead of, delayed, moved to, until.

📍Place

Listen for directions and location words.

🔄Change

The first information may be corrected later.

💡Reason

Listen for because, due to, so, since.
