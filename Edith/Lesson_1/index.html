11 · QUICK GRAMMAR WIN

I like doing vs I’d like to do

A simple distinction that immediately helps you talk about yourself.

5 min
habit / enjoyment

I like + -ing

Something you generally enjoy.

I like travelling.
I like watching series.
wish / goal

I’d like to + verb

Something you want to do.

I’d like to visit Canada.
I’d like to speak more confidently.

Make it personal

WEEKEND NOTE

We arrived in Porto on Friday evening and stayed in a small hotel near the river. On Saturday morning, we walked through the old town before taking a boat trip. The weather changed in the afternoon, so we visited a museum instead of going to the beach. It wasn't what we had planned, but it turned out to be one of the best parts of the weekend.

Your mission

A2+/clear

I would like to visit Scotland because I love beautiful landscapes. I would like to see Edinburgh and travel through the countryside. I think it would be a good destination for walking and discovering a different culture. I would recommend going in spring if the weather is not too cold.

B1/more developed

One destination I would really like to explore is Scotland. What attracts me most is the combination of dramatic landscapes, historic cities and local culture. I’d like to spend a few days in Edinburgh and then travel north to see the Highlands. I would probably recommend travelling outside the busiest summer period because it might be easier to enjoy the scenery and smaller towns.
