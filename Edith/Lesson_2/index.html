02 · LANGUAGE LAB

Say it naturally: small upgrades, big difference

The focus is not “more grammar.” It is choosing the natural structure quickly when you speak.

FOR

duration

I worked there for thirty years.

J’y ai travaillé pendant trente ans.

SINCE

starting point

I have lived here since 2010.

J’habite ici depuis 2010.

AGO

finished past

I stopped working a year ago.

J’ai arrêté de travailler il y a un an.

USED TO

past habit / situation

I used to work in banking.

Avant, je travaillais dans la banque.

Precision challenge

Choose the most natural sentence.

1. You want to describe a completed thirty-year career.

2. You stopped working one year before now.

3. Choose the natural travel sentence.

4. Complete: “France and ___ countries...”

Make your answer longer without making it harder

Build a B1-style answer with three moves.

1 · FACT

I travelled to Egypt this year.

2 · DETAIL

I spent two days in Cairo before the cruise.

3 · REACTION

What impressed me most was the contrast between the city, the ancient sites and the Red Sea.

Astuce : un bon développement oral n’a pas besoin de phrases très compliquées. Donnez un fait, un détail, puis une réaction personnelle.

04 · LISTENING PRECISION

Hear the detail, not just the general idea

Your initial assessment showed good global understanding. Here, the challenge is to catch the exact detail.

SCENE A · HOTEL IN CAIRO

A small change of plan

Good evening. Your room is ready, but breakfast will be served from 6:30 tomorrow because several guests are leaving early for a tour. The museum transfer will meet you in the lobby at 7:15, not 7:30. Please bring your ticket and a bottle of water.

What time does the museum transfer leave?

SCENE B · MEETING ANOTHER TRAVELLER

Follow the story

I came to Egypt with a group, but I added three days by the Red Sea after the cruise. I nearly booked a diving excursion, although in the end I chose a boat trip because I wanted a more relaxing day. Tomorrow I'm meeting two people from our group for dinner near the marina.

What activity did the speaker finally choose?

05 · READ BETWEEN THE LINES

A traveller’s message

Move beyond literal details: identify the reason, contrast and implied preference.

“I usually prefer independent travel because I like changing my plans at the last minute. This time, however, I joined a small group for part of the trip. It was less flexible, but I enjoyed having a guide at the historical sites. After the organised part, I stayed on for two days on my own. That combination suited me surprisingly well.”

What is the main idea?

What does “however” signal?

06 · CONVERSATION ENGINE

Keep the conversation alive

The target is not a perfect monologue. It is a real exchange: react, ask, connect, continue.

REACT

Show interest

Really? / That sounds amazing. / I can imagine.

ASK

Open the door

What was it like? / How long did you stay? / What did you enjoy most?

CONNECT

Add your experience

I had a similar experience in… / That reminds me of…

CONTINUE

Go one step further

Would you go back? / What would you do differently next time?
