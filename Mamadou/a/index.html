03 · GRAMMAR CONTROL

Use key words to choose the tense

Do not choose a tense by feeling. First look for the time signal, then choose the structure.

Ne choisissez pas un temps au hasard. Repérez d’abord le mot-clé ou l’expression de temps, puis choisissez la structure. Les mots-clés ci-dessous sont volontairement très visibles.
🔁

Present simple

routine · fact · regular responsibility

I / you / we / they + base verb
KEY WORDSusuallyalwaysoftensometimesevery dayevery weekon Mondaysnormally

You usually review risks before work starts.

Vous utilisez le présent simple pour vos habitudes, faits et responsabilités régulières.

⏳

Present continuous

now · temporary current action

am / is / are + verb-ing
KEY WORDSnowright nowat the momenttodaythis weekcurrently

You are preparing for the CLOE this week.

Vous utilisez le présent continu pour une action en cours ou une situation temporaire actuelle.

↩️

Past simple

finished action at a finished time

subject + past verb
KEY WORDSyesterdaylast weeklast yeartwo days agoin 2024when I was…

Last week, you identified a new hazard.

Vous utilisez le prétérit pour une action terminée dans une période passée terminée.

🧭

Present perfect

experience · duration connected to now

have / has + past participle
KEY WORDSforsincealreadyyeteverneverso farrecently

You have worked in this field for several years.

Vous utilisez le present perfect pour une expérience ou une durée liée au présent. for + durée, since + point de départ.

🗓️

Going to

decided plan or intention

am / is / are + going to + verb
KEY WORDStomorrownext weeknext yearthis weekendI plan to…I intend to…

You are going to continue your studies next year.

Vous utilisez going to pour un projet ou une intention déjà décidée.

🔭

Will

prediction · instant decision · future result

will + base verb
COMMON SIGNALSI thinkprobablymaybeI’m surein the future

This preventive action will reduce the risk.

Vous utilisez souvent will pour une prédiction, une décision immédiate ou un résultat futur.

05 · CONTROLLED PRACTICE

More practice: choose, build and repair

Each block has its own reset, and every answer is saved automatically.

05A

Present simple vs present continuous

First find the signal word.

Routine: usually, every…, often, normally → present simple. Now: now, at the moment, today, this week, currently → present continuous.
05B

Past simple vs present perfect

Finished past time or connection to now?

Finished past: yesterday, last…, ago, in 2024 → past simple. Connected to now: for, since, already, yet, ever, never, so far → present perfect.
05C

Future: going to vs will

Decided plan, prediction or future result?

Decided plan: be going to + verb. Prediction / immediate decision / future result: will + verb.
05D

Sentence order: build clear English

Click a word to move it into your sentence. Click it again in your sentence to put it back.

Reliable pattern: Subject + verb + object + place + time. The words start shuffled.
05E

Error repair: make the sentence professional

06 · READING

Reading: a near-miss report

Find the situation, hazard, action, cause and result.

Lisez d’abord pour comprendre la situation générale. Puis cherchez : le danger, l’action immédiate, la cause et la mesure préventive.

During a maintenance operation, a temporary barrier had been positioned too close to a vehicle access route. A maintenance vehicle passed the area and came close to the barrier. Nobody was injured, but the situation was recorded as a near miss because there was a potential collision risk.

The supervisor stopped the operation and asked the team to keep the access route clear. The barrier was moved to a safer position. A short review was organised to identify the cause. The team concluded that the work area had not been checked carefully enough before the operation started.

As a preventive action, the team agreed to verify access routes before each maintenance task and to report any change in the work area immediately.

Pendant une opération de maintenance, une barrière temporaire était placée trop près d’une voie d’accès pour véhicules. Un véhicule de maintenance est passé près de la barrière. Personne n’a été blessé, mais la situation a été enregistrée comme un presqu’accident car il existait un risque potentiel de collision. Le superviseur a arrêté l’opération, la barrière a été déplacée et une analyse a été réalisée afin d’identifier la cause et d’éviter que la situation se reproduise.
07 · LISTENING

Safety briefing: listen for action words

Listen without the transcript first. Then reveal it only when you need it.

Écoutez d’abord sans lire. Identifiez le risque principal et les verbes d’action. Utilisez ensuite le bouton Transcript pour vérifier.
Transcript

Good morning. Before work starts, please remember that the main risk today is vehicle movement near the maintenance area. Use the marked pedestrian route at all times and keep the access lane clear. If you notice a new hazard, stop, keep a safe distance and report it to your supervisor. We will review any changes to the work area at eleven o'clock. Thank you.

Le risque principal aujourd’hui concerne la circulation de véhicules près de la zone de maintenance. Utilisez le chemin piéton balisé, gardez la voie d’accès libre et signalez immédiatement tout nouveau danger.
08 · SPEAKING LAB

Answer in layers: answer + reason + example

Use short reliable sentences first, then develop them.

Commencez par une réponse courte et correcte. Ajoutez ensuite une raison, puis un exemple. Vous pouvez demander de répéter ou de reformuler si vous ne comprenez pas.
GAIN TIME

Let me think for a moment.

Laissez-moi réfléchir un instant.

CLARIFY

Could you repeat the question, please?

Pouvez-vous répéter la question, s’il vous plaît ?

REFORMULATE

Do you mean…?

Est-ce que vous voulez dire… ?

DEVELOP

The main reason is that…

La raison principale est que…

🪪

1. Tell me about your current role.

Include your field, sector, two responsibilities and one reason the work matters.

Model answer

I work in safety and environmental risk management in the railway sector. I usually analyse hazards and assess risks before operations. I also contribute to preventive actions. This work is important because good risk control protects people and supports reliable operations.

⚠️

2. You notice a new hazard. What do you do?

Use sequence words: first, then, after that, finally.

Model answer

First, I stop and keep a safe distance. Then I inform the supervisor and explain what I have observed. After that, the area can be checked and the risk can be assessed. Finally, we decide what control measure is necessary before work continues.

🎓

3. Why are you improving your English?

Connect your present situation, future plan and professional goal.

Model answer

I am improving my English because it is important for my studies and my professional development. I am currently preparing for the CLOE, and I am going to continue my studies in safety management and industrial risk. In the future, stronger English will also help me communicate more confidently in an international professional environment.
