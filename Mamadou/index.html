01

Present simple or present continuous?

Routine / fact: present simple. Now / temporary: present continuous.

I work in safety. → usual job   |   I am preparing for the CLOE. → happening now / current period
02

Present perfect: experience and duration

have/has + past participle connects past experience to now.

I have worked in the railway sector for four years. → for + duration   |   since 2022 → starting point
03

Sentence order: build clear English

Start with a reliable pattern: Subject + verb + object + place + time. The words below are deliberately mixed: rebuild each sentence before checking.

04

Reading: a near-miss report

First find the situation, then the hazard, action and result.

Near-miss during track maintenance

During a routine maintenance operation, a team noticed that a temporary barrier had been placed too close to an access route. No one was injured, but the position of the barrier could have created a collision risk for a maintenance vehicle. The supervisor stopped the operation, moved the barrier to a safe position and reminded the team of the access procedure. The event was recorded as a near miss. A short review was organised to understand why the barrier had been installed incorrectly and to prevent the same situation from happening again.

Pendant une opération de maintenance, une barrière temporaire était trop proche d'une voie d'accès. Il n'y a pas eu de blessé, mais il existait un risque de collision. Le superviseur a arrêté l'opération, déplacé la barrière et rappelé la procédure. L'événement a été enregistré comme presqu'accident puis analysé pour éviter qu'il ne se reproduise.
05

Listening: morning safety briefing

Listen twice. First: main idea. Second: details. Use the transcript only after your first listening attempt.

Voice: UK English
TRANSCRIPT

Good morning. Before the inspection starts, please make sure that you are wearing the required personal protective equipment. Today, the main risk is vehicle movement near the maintenance area, so use the marked pedestrian route and do not cross the access lane unless the supervisor confirms that it is safe. If you notice a new hazard, stop, keep a safe distance and report it immediately. We will review any changes to the work area at eleven o'clock.

ORAL SURVIVAL SYSTEM

What to say when you need time or clarification

Ces phrases vous permettent de rester dans l'interaction même si vous n'avez pas tout compris. Les utiliser correctement est une compétence, pas un échec.

Response pattern: Answer → Reason → Example

Question: Why is risk assessment important in your work?

ANSWER

Risk assessment is important because it helps us identify hazards before an incident happens.

REASON

It allows the team to decide which preventive measures are necessary.

EXAMPLE

For example, if there is vehicle movement near a work area, we can define a safe route and additional controls.

SCENARIO 1 · INTRODUCTION

An English-speaking auditor joins your team

Introduce your role and explain two responsibilities. Then answer: “What is the most important part of your job?”

Useful language

My role involves… · I am responsible for… · One of my main priorities is… · In my experience…

Model B1 answer

I work in safety, quality and environmental risk management in the railway sector. My role involves identifying risks and helping teams apply preventive actions. I also contribute to improving safety and environmental performance. I would say the most important part of my job is prevention, because identifying a risk early can help avoid an incident and protect both people and operations.

⚠️
SCENARIO 2 · NEAR MISS

Explain a safety problem to a supervisor

A temporary barrier is blocking part of a safe access route. Explain the hazard, the risk and the immediate action you recommend.

Useful language

I have noticed that… · This could create a risk of… · I recommend that we… · Before work continues, we should…

Model B1 answer

I have noticed that the temporary barrier is blocking part of the marked access route. This could create a collision risk because pedestrians may move closer to maintenance vehicles. I recommend stopping movement in this area for a moment and repositioning the barrier. Before work continues, we should check that the safe route is clear and inform the team about the change.

🌱
SCENARIO 3 · DISCUSSION

Safety and environment: can both improve together?

Give your opinion. Explain one way that good planning can reduce both operational risk and environmental impact.

Useful language

In my opinion… · One reason is that… · For example… · On the other hand… · Overall…

Model B1 answer

In my opinion, safety and environmental performance can improve together when work is planned carefully. One reason is that good planning reduces unexpected events, waste and unnecessary vehicle movements. For example, if materials and access routes are organised before maintenance starts, the team can work more safely and may also reduce fuel use and disruption. Overall, prevention can support both safety and environmental objectives.
