1) Confidence reset

Use English in real places

You have already studied many rules. Today, you will use them in real-life places: a boutique, a supermarket, a pharmacy, a butcher’s, a restaurant, a campsite, and a petrol station.

3) New grammar in a story

Past continuous + past simple

Use the past continuous for the background action. Use the past simple for the event that happened.

Utilise le past continuous pour l’action en cours dans le passé. Utilise le past simple pour l’événement qui arrive / interrompt.

Background action

I / he / she / it was + verb-ing

you / we / they were + verb-ing

I was waiting at the checkout.

They were looking for the shampoo.

What happened

past simple

The card machine stopped working.

A customer arrived late.

Story sentence

While I was paying, the card machine stopped working.

I was filling up the car when my card was refused.

While = pendant que. When = quand / lorsque.

SituationPast continuousPast simple event
At the supermarketI was waiting in the queuewhen I dropped my wallet.
At the pharmacyI was explaining my problemwhen the pharmacist asked for my card.
At the petrol stationI was filling up the carwhen the pump stopped.
At the salonI was helping a clientwhen the phone rang.
