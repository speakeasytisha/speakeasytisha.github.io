ASK POLITELYCould you…? / Would you mind…?

Could you speak a little more slowly, please?

RECOMMENDYou could… / I’d recommend…

If you like the sea, I’d recommend walking along the coast.

BUY TIMELet me think… / That’s a good question.

Use a short phrase instead of freezing when you need a second.

REPAIRSorry, I didn’t catch that.

Ask for repetition, a slower pace or a different explanation.

TELL A STORYFirst… then… after that… finally…

Four small connectors are enough to make a story easy to follow.

KEEP IT GOINGHow about you? / What about…?

Return the question and use one detail from the other person’s answer.

118–120 MIN · FROM TISHA

One last thing I want you to know

You should be very proud of what you have done.

Your progress did not come from one lesson, one grammar rule or one test. It came from your dedication. You took notes on everything. You practised. You did the exercises. You bought extra grammar books and worked in them. You listened to English. You read in English. You even went to campsites and looked for opportunities to hear people speaking and to try speaking yourself.

That is what made the difference.

You kept trying even when something felt difficult. You cared about understanding, not simply finishing an exercise. You wanted to improve, and you put in the work to make that happen.

I am genuinely proud of you, and I hope you are proud of yourself too. Keep using your English. Keep listening. Keep asking questions. Keep speaking even when it is not perfect. The more you use it, the more natural it will become.

Thank you for all the time we spent together, for your trust, your effort, your humour and your commitment. You will be missed.

Keep going. You have already proved that you can.

With pride,
Tisha
