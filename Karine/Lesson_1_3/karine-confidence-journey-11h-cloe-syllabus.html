Why this is different

This is your confidence phase

Before: learning the tools

Goal: communication

You do not need perfect English. You need clear sentences, polite phrases, one useful detail, and the confidence to continue.

Le but n’est pas la perfection. Le but est de communiquer clairement, avec plus de confiance.

Your CLOE speaking method

The 5-step answer path

1ListenWhat is the question?
2Find the clueusually, now, yesterday, tomorrow...
3Choose the tensepresent, past, future, experience
4Answer clearlyone complete sentence
5Add detailtime, place, reason, example

Simple target: Answer + detail + time expression.

Example: “Last weekend, I went to a restaurant with my husband. It was very nice because the restaurant had a sea view.”
