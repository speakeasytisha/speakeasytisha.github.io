GRAMMAR LAB 1 · THE MOST USEFUL TRAVEL SKILL

Build polite questions without translating

Instead of starting with French word order, choose an English question frame first. Then add the information you need.

IS THERE…?

One thing / uncountable

Is there a shuttle to the beach?

Is there parking at the hotel?

Is there + singular / uncountable?

Y a-t-il… ?

ARE THERE…?

More than one

Are there any restaurants nearby?

Are there towels by the pool?

Are there + plural?

Y a-t-il des… ?

DO YOU HAVE…?

Ask a business directly

Do you have an automatic eight-seater?

Do you have a table for six?

Do you have + noun?

Avez-vous… ?

DOES IT INCLUDE…?

Check what is included

Does the price include breakfast and dinner?

Does the insurance include roadside assistance?

Does + singular subject + base verb?

Est-ce que cela comprend… ?

COULD / WOULD

Make it more polite

Could you confirm the room type?

Would it be possible to check in early?

Could you + verb…? / Would it be possible…?

Pourriez-vous… ? / Serait-il possible… ?

DOyou / we / they + base verbDo you have…?
DOEShe / she / it + base verbDoes it include…?
ISsingular / uncountableIs there parking?
AREpluralAre there shops?
COULDpolite request + base verbCould you explain…?
Important: after does, the next verb returns to the base form: Does the price include…?, not Does the price includes…?. After could, there is no to: Could you confirm…?

GRAMMAR LAB 2 · YOUR SEPTEMBER PLANS

Going to, present continuous & would like

You already know future language. Now use each form for the job it does best in a travel conversation.

PLAN / INTENTION

be going to + verb

We’re going to explore the coast.

You have decided the plan; the exact details may still change.

Projet / intention déjà décidé(e).

ARRANGEMENT

present continuous

We’re picking up the car at the airport.

There is an arrangement, reservation or fixed detail.

Arrangement prévu / réservé.

PREFERENCE

would like + noun / to + verb

I’d like to visit Tavolara.

This is a wish or preference, not necessarily a fixed plan.

Souhait / préférence.

QUICK CONTRAST

All three can be true

We’re staying near Olbia. We’re going to explore by car. I’d like to take a boat trip.

GRAMMAR LAB 3 · HOTEL + RESTAURANT ENGLISH

Some, any, much & many — only what you need

Use this mini-system to ask about food, drinks, services and quantities without getting stuck.

SOME

Positive statements + offers

There is some water in the room.

Would you like some bread?

ANY

Most questions + negatives

Do you have any vegetarian dishes?

There isn’t any parking.

MANY

Countable plural

How many people is the table for?

How many bags do you have?

MUCH

Uncountable

How much water would you like?

How much does the deposit cost?

Travel shortcut: for prices, English normally asks How much is it? or How much does it cost? “How many” is for countable things: people, bags, nights, rooms.

HOTEL CHECK-IN

Listen for the four confirmed details

Show / hide transcript

Receptionist: Good afternoon. Welcome. How can I help you?

You: Hello. I have a reservation under the name Karine for seven nights, from September 20th to the 27th.

Receptionist: Yes, I have it here. You booked a sea-view double room with half board.

You: Great. Could you confirm what half board includes, please?

Receptionist: Of course. Breakfast and dinner are included. Drinks at dinner are not included.

You: Thank you. Is there free parking at the hotel?

Receptionist: Yes, parking is free for hotel guests.

You: Perfect. And are beach towels provided?

Receptionist: Yes. You can collect them from reception with a €10 refundable deposit.

Collect your eight-seater without guessing

02Drivers

03Money

deposit · excess · card · extra charge

04Protection

insurance · damage · roadside assistance

05Return

fuel policy · return time · drop-off location

C

AT THE RENTAL DESK

Listen for the conditions

Show / hide transcript

Agent: May I see your driving licence and the credit card used for the booking?

You: Of course. We reserved an eight-seater vehicle for seven days. Is it an automatic?

Agent: Yes. The booking includes one main driver and basic insurance.

You: Can we add another driver?

Agent: Yes, for an additional charge.

You: What is the fuel policy?

Agent: Full to full. Please return the vehicle with a full tank.

You: And how much is the deposit?

Agent: €600. It will be blocked on the main driver’s credit card.

You: Could you show me where to return the vehicle next Sunday?

SPEAKING MISSIONS

Your voice does the travelling now

Choose one mission live. Try first, then compare with the models. The A1 model is safe, the A2 model is your target, and the B1 model is a stretch — not an expectation.

HOTEL · CHECK-IN

Confirm the reservation

Say your name, dates and number of nights. Confirm the sea-view room and half board. Ask one practical question.

A1 model

Hello. I have a reservation for seven nights. We have a sea-view room and half board. Is that correct? What time is dinner?

A2 target model

Hello. I have a reservation under the name Karine from September 20th to the 27th. We’re staying for seven nights. Could you confirm that we have a sea-view room with half board, please? Also, what time is dinner served?

B1 stretch model

Good afternoon. I have a reservation under the name Karine for seven nights, checking in today and leaving next Sunday. I’d just like to confirm that our booking is for a sea-view room with half board. Could you also tell me what time dinner is served and whether we need to reserve a table?

CAR · PICK-UP

Collect the eight-seater

Confirm the vehicle, ask about another driver, fuel policy and deposit.

A1 model

Hello. We reserved an eight-seater. Is it automatic? Can we add another driver? What is the fuel policy? How much is the deposit?

A2 target model

Hello. We have a reservation for an eight-seater vehicle for seven days. Could you confirm that it’s an automatic? We may need an additional driver. How much does that cost? Also, what is the fuel policy and how much is the deposit?

B1 stretch model

Hello. I’m here to collect an eight-seater vehicle that we reserved for the week. Before we take the keys, could you confirm whether the booking includes an automatic vehicle and basic insurance? We’d also like to add a second driver. Could you explain the additional charge, the fuel policy and the deposit, please?

RESTAURANT · DINNER

Book and order

Ask for a table, request a sea view if possible, ask for a recommendation and order a drink.

A1 model

Hello. Do you have a table for six at 8 p.m.? We would like a table outside, please. What do you recommend? Could we have some sparkling water?

A2 target model

Hello. Could I book a table for six for 8 p.m., please? If possible, we’d like to sit outside with a sea view. We’d also like to try a typical Sardinian dish. What would you recommend? And could we have some sparkling water for the table?

B1 stretch model

Good evening. I’d like to book a table for six at around 8 p.m. If you have availability, we’d love an outdoor table with a sea view. We’re interested in trying something typical from Sardinia, so could you recommend one local pasta dish and one fish dish? We’ll also have a bottle of sparkling water for the table.

PROBLEM · ROOM

The view is wrong

You booked a sea-view room, but your room faces the car park. Stay polite and ask for a solution.

A1 model

Excuse me. We booked a sea-view room, but this room does not have a sea view. Could you check the booking, please? Can we change rooms?

A2 target model

Excuse me. I think there may be a problem with our room. We booked a sea-view room, but this room faces the car park. Could you check our reservation, please? Would it be possible to move to the room type we booked?

B1 stretch model

Excuse me. I’m sorry to bother you, but I think there’s been a mistake with our room. Our confirmation says that we booked a sea-view room, whereas the room we’ve been given faces the car park. Could you check the reservation and let us know whether it would be possible to move to a sea-view room?
