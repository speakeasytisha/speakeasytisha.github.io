Exam mantra

Simple, complete and clear is enough.

What you need to understand

The exam is not asking for perfect English

Reading

You do not need to translate the full text. You need to find the information that answers the question.

Writing

You do not need a beautiful email. You need a complete email that answers the questions clearly and politely.

Speaking

You do not need perfect grammar. You need to keep speaking, organise your answer and give enough information.

Timer

The timer is not there to scare you. It tells you when to stop planning, when to write, and when to move on.

Success techniques — must be automatic

Three methods to repeat until they feel natural

Emergency anti-panic routine

  1. Stop for 2 seconds. Do not click randomly.
  2. Breathe once. Relax shoulders and jaw.
  3. Read the task again. What exactly is asked?
  4. Choose the simplest path. Direct answer first.
  5. Continue. One imperfect answer is not the whole exam.
