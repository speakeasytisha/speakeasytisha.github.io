1 • Grammar foundation

I / me / my / mine / myself

Use I before the verb, me after a verb or preposition, my before a noun, and myself when the subject and object are the same person.

I

I = subject, the person doing the action.

I live in Kientzville.
I worked in technical support.

me

me = object, after a verb or preposition.

Tisha helped me.
Could you tell me?

my / mine

my + noun. mine replaces my + noun.

This is my village.
This lesson is mine.

myself

myself = reflexive or emphasis.

I introduce myself.
I prepared myself for the exam.

Important correction

My husband and I = subject: My husband and I travelled to Paris.

my husband and me = object: Tisha gave my husband and me the link.

2 • Communication grammar

Say vs Tell

Say

Use say for the words or information.

  • She said, “Good morning.”
  • She said that she was ready.
  • She said to me that I was improving.

Do not say: She said me.

Tell

Use tell with a person.

  • She told me the answer.
  • She told me that I was ready.
  • She told me to practise.
  • She told a story.

Do not say: She told to me.

Fast rule

say + words/information

tell + person + words/information
