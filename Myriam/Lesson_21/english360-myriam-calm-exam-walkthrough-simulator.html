    How to succeed

    Listening

    Read the question and answers first. Listen for keywords and synonyms. Choose the best answer, not every word.

    Grammar

    Look before and after the blank. Decide: preposition, verb form, tense, modal or expression.

    Reading

    Read the question first, then scan the text. The answer is usually in one sentence or just around it.

    Writing

    Greeting + answer all questions + details + polite closing. Simple, complete and logical.

    Speaking

    Answer + detail + reason + example. Keep speaking until the end.

    Panic reset
