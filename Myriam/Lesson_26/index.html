1 · NATURAL ENGLISH1 pt

After I, use agree, not “am agree”. Compare with -er: quiet → quieter.
✅ I agree because the countryside is quieter.
Clear opinion + reason + correct comparative.
2 · PAST SIMPLE1 pt

Choose the correct form.

Last weekend, we ___ for a long walk and ___ some mushrooms.

Both verbs are irregular: go → went; find → found.
3 · CONNECTORS1 pt

Which answer sounds the most complete?

Look for: opinion + reason + example + conclusion.
4 · SENTENCE BUILDER2 pts
