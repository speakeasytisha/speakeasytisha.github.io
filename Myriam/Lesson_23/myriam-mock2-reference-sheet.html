A calm village in Alsace

2 min

Kientzville is a small village in Alsace, close to Sélestat and about 45 kilometres from Strasbourg. It has about 500 inhabitants, so it is much quieter than a large city. There are no shops in the village, but the area is pleasant for people who enjoy nature and peaceful surroundings.

The village has an interesting local history. It was created after the Second World War by Mr Kientz, a textile industrialist whose factory was in Scherwiller, about one kilometre away. He bought land to build houses for workers.

Today, Kientzville is a calm place to live. Visitors can explore nearby towns, go for walks and discover other villages in Alsace. It is a good choice for people who like quiet places rather than busy tourist centres.

R6

Preparing for an online exam

2 min

Before an online language exam, it is important to prepare both the English and the technical environment. A quiet room, a stable internet connection and a working microphone can help you feel more comfortable before the first question appears.

During the test, it is better to keep a simple method for each section. In reading, read the question first and scan the text for keywords. In writing, answer all parts of the prompt with short clear paragraphs. In speaking, give a direct answer, add a detail and finish with a reason or example.

The objective is not to produce perfect English. The objective is to communicate clearly, manage time and continue calmly until the final screen.
