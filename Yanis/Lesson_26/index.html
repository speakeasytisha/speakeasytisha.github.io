CANDIDATE CHECK-IN

Know what “B1” needs to look like

Simulation note. This page follows the current LILATE professional competency framework and the published maximum test duration, but the scenarios and interface are original preparation material—not official exam questions or an official copy of the platform.
01

Understand first

Catch the person, the problem and the important detail before you answer.

Identifiez la personne, le problème et le détail important avant de répondre.

02

Interact

Ask a useful question when information is missing. Interaction is stronger than guessing.

Posez une question utile si une information manque. Il vaut mieux interagir que deviner.

03

Reformulate

Show the examiner that you understood by saying the idea in your own words.

Montrez que vous avez compris en reformulant avec vos propres mots.

04

Give a next step

Close with an action: what you will check, explain, report or do next.

Terminez par une action claire : vérifier, expliquer, transmettre ou agir.

B1 TARGET

Clear beats complicated

You can make mistakes and still show B1 if your message stays understandable, connected and professionally useful.

UNDERSTAND → CLARIFY → REFORMULATE → ACT → CONFIRM

What to say if you get stuck

02

PRE-EXAM LANGUAGE CHECK

Grammar you need under pressure

12–15 min
ROUTINE / SCHEDULE

Present simple

The flight leaves at 14:20.

Keywords: usually, every day, normally, always, timetable.

HAPPENING NOW

Present continuous

Passengers are boarding now.

Keywords: now, at the moment, currently, right now.

FINISHED EVENT

Past simple

The passenger lost her passport this morning.

Keywords: yesterday, last, ago, this morning (finished).

RESULT NOW

Present perfect

The gate has changed.

Keywords: already, just, yet, ever, never, recently.

REAL FUTURE

First conditional

If + present, will + base verb

If the seat is free, I will move you.

HYPOTHETICAL

Second conditional

If + past, would + base verb

If a passenger felt unwell, I would call the senior crew member.

PROFESSIONAL CONTROL

Modals

must / have to obligation · should advice · could polite request.

Could you place your bag under the seat?

SAFE REPORTING

Reformulation frames

If I understand correctly…

According to the message…

The main issue is…

How to read fast
  1. Find the category in the question.
  2. Locate the exact line.
  3. Answer with the fact.
  4. If asked “what would you do?”, connect the fact to an action.

ORAL TRANSFER

Passenger with a short connection

A passenger to Edinburgh asks: “Can you guarantee that I can leave the aircraft first?”

B1 target: “I understand you have a short connection. I can check the latest connection information, but I cannot guarantee priority disembarkation. If you may need assistance, I’ll inform the senior crew member.”
