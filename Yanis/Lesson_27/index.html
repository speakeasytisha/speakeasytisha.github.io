High-yield grammar rescue

Polite professional requests

Could you + base verb…? · Could you show me your boarding pass?
Would you mind + -ing…? · Would you mind moving your bag?

Explain a situation now

Present continuous: The crew is checking the cabin. Boarding is starting now.

Report what happened

Past simple: A passenger reported a missing bag. The gate agent called the crew.

Give the next step

will / be going to: I will check that for you. We are going to update you as soon as we have more information.

Use a safe conditional

If + present, will + base verb: If a seat becomes available, I will let you know.

Relay information simply

According to… / The message says…: According to the gate agent, boarding will start at 18:20.

Mission 1

Professional introduction

“Good afternoon. Before we begin, could you tell me about yourself and the role you would like to do?”

Your task

Give a natural 45–60 second introduction. Explain your professional goal and why you would like to work as cabin crew.

Simple structure: present role → strengths → cabin-crew goal → why English matters.
A2 support

I work as a receptionist. I like helping people and working in a team. I would like to become a steward because I enjoy customer service and travel. I am improving my English to communicate with passengers.

B1 target

I currently work in reception, so I am used to welcoming people, solving small problems and staying calm. I would like to become cabin crew because I enjoy customer contact and teamwork. I know that clear English is essential for helping international passengers, especially when something unexpected happens.

B1+ upgrade

My current experience in reception has taught me to listen carefully, adapt to different people and remain professional under pressure. Cabin crew appeals to me because it combines safety, service and teamwork. I am particularly motivated to strengthen my English so I can react clearly and confidently with international passengers.

Mission 2 · Passenger role-play

Seat problem during boarding

Passenger

“Excuse me, I think someone is sitting in my seat. My boarding pass says 14A, but the passenger says this is his seat.”

Your task

Take control of the interaction. Ask for the information you need, clarify the problem and explain the next step without making an unrealistic promise.

The examiner may change the situation after your first response.

Could I see both boarding passes, please? · Let me check the seat numbers. · I’m sorry about the confusion. · I’ll find the correct solution for you.

A2 support

I’m sorry. Can I see your boarding pass, please? I will check the seat numbers.

B1 target

I’m sorry about the confusion. Could I see both boarding passes, please? I’ll check the seat numbers and passenger names. If there is a duplicate seat assignment, I’ll contact the appropriate colleague and explain the next step.

B1+ upgrade

I understand why that is confusing. May I check both boarding passes? I’ll verify the seat numbers and names first. If there has been a duplicate allocation, I’ll coordinate with the gate team or senior crew member so we can resolve it as quickly as possible.

Ready
Mission 3 · Pressure follow-up

Maintain the interaction

Examiner follow-up

“I only have thirty-five minutes before my next flight. I’m in row 29. Can I get off first?”

Your task

Respond professionally, show empathy, avoid an unauthorised promise and give a realistic next step.

A2 support

I understand. I’m sorry, but I cannot promise that. I will inform my colleagues about your connection.

B1 target

I understand that you’re worried about your connection. I can’t promise that you’ll be able to leave the aircraft first, but I can inform the crew about your situation and make sure your connection is reported. We’ll give you the latest information as soon as we have it.

B1+ upgrade

I completely understand your concern. I’m not able to guarantee priority disembarkation, but I can pass your connection details to the crew and ask what assistance is available on arrival. I’ll keep you updated as soon as we receive confirmed information.

Captain’s operational message

B1 target

Ladies and gentlemen, the captain has advised us that we may experience moderate turbulence approximately twenty minutes after take-off. B1+ upgrade

Ladies and gentlemen, the captain has advised us to expect a period of moderate turbulence around twenty minutes after departure. Please remain seated with your seat belt securely fastened whenever the sign is on. As a precaution, the hot drinks service will be delayed until conditions are stable. We’ll provide another update as soon as we can. Thank you for your cooperation.

Mission 2 · Passenger complaint

Connection under pressure

Passenger

“This delay is ridiculous. My connection leaves in forty minutes and nobody is telling me anything. I need you to guarantee that someone will be waiting for me.”

Your task

De-escalate the situation. Separate what you know from what you cannot guarantee. Ask one useful question and give a realistic next step.

The passenger may challenge your first answer.

1 Empathise → I understand why you’re concerned. 2 Clarify → What is your connecting flight number? 3 Boundary → I can’t guarantee… 4 Action → I can report your connection and give you the latest confirmed information.

A2 support

I understand. What is your next flight number, please? I cannot guarantee that someone will wait, but I can inform my colleagues and give you the latest information.

B1 target

I understand why you’re frustrated, especially with a tight connection. Could you give me your connecting flight number, please? I can’t guarantee that someone will be waiting for you, but I can report your connection to the crew and check what confirmed assistance is available when we arrive.

B1+ upgrade

I understand how stressful that is when your connection is close. May I take your onward flight number first? I don’t want to make a promise that I can’t confirm, but I can make sure your connection is reported and ask for the latest arrival assistance information so we can give you a clear next step.

Ready
Mission 3 · Crew handover

Transmit the situation to the senior crew member

Your task

After speaking with the passenger, give the senior crew member a 30–45 second factual handover: passenger concern → verified information → what you said → action needed.

B1 target

The passenger in 29C has a tight connection and is concerned about missing flight KL1423. I explained that I couldn’t guarantee priority disembarkation or that the next flight would wait. I asked for the connection details and told the passenger I would report the situation. Could you confirm what arrival assistance we can offer?

DELAYED +35

Flight timeline

  • 18:22 Technical inspection extended

Passenger alerts

Crew note

The gate team will provide a new update at 18:50. Do not promise rebooking before confirmation. Passengers with tight connections should be identified and reported to the senior crew member.

Service note

Water is available at the gate. Boarding order is unchanged. Cabin crew should be ready to support families and passengers requiring assistance once boarding begins.

Identify your priorities

Tell the examiner the three priorities you would deal with first and explain why.

Ready
B1 target

First, I would identify and report the passenger with the tight connection because the delay may affect the onward flight. Second, I would make sure the passenger requiring mobility assistance remains correctly coordinated for arrival. Third, I would prepare to support the family with young children during boarding. I would also make sure nobody is promised rebooking before we have confirmation.

Task B · Passenger interaction

Explain the delay

Ms. Novak asks: “Am I going to miss my connection? Can you rebook me now?” Respond using only confirmed information.

Ready
B1 target

I understand your concern. At the moment, departure is estimated for 19:25, so your connection may be tight. I can’t confirm a rebooking yet because we haven’t received that instruction. I’ll report your connection to the senior crew member, and the gate team will give another update at 18:50. I’ll share any confirmed information with you as soon as possible.

Task C · Written exploitation

Write the handover after the disruption

Write a professional 80–110 word handover to the senior crew member. Include the delay, the most urgent passenger issues, what is confirmed, what is not confirmed, and the next update.

1 Situation → flight + delay. 2 Priority passengers → connection / assistance / family. 3 Boundaries → no rebooking promise. 4 Next action → report + update time.

B1 target

AF604 to Copenhagen is currently delayed by approximately 35 minutes, with departure estimated at 19:25. Ms. Novak in 21B has a connection at 20:50 and should be reported as a tight-connection passenger. Mr. Lee in 8D has reduced-mobility assistance confirmed on arrival. The Martin family in row 17 is travelling with two young children. We have not received authorisation to promise any rebooking. The gate team will provide another operational update at 18:50, and passengers should be informed only with confirmed information.
