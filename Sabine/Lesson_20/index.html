Your full travel conversation

One-hour review: 10 min introduction and work · 8 min small talk · 10 min verbs and preparation · 22 min travel counters, hotel, restaurant and directions · 10 min final role-play and recap.

Use your own trip, or imagine travelling from France to Boston and then visiting Provincetown. The times and bookings in the practice are examples.

  1. Introduce yourself and give two details about your work. Ask your partner a question.
  2. At the airport, show your passport, check a bag and confirm the gate. On arrival, find your bag and ask how to get into town.
  3. At the hotel, give the booking name and ask about breakfast. At the restaurant, order and ask for the check.
  4. Show your ticket at an attraction. Ask for directions back and repeat one detail to confirm.
