03 / YOUR TRAINING CENTRE

How your Welcome training works

Your training is organised through Welcome. Your trainer guides your lessons; the training centre handles the administrative follow-up.

Before the lesson

Join your scheduled video session, open your lesson page and have your notes ready.

During the lesson

Discuss, practise, ask questions and work on objectives linked to your needs.

After the lesson

Review your lesson link, revisit exercises and use your notes to prepare for next time.

Your planned training: 19 hours · Starting reference: A2.2+ · Target: B1.1−. These are initial reference points, to be discussed together. The LILATE certification is part of the overall pathway; we will return to its format later.
How will a typical lesson unfold?

1. Short conversation and review. 2. Clear vocabulary and language explanation. 3. Guided exercises with feedback. 4. Realistic speaking or writing scenario. 5. Summary and next steps. The balance will change depending on your preferences.

04 / SIGNING YOUR ATTENDANCE

Digiforma, step by step.

Welcome uses Digiforma for administrative follow-up. One important action is signing your attendance for each lesson (l'émargement). Here is what to look for when a signature is requested.

01
FIRST · CHECK YOUR EMAIL

Find the attendance-signature message

Look in the inbox of the email address given to Welcome for a message about émargement or signature de présence. Check spam if needed. This is a signature request, not your video-class link.

Cherchez l'e-mail contenant la demande de signature de présence. Le lien d'émargement est différent du lien de visioconférence.

02
THEN · OPEN THE LINK

Open the signature link in that message

Use the specific link provided for your attendance form. Check that it concerns your name and the correct session/date before proceeding. Do not sign another person's form.

03
NEXT · SIGN

Complete the electronic signature

Follow the instructions shown on the attendance page. Depending on the form, you may be asked to draw your signature with your mouse, trackpad or finger, or confirm it another way. Select the on-screen confirmation or validation button if shown.

Signez avec la souris, le pavé tactile ou le doigt selon le support, puis validez si un bouton de confirmation apparaît.

04
FINALLY · CHECK

Make sure the request was completed

Check for an on-screen completion message if one is displayed. If the link has not arrived, is expired, or the form is unclear, tell your trainer or contact Welcome so it can be checked.

Important: The email, timing and exact buttons depend on how Welcome sends the signature request. We will look at the actual message together if available. This lesson does not contain a real signing link, and no signature can be made here.

13 / YOUR AUDIO SETUP

Choose the voice that helps you learn.

Audio is optional and never plays automatically. You can select any installed voice your browser makes available, including British or American English.

Voice preferences

Your browser will list the voices currently available on your device.

Speaking & recording

Use the microphone button below to try speech recognition only if your browser supports it. You can always speak aloud with your trainer even when recording is unavailable.

Speech recognition is optional and browser-dependent.

Microphone permission may be requested. Recognition may use a browser service. Availability varies by browser, operating system, language and permissions; this feature is not guaranteed on Mac or PC. No recording is stored or sent by this page itself.

Windows PC · how to add or change English voices
  1. Open Settings → Time & language → Language & region.
  2. Under preferred languages, add English (United Kingdom) or English (United States) if needed.
  3. Open the language's options and install available speech/text-to-speech features where offered. Some Windows versions list voices under Accessibility → Narrator.
  4. Restart your browser, refresh the lesson and choose a voice in the dropdown above. Available voices depend on the browser and the installed language packs.
Mac · how to choose voices
  1. Open System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content (or the speech/voice setting shown on your macOS version).
  2. Open System Voice or Manage Voices, then explore available British or American English voices and download one if offered.
  3. Restart your browser, refresh this page and choose from the available voice list above.
  4. Microphone-based speech recognition may not be supported in your browser, even when audio playback works. Safari and Chrome can behave differently.
Not seeing the voice you installed? Try closing and reopening the browser, then refresh. Browser voice lists are not identical on every device. The UK/US quick switch at the top chooses an accent automatically when an appropriate voice is available.
