03 · Grammar

that, which and where: add useful detail

that

For a thing or animal when the information identifies it.

It is a machine that carries visitors.

which

For a thing, often to add extra information.

The carousel, which has three levels, is extraordinary.

where

For a place.

It is an island where you can see unusual machines.

that = qui/que pour une chose ; which = qui/que, souvent pour ajouter une information ; where = où pour un lieu.

04 · Grammar review

Tell what happened and what was happening

PAST SIMPLE

Completed events

You went to Nantes.

You visited the attraction.

You rode the carousel.

PAST CONTINUOUS

Background / action in progress

People were watching.

The elephant was moving.

Visitors were taking photos.

WHEN

People were watching when the elephant sprayed water.

WHILE

While you were exploring, visitors were taking photos.

Le Past Simple fait avancer l’histoire. Le Past Continuous décrit une action en cours ou le décor. when relie souvent un décor à un événement court ; while relie souvent deux actions en cours.

06 · CLOE reading

A mechanical world on the Île de Nantes

On the Île de Nantes, visitors can enter an unusual world of mechanical creatures. One of the best-known attractions is the Grand Éléphant, a large moving machine that carries visitors. Nearby, the Carrousel des Mondes Marins presents an imaginary mechanical ocean on three levels: the seabed, the abyss and the sea surface. Its strange creatures combine engineering, movement and imagination. The Galerie des Machines continues the experience with a mechanical bestiary. What makes the site memorable is the way art and engineering work together. Visitors are not simply looking at static objects: they can watch machines move, discover how mechanisms work and experience the atmosphere around them.

Sur l’Île de Nantes, les visiteurs découvrent un univers de créatures mécaniques. Le Grand Éléphant transporte des visiteurs. Le Carrousel des Mondes Marins présente un océan mécanique imaginaire sur trois niveaux. L’art, l’ingénierie et le mouvement rendent le lieu mémorable.

12 · Final mission

CLOE oral challenge: a memorable weekend

Tell the examiner about your weekend in Nantes.

Explain where you went, who you went with, what you saw and did, one unusual detail, and why the experience was memorable.

5-part route: 1) Last Sunday... 2) First, we... 3) While... 4) After that... 5) What I liked most was... because...
Last Sunday, I went to Nantes with my daughter and my granddaughter for a family day out. We visited the Île de Nantes, where there are some extraordinary mechanical attractions. First, we discovered the Grand Éléphant, a huge moving machine that visitors can go inside. While we were exploring, the elephant was moving slowly and people were watching it. Afterwards, we went on the Carrousel des Mondes Marins, which has three levels filled with unusual mechanical sea creatures. What I liked most was the originality of the attractions because they combine art, engineering and movement. It was a fascinating and memorable day with my family.
