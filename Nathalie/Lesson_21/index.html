03 · GRAMMAR ENGINE 1

Past simple or past continuous?

Use one tense for the main events, and the other to paint the scene around them.

PAST SIMPLE

Finished event

Subject + past form

We stopped near the coast.

We saw a beautiful lighthouse.

Think: What happened next?

PAST CONTINUOUS

Scene / action in progress

was / were + verb-ing

The wind was blowing.

We were driving along the coast.

Think: What was happening at that moment?

TOGETHER

Use when and while

Past continuous + when + past simple

We were walking on the beach when it started to rain.

While + past continuous, past simple / past continuous

While we were having lunch, the sun came out.

04 · GRAMMAR ENGINE 2

Time, distance and duration

These small structures make a travel story precise and natural.

It took us + time + to…

It took us four hours to get there.

We stayed + for + duration

We stayed for three nights.

We arrived + in / at

We arrived in Brittany / at the campsite.

We drove + from…to…

We drove from one village to another.

Remember: for answers “how long?” · in = city/region/country · at = precise place.

TOO CHOPPY

We arrived near the sea. It was windy. We walked on the beach. We were happy.

MORE NATURAL

When we arrived near the sea, it was very windy, but we still walked on the beach because the view was beautiful.

07 · WRITING

A short travel diary

Write 90–120 words. Keep it personal, clear and connected.

Your mission

Write about your Brittany road trip with your granddaughter. Include:

  • where you went and how you travelled
  • how long the journey / trip lasted
  • what the weather was like
  • one activity or special moment
  • your favourite place and why
0 wordsAim for 90–120 words

MODEL ANSWERS

Compare only after you write

We went to Brittany in a camper van and travelled along the coast. The journey took several hours, but it was comfortable. We stayed for a few days and visited different places. The weather was sometimes sunny and sometimes windy. One day, while we were walking near the sea, we saw a beautiful view. I really enjoyed travelling with my granddaughter because we had time together. My favourite place was near the coast because it was peaceful and very beautiful. I would like to go back one day.

During our road trip in Brittany, my granddaughter and I travelled by camper van and discovered several places along the coast. It took us a few hours to reach our first stop, and we stayed in the region for several days. Although the weather changed quite often, we were lucky to have some beautiful sunny periods. While we were driving from one place to another, we could see the sea, small villages and impressive landscapes. What I enjoyed most was the freedom of travelling by camper van and sharing the experience with my granddaughter. My favourite stop was the one with the most spectacular coastal view, because the atmosphere was both wild and peaceful.
