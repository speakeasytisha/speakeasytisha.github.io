Preparation hub

Instructions, grammar and vocabulary before you start

Vocabulary

Read the full sentence first. Eliminate words that do not fit the grammar, register or collocation.

Grammar

Look for time markers, auxiliaries, prepositions, countability and the structure after modals.

Expressions

Choose the response that is both grammatically correct and socially appropriate for the situation.

Reading

Answer from the text. Separate the main purpose from supporting details and beware of one-word traps.

Listening

Listen for intention, dates, numbers, actions and contrasts. Do not panic if you miss one word.

Oral

Answer, explain, give an example, then invite interaction. If necessary, ask for repetition naturally.

Stratégie : cherchez les indices grammaticaux et le sens global. À l’oral, développez vos réponses avec une raison et un exemple.
