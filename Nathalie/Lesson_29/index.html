Mission 02 · 10 minutes

Your story: work, family and life

Present simple

Habits and facts: I live near… / I enjoy…

Negative: I don’t work now.

Question: Where do you live?

Past simple

Finished periods: I worked with children.

Negative: I didn’t work in a hotel.

Question: What did you do?

Used to

Past routine, different now: I used to work in childcare.

Present perfect

Experience or continued period: I have lived here for many years.

Présent simple : habitudes/faits. Prétérit : période terminée. Used to : habitudes passées qui ont changé. Present perfect : expérience ou situation qui continue jusqu’à maintenant.

Your life in six steps

  1. Where do you live? What do you like about the area?
  2. What did you use to do for work?
  3. What were your responsibilities in childcare?
  4. What do you enjoy doing now?
  5. How long have you lived in your area?
  6. What is something you are proud of?
I live near the Lac de Grand-Lieu. I used to work with young children. I helped them with their daily activities. Now I am retired. I enjoy spending time with my family and travelling.
I live near the Lac de Grand-Lieu, an area I enjoy because of its natural surroundings. Before I retired, I worked in early childhood care, where my responsibilities included looking after young children and supporting their daily activities. These days, I enjoy travelling and spending time with my family.

Mission 03 · 10 minutes

Your adventures make wonderful stories

Past simple

Sequence: We went to Nantes. We visited the elephant.

Past continuous

Background: People were taking photos.

When / while

We were watching when the elephant sprayed water.

That / which / where

Nantes is a city where you can see mechanical animals.

Past simple : action terminée qui fait avancer le récit. Past continuous : action en cours. When : événement qui intervient. While : souvent pendant deux actions en cours. Where = où (lieu).
