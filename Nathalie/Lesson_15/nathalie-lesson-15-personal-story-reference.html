FAITHFUL CORRECTION

✨ Your corrected natural version

Teacher’s note: This text keeps your information and your personality while making your English more natural. “I’m not officially retired” explains that you stopped working without necessarily being officially retired.

My name is Nathalie, and I live in La Chevrolière, near Nantes.

In La Chevrolière, we have Lake Grand-Lieu, which is home to many birds, including ducks, geese, wading birds and great egrets. It is a nature reserve. In the distance, you can see the Guerlain family’s house because Lake Grand-Lieu once belonged to the Guerlain family, who are famous for perfume. Seven professional fishermen are allowed to fish for eels and American crayfish there.

I’m 61 years old. I’m not officially retired, but I stopped working as a childcare assistant when I was 60 so that I could travel more. I worked in a hospital, a nursery and in people’s homes.

I was born in northern France. I lived in Senegal for two years and near Paris for 35 years.

I’m married, and I have three children: one daughter and two sons. I also have a five-year-old granddaughter.

In my free time, I enjoy walking, gardening, knitting, sewing, reading, African dance, African percussion, spending time with my family and, above all, travelling.

I enjoy discovering new cultures, walking around cities, trying local food, relaxing on the beach, hiking, visiting elephant sanctuaries, meeting children at their schools and learning about different ways of life.

My husband and I often travel to Asia because we find the people very kind, friendly and peaceful. We usually go to Asia in winter because the weather is warmer than in France. In spring, we like visiting English-speaking countries because I enjoy British culture.

I particularly like pubs. They are lively and welcoming, and you often see people with their families and dogs, singing and playing guitars.

I practised karate for many years with my son before discovering African dance. I love dancing, and I also play a little guitar.

I love animals. I have two cats, some chickens and three sheep because we have a large garden.

Every Wednesday and during the school holidays, I look after my granddaughter. Spending time with her is a real pleasure.

My dream is to feel more confident speaking English and to understand it more easily.

TWO READY-TO-SPEAK LEVELS

🎤 Choose the model that feels right

The A2 model is short and secure. The B1 model uses more detail, connectors and personal explanations.

A2

About 1–1½ minutes

Hello, my name is Nathalie. I’m 61 years old, and I live in La Chevrolière, near Nantes.

My town is close to Lake Grand-Lieu. It is a beautiful nature reserve with many birds. There are ducks, geese and great egrets.

I worked as a childcare assistant in a hospital, a nursery and in people’s homes. I stopped working when I was 60 because I wanted to travel more.

I’m married and I have three children and one granddaughter. She is five years old, and I look after her every Wednesday.

In my free time, I enjoy walking, gardening, knitting, sewing, reading, African dance and travelling. My husband and I often travel to Asia in winter because the weather is warmer than in France.

I also love animals. I have two cats, some chickens and three sheep.

My goal is to feel more confident speaking English and to understand people more easily.

Keywords: La Chevrolière • childcare assistant • family • hobbies • Asia • animals • English goal
B1

About 2–3 minutes

Hello, my name is Nathalie. I’m 61 years old, and I live in La Chevrolière, a small town near Nantes.

One of the most special places near my home is Lake Grand-Lieu. It is a protected nature reserve and is home to many species of birds, such as ducks, geese, wading birds and great egrets. In the distance, you can also see the Guerlain family’s house. The lake once belonged to this family, who are well known for perfume. Today, only seven professional fishermen are allowed to fish there for eels and American crayfish.

I’m not officially retired, but I stopped working when I was 60 so that I could have more time to travel. I worked as a childcare assistant in several different settings, including a hospital, a nursery and people’s homes. I was born in northern France, lived in Senegal for two years and then spent 35 years near Paris.

I’m married and have three children: one daughter and two sons. I also have a five-year-old granddaughter. I look after her every Wednesday and during the school holidays, and spending time with her is always a pleasure.

I have many hobbies. I enjoy walking, gardening, knitting, sewing, reading, African dance and percussion. I also play a little guitar. However, travelling is probably my greatest passion because it gives me the opportunity to discover new cultures, try local food and learn about different ways of life.

My husband and I often travel to Asia in winter because the climate is warmer than in France and we find the people very kind and peaceful. In spring, we enjoy visiting English-speaking countries because I’m interested in British culture. I particularly like the friendly atmosphere in pubs, where people sometimes come with their families and dogs and sing while someone plays the guitar.

I’m also very fond of animals. Because we have a large garden, we have two cats, some chickens and three sheep.

My main goal now is to become more confident when I speak English and to understand spoken English more easily, especially when I travel.

Connectors: One of the most special… • including… • so that… • however… • because… • especially…
