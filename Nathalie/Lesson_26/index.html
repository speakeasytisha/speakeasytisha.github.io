04 · Question power

Who · what · when · where · why · how

Main rule: question word + auxiliary + subject + verb. FR: mot interrogatif + auxiliaire + sujet + verbe.
WHO

people

Who did you work with?
WHAT

things / actions

What did you do?
WHEN

time

When did you start?
WHERE

place

Where did you work?
WHY

reason

Why did you enjoy it?
HOW

method / manner

How did you solve the problem?

08 · Your professional story

Your childcare career — turn experience into confident English

Your confirmed background: you worked as a childcare assistant in hospitals, nurseries and private homes. Choose only the responsibilities below that match your real experience; the builder will never force an unconfirmed detail into your answer.

Start your answer

I worked as… · My role was to… · I used to…

Add responsibilities

I was responsible for… · I had to… · I often…

Add skills

The job required patience, observation, communication and adaptability.

Add reflection

What I liked most was… · This experience taught me…

Choose the responsibilities that are true for you

Job + workplace → 2–4 responsibilities → who you worked with → skills → what you learned/enjoyed. Use Past Simple for finished work, used to for former routines and Present Perfect for life experience.
CLEAR A2+
I worked as a childcare assistant in hospitals, nurseries and private homes for many years. Choose your real responsibilities above to personalise the rest of this answer.
STRONG B1
I worked as a childcare assistant in hospitals, nurseries and private homes for many years. The work required patience, attention, communication and the ability to adapt to different children and situations.
STRETCH
My professional background is in childcare. I worked in several environments, including hospitals, nurseries and private homes, which taught me to adapt my communication and stay calm when needs changed.
