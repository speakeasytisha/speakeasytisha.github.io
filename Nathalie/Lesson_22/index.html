01 · GRAMMAR MASTERCLASS

Past Simple + Past Continuous

First understand the meaning. Then choose the form.

Le Past Continuous décrit une action en cours à un moment du passé. Le Past Simple décrit une action terminée, souvent plus courte, qui fait avancer l'histoire.
BACKGROUND

Past Continuous

was / were + verb-ing

Use it for an action already in progress.

I was walking near the harbour.

Action en cours / décor.

EVENT

Past Simple

past form / -ed

Use it for a completed event.

My phone rang.

Événement terminé.

INTERRUPTION

when

Long action + when + shorter event.

I was walking when my phone rang.

Quand un événement arrive pendant une action.

SIMULTANEOUS

while / as

Two actions can happen at the same time.

While we were eating, the children were playing.

Deux actions qui se déroulent en même temps.

Useful clueswhenwhileassuddenlyat that momentall of a suddenat 8 p.m.

TRUE CONJUGATION PRACTICE

Build the Past Continuous form

Formula: subject + was / were + verb-ing. I/he/she/it → was · you/we/they → were.

QUICK REVIEW BEFORE PRACTICE

Choose the tense because of the meaning

BackgroundWhat was already happening?
Main eventWhat happened next?
whenoften introduces the shorter event
while / asoften introduce the ongoing action

05 · READING

A near-miss on the coast

Read for the situation first. Then look at the grammar.

Première lecture : comprendre l'histoire. Deuxième lecture : repérer les actions longues et les événements courts.

TRAVEL DIARY · ENTRY 27

A sudden problem near the sea

Last Saturday, I was driving along a quiet coastal road with my family. The sun was shining and we were talking about where to stop for lunch. As we were passing a small village, we suddenly heard a strange noise from the back of the car.

I slowed down and stopped near a safe area. While I was looking at the rear wheel, another driver stopped and asked if we needed help. We discovered that one tyre was losing air. Fortunately, the other driver knew a garage nearby and showed us the way on his phone.

We were waiting for the repair when it started to rain heavily. We finally left about an hour later. The delay changed our plans, but the experience reminded us that unexpected moments can become part of a good travel story.

Reading strategy: What was happening? → What changed? → What did they do? → How did it end?
