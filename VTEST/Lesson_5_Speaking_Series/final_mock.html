01 · FINAL MOCK BRIEFING

One complete Speaking route. No safety net.

This final mock follows the published Business English order: 9 Rejoinders, one Read Aloud + two spoken questions, then four Discuss Topics prompts. Coach support is removed in Exam-feel Mode.

Scoring rule: Part 1 screening is displayed separately. The training Final Speaking score comes only from trainer rubrics for Parts 2 and 3, mirroring the fact that official Part 1 does not enter the final Speaking score.
