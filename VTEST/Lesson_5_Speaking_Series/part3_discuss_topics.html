01 · PART 3 BRIEFING

Four prompts. Prepare quickly. Keep speaking.

VTest describes Part 3 as four work-and-business prompts. They can ask you to describe your job, explain how to do something, or discuss common workplace situations. The official product page gives examples such as remote work, leadership and first jobs.

Official behaviour: you receive preparation time for each prompt and then 60 seconds to speak. Try to answer every point and use the full speaking time.

Conseil : ne cherchez pas une réponse parfaite. Pendant la préparation, notez seulement 3–4 idées : réponse directe, deux détails, un exemple, conclusion.

05 · PROMPT DECODER

First identify what kind of speaking job the prompt gives you.

DESCRIBE · EXPLAIN · DISCUSS · RECOMMEND
DESCRIBE

Who/what? What happens? What is it like? Give concrete details.

EXPLAIN HOW

Sequence the steps: first → then → after that → finally, with reasons where useful.

DISCUSS

Give an opinion, two supporting points, example, limitation or contrast, conclusion.

RECOMMEND / SOLVE

State the problem, propose action, explain why, mention risk, finish with next step.
