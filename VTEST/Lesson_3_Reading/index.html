01 · READING BRIEFING · 7 MIN

You do not need every word. You need the answer and its proof.

VTest Reading rewards controlled reading: understand the question, skim for the situation, scan for the evidence, eliminate distractors, and move on.

Reading rule: never choose an answer only because it repeats a word from the text. Choose the option that matches the meaning.

En clair : lisez d’abord la question, puis survolez le texte pour comprendre la situation. Ensuite, cherchez la preuve précise. Une réponse qui reprend les mêmes mots n’est pas forcément correcte : il faut vérifier le sens.

02 · THE EVIDENCE METHOD · 10 MIN

Question first. Then skim. Then scan.

PRACTICE01:30
1 · QUESTIONWhat exactly are you looking for?
2 · DIFFERENCEWhat changes between A, B and C?
3 · SKIMWho? Why? What kind of text?
4 · SCANFind the proof line.
5 · VERIFYMeaning matches? Choose + move.
INTERNAL NOTICE

Visitor parking

From Monday, visitor parking spaces beside Reception will be unavailable while resurfacing work is completed. Guests should use the east car park and enter through Gate B. Employees may continue using their normal spaces.

QUESTION-FIRST PRACTICE90 SEC

What should visitors do from Monday?

A · Park beside Reception
B · Use the east car park and Gate B
C · Use employee parking
Proof: “Guests should use the east car park and enter through Gate B.” Notice how visitors becomes guests: the answer may use a synonym.

Understand the situation in one sentence.

GUIDED SET 1

Before you hunt for details, identify the document type, writer, reader and purpose.

04 · DETAILS & PROOF · 12 MIN

Dates, conditions and exceptions change the answer.

GUIDED SET 2
1

Same-word trap

An option copies a text word but changes the meaning.

“free delivery” ≠ “delivery is always free”
2

Exception trap

A rule is true except for one group or situation.

“all staff except contractors…”
3

Time trap

Old, current and future arrangements appear together.

“currently… from 1 May…”
05 · REFERENCE & VOCABULARY IN CONTEXT · 10 MIN

Use the sentence around the unknown word.

GUIDED SET 3

You may need to understand what it, they, this change, the latter refer to, or infer a word from context without translating it.

06 · INFERENCE, TONE & PURPOSE · 10 MIN

Higher-level questions hide the answer between the lines.

GUIDED SET 4

At B2/C1, ask: What is the writer implying? Why was this message written? What is the likely next action?

07 · ADAPTIVE CHALLENGE · 10 MIN

Longer text. Same evidence method.

A2 → B1 → B2/C1
DO NOT SPEED UP

Harder reading means denser evidence.

When the text becomes more complex, keep the sequence: question → text map → proof → elimination. Do not reread the whole passage for every question.
