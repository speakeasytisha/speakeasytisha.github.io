Do not guess the order. Build from grammar.

The pre-writing stage contains nine scrambled sentences. Your job is to recognize the sentence skeleton, place auxiliaries and verbs correctly, then add complements and time phrases.

Today’s writing task: The manager moved the meeting to Friday. Express the message accurately and explain the grammar.
01 · Sentence backbone and word order Teach + try

Form: Subject + verb + object / complement

Affirmative: We sent the report yesterday.

Negative: We did not send the report.

Question: Did we send the report?

FR · Meaning: En anglais, le sujet est normalement exprimé et l’ordre sujet–verbe est essentiel.

Rules, exceptions & common errors: With be: She is ready / She is not ready / Is she ready? Do not use do with be. Adverbs of frequency usually precede the main verb but follow be.

Now practise: Choose the correct statement.

02 · Past simple Teach + try

Form: Subject + past verb (regular -ed / irregular)

Affirmative: We completed the audit last week.

Negative: We did not complete the audit.

Question: Did we complete the audit?

FR · Meaning: Événement terminé à un moment précis du passé : yesterday, last week, in 2024.

Rules, exceptions & common errors: After did / didn’t the main verb returns to its base form. Irregular forms include went, sent, wrote and made. Past of be: was/were, not did be.

Now practise: Which question is correct?

03 · Present perfect Teach + try

Form: Subject + have/has + past participle

Affirmative: The team has finished the report.

Negative: The team has not finished it yet.

Question: Has the team finished it?

FR · Meaning: Résultat ou expérience en lien avec maintenant. Différent du passé simple daté.

Rules, exceptions & common errors: Common markers: already, yet, just, ever, never, for, since. Do not normally combine with a finished time such as yesterday. Irregular participles: written, gone, done. With a finished date, use past simple.

Now practise: Choose the correct sentence.

04 · Passive voice — taught from the beginning Teach + try

Form: Receiver + be (correct tense) + past participle (+ by agent)

Affirmative: The meeting was moved to Friday.

Negative: The meeting was not moved.

Question: Was the meeting moved?

FR · Meaning: Voix passive : on met l’action ou son résultat au premier plan, plutôt que la personne qui agit. Active: The manager moved the meeting. Passive: The meeting was moved (by the manager).

Rules, exceptions & common errors: Match tense: is moved / was moved / has been moved / will be moved. The participle does not change with the subject; be does. Irregular participles: sent, written, made, done. Use by only when the agent matters. Not every verb is naturally passive: happen, arrive, sleep are intransitive. Avoid “was happened”.

Now practise: Choose the correctly formed passive.

05 · Questions and auxiliaries Teach + try

Form: Do/Does/Did + subject + base verb?; Be/Have/Modal + subject ...?

Affirmative: Did the client approve the plan?

Negative: The client did not approve the plan.

Question: Did the client approve the plan?

FR · Meaning: Les questions anglaises demandent souvent un auxiliaire avant le sujet.

Rules, exceptions & common errors: No extra do with be or modals: Is she available? Can we help? Have they finished? Wh-questions: Why did the team postpone the meeting? Subject question exception: Who approved the plan? (no did).

Now practise: Choose the correct question.

06 · Modals and recommendations Teach + try

Form: Subject + should/could/must/might + base verb

Affirmative: We should inform the team.

Negative: We should not delay the decision.

Question: Should we inform the team?

FR · Meaning: Should = conseil; could = possibilité; must = obligation; might = possibilité incertaine.

Rules, exceptions & common errors: Never add to after should/could/must: should send, not should to send. No -s in third person: she should send. Stronger recommendations include I would recommend + -ing / that + clause. Must not = prohibition, not absence of obligation.

Now practise: Choose the correct recommendation.

07 · Reason, contrast and result Teach + try

Form: because + clause; although + clause; however, + sentence; therefore, + result

Affirmative: We delayed the launch because testing was incomplete.

Negative: The launch was not delayed because of cost.

Question: Why was the launch delayed?

FR · Meaning: Because explique pourquoi; although/however opposent; therefore/as a result indiquent une conséquence.

Rules, exceptions & common errors: Because + subject/verb; because of + noun/gerund. Although cannot normally be paired with but in the same clause. However and therefore need punctuation when linking separate sentences. Avoid comma splices.

Now practise: Choose the correct connector.

08 · Examples, precision and paragraph flow Teach + try

Form: Point → reason → specific example → consequence → action

Affirmative: Training helps because people understand their tasks. For example, a mentor can explain the system. As a result, errors may decrease.

Negative: An example alone is not an argument.

Question: How could this help new employees?

FR · Meaning: Le lecteur doit voir une idée, une explication, un exemple concret et la conséquence.

Rules, exceptions & common errors: For example / for instance introduce a real illustration. Such as is usually followed by a noun phrase. Avoid claims without support, repetitions and overly long sentences. A strong paragraph remains relevant to the prompt.

Now practise: Which addition best develops the idea “Mentors help”?

READ THE TASK FIRST · SAME QUESTION FOR ALL FOUR LEVELS

Writing subject / Sujet de rédaction

The manager moved the meeting to Friday. Express the message accurately and explain the grammar.

  1. Identify the situation or problem.
  2. Give practical solutions with at least one example.
  3. Explain the benefits or expected results.

A2 model · Sentence accuracy

The meeting is on Friday.

Why this works / how to progress: Simple active statement; clear time phrase.

B1 model · Sentence accuracy

The manager moved the meeting to Friday.

Why this works / how to progress: Correct past simple with clear agent and object.

B2 model · Sentence accuracy

The meeting was moved to Friday because the manager was unavailable.

Why this works / how to progress: Correct past passive plus a reason; the event matters more than the agent.

C1 model · Sentence accuracy

Because several participants were unavailable, the meeting was rescheduled for Friday; everyone has since been informed.

Why this works / how to progress: Precise passive, complex causal clause and controlled present perfect.

02 · SENTENCE MAP · 12 MIN

Recognize the structure before touching the words.

Statement

Subject + verb + object/complement

We sent the revised proposal yesterday.

Present perfect

Subject + have/has + past participle

The team has completed the first phase.

Passive

Subject + be + past participle

The meeting was moved to Friday.

Question

Auxiliary + subject + base verb...?

Did the client approve the budget?

Modal

Subject + modal + base verb

We should confirm the booking today.

Linking clause

Main clause + connector + clause

We postponed the launch because testing was incomplete.

Watch-outs: after did, use the base verb; after a modal, use the base verb; present perfect needs a past participle; passive forms need be + past participle.
03 · GUIDED LAB · 20 MIN

Build nine sentences with automatic immediate correction.

AUTO-CORRECTION · HINTS · WHY · 2-LEVEL MODELS
4-STEP METHOD

1. Find the subject.
2. Find the verb group.
3. Add the object/complement.
4. Place time/reason phrases.

For questions, move the auxiliary before the subject. For passive sentences, keep the complete verb group together.

Coach Mode: use HINT before MODEL. Ask the learner to explain the grammar rule aloud before checking.
