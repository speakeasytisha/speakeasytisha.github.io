02 · THE 30-SECOND PREVIEW · 8 MIN

Read first. Listen second.

PREVIEW00:30
THE 5-STEP PREVIEW
  1. Read the question first. What exactly must you find?
  2. Underline the difference. Time? reason? person? action?
  3. Predict vocabulary. Think of synonyms before the audio starts.
  4. Expect a correction. The first number or idea may not be the final answer.
  5. Do not translate. Stay focused on evidence.
LISTENING · PREVIEW PRACTICEQUESTION 1

What time will the client presentation begin?

Before listening: the difference between the answers is only the time. Listen especially for a correction such as instead, actually, moved to, not… but…

03 · GIST & PURPOSE · 10 MIN

Catch the situation before the details.

GUIDED SET 1

First listen for who, where, why. Do not chase every unknown word.

04 · DETAILS & CHANGES · 12 MIN

The first detail is often a trap.

GUIDED SET 2
1

Correction trap

A speaker gives one detail, then changes it.

“At two — sorry, make that two-thirty.”
2

Distractor trap

You hear a word from an option, but it does not answer the question.

The meeting is in Paris, but the question asks where the client is based.
3

Synonym trap

The answer uses different words from the recording.

“postpone” → “move to a later date”
05 · INTENTION & INFERENCE · 10 MIN

Listen for what the speaker means, not only what they say.

GUIDED SET 3

At higher levels, the answer may depend on tone, implication, purpose, or what is most likely to happen next.

07 · ADAPTIVE CHALLENGE · 10 MIN

If it gets harder, keep the same method.

A2 → B1 → B2/C1
YOUR JOB DOES NOT CHANGE

Difficulty changes. Method stays stable.

As the language becomes faster or more indirect, return to the question. Identify the evidence you need and ignore information that does not help answer it.
