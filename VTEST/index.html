05 · READING TASTER · 8 MIN

Do not read every word first.

PASSAGE03:00
INTERNAL EMAIL

Updated visitor procedure

From: Facilities Team
Subject: Visitor access from 1 October

From 1 October, all external visitors must be registered in the reception system by 4 p.m. on the previous working day. Employees should enter the visitor’s full name, company and expected arrival time. Visitors who have not been registered may experience delays while reception contacts the host.

The procedure for delivery drivers has not changed. Drivers should continue to use the service entrance and show their delivery reference to security. If you are expecting a large group of more than eight visitors, please contact Facilities at least two working days in advance so that additional badges can be prepared.

Why should visitors be registered before the day of arrival?

Scan for the consequence of “not been registered.”

Which situation requires two working days’ notice?

Reading formula: Questions → keywords → skim for meaning → scan for proof → eliminate distractors.

PART 2 · WORKPLACE WRITING1 min prepare · 20 min write
PRACTICE PROMPT

Your team is considering introducing two remote-working days per week.

Write a short response for your manager. Include:

  • one advantage for employees
  • one possible difficulty for the team
  • one practical recommendation
0 wordsB1/B2 practice target: 100+ words
4-block plan: opinion → advantage → difficulty → recommendation + reason.
B1 model: I think two remote-working days could be a good option for our team. First, employees could save commuting time and have more flexibility. However, communication may be more difficult when several people are working from home on different days. I recommend choosing the same two remote days for most of the team and keeping at least one full team day in the office. We should also use a shared calendar and short online meetings to keep everyone informed. This system could improve flexibility without making teamwork more complicated.
B2 model: Introducing two remote-working days per week could improve both flexibility and concentration, particularly for tasks that require uninterrupted focus. The main risk is that information may become fragmented if team members are rarely present at the same time. I would therefore recommend establishing common remote-working days, while keeping one fixed in-office collaboration day. In addition, clear response-time expectations and a shared project dashboard would help prevent communication gaps. A short trial period of six weeks would allow the company to evaluate productivity and employee feedback before making the arrangement permanent.

PRE-SPEAKING · REJOINDER

Choose the best response.

“I’m afraid the supplier won’t be able to deliver before Friday.”

READ ALOUD + DISCUSS

Read naturally, then answer.

Our customer support team is introducing a new ticket system next month. The aim is to make requests easier to track and reduce response times. All staff will receive a short training session before the system goes live.

Q1. What is the main purpose of the new system?

Q2. In your opinion, what makes a new workplace system easy to adopt?

B1 model: The main purpose is to track customer requests more easily and answer faster. In my opinion, a new system is easier to use when the training is clear and people can practise before using it with customers. It also helps to have simple instructions and someone to contact if there is a problem.
B2 model: The new system is intended to improve visibility over customer requests and shorten response times. I think adoption is much smoother when employees understand why the change is being introduced, not just how the tool works. Short practical training, realistic examples and accessible support during the first few weeks can make a significant difference.
3
DISCUSS TOPICS

Build a 60-second answer, not a one-sentence answer.

Describe a typical busy day in your work or in a workplace you know. What makes it busy, and how do people organize priorities?

THE RULE TO REMEMBER

Keep moving. Answer everything. Use the time you are given.

Difficulty changing is normal in an adaptive test. Your job is not to feel perfect; your job is to give the clearest English you can at each stage.
