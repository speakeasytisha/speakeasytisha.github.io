03 · Grammar from your writing

Three upgrades that will immediately improve your answers

These rules target the patterns that appear when your ideas are strong but the sentence is still organised like French.

01

Articles: a / the / no article

a / an = one non-specific item introduced for the first time.

a supplier · a sample · an urgent meeting

the = a specific item both people can identify.

the supplier meeting · the collection review · the Industrial Director

no article with general fields and many uncountable ideas.

footwear · luxury · English · production · information

✓ I organised a visit to the headquarters.✓ I have worked in the luxury industry for more than ten years.

a/an = un élément non spécifique ; the = élément précis/connu ; pas d’article pour certaines notions générales comme footwear, English, production, information.

02

Professional prepositions

Learn the whole phrase, not the preposition alone.

work in the luxury industry work for Cartier coordinate with suppliers be responsible for reporting prepare for a review travel to Italy by Monday / on Monday / at 10:00
✗ organise a visit headquarters✓ organise a visit to headquarters / to the headquarters

Apprenez l’expression complète : work in, work for, coordinate with, responsible for, prepare for, travel to.

03

Build longer answers safely

Use a simple four-part ladder instead of adding “and” repeatedly.

Point → Example → Result → Relevance

Point: I learn new environments quickly. Example: At Chloé, I worked between the studio and operational teams. Result: I became comfortable coordinating different priorities. Relevance: That would help me adapt quickly to the footwear vocabulary and workflow.

Useful connectors: because · so that · while · which means that · as a result · therefore

Pour développer sans perdre le contrôle : idée → exemple → résultat → lien avec le poste.

05 · Shoe-sector situations

Questions that test judgement more than technical knowledge

The details are footwear-specific; the skill is still executive coordination: clarify → identify impact → propose a route → communicate → follow up.

Live scenario

A development sample from Italy is delayed, and the Development Director has a collection review tomorrow morning.

What would you do?

ITALY→DELAY→REVIEW
Structure: confirm facts → understand what the review needs → contact the right owner → prepare an alternative → update the director early → document next steps.

“I would first confirm the exact status of the sample and what the Development Director needs for tomorrow’s review. I would contact the relevant development or supplier contact to understand whether the sample can still arrive, whether photos or technical information can be shared in the meantime, and what the realistic timing is. I would then brief the director early with the facts and at least one alternative, so the director can decide whether to adapt the review. My role would be to keep the information clear and avoid a last-minute surprise.”
