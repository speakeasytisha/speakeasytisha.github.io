COMPLETE GRAMMAR RULE

Present perfect + past simple

Use the present perfect to introduce experience. Change to the past simple for the finished, dated example.

Present perfect pour introduire l’expérience ; past simple pour raconter l’exemple terminé ou daté.

PRESENT PERFECT

Experience connected to now

have / has + past participle

I have handled many contracts.

I have worked with demanding customers.

Have you ever solved a difficult problem?

everneveralreadyrecentlyforsince

A completed professional event

verb + -ed / irregular form

FASTER LISTENING

Listen for the follow-up, not every word

Listen once for the situation, twice for key facts, then reformulate. Reveal the transcript after attempt two.

RECRUITER · QUESTION 1

Accuracy under pressure

You mentioned that you are attentive to detail. Could you give me a specific example of a time when you identified an error before it caused a problem? What did you do, and what was the result?
A2.2+

I once found missing information in a contract. I contacted the customer and corrected the document. The contract was completed on time.

B1.1

Last month, while checking a company contract, I noticed an important document was missing. I contacted the customer immediately, explained what was required and updated the file. As a result, the contract was processed on time.

RECRUITER · QUESTION 2

Adapting to change

Agricultural work can change quickly because of weather, deliveries or staffing. Tell me about a situation when your priorities changed unexpectedly. How did you reorganise your work?
A2.2+

One day, I received an urgent customer request. I changed my schedule and completed the urgent file first. I finished my other tasks later that day.

B1.1

I have often had to adapt when an urgent request changed my priorities. Recently, a customer needed an amendment before a deadline. I reorganised my task list, informed my colleagues and dealt with it first. The amendment was completed on time.

Hello Ms Morgan,

Thank you for your message. I currently work for an insurance company, where I check contracts for business customers. Last month, I found some missing information in a contract. I contacted the customer, explained which document was needed and updated the file. The contract was completed before the deadline. This experience shows that I am organised and attentive to detail. This experience has strengthened my ability to identify discrepancies, communicate clearly and follow procedures. I am confident that these transferable skills would help me update records and support your harvest team effectively.

FINAL LILATE MISSION

Your experience, your evidence, your future

Speak for 90 seconds to two minutes. Then answer two follow-up questions.

MAIN QUESTION

Tell me about a professional problem you have handled successfully. What happened, what did you do, and how could this experience help you in Australia?

  1. Introduce your accumulated experience.
  2. Set one precise past situation.
  3. Explain your action and result.
  4. Connect the skills to Australia.
A2.2+ · 60 seconds

I have handled many contracts in my current job. Last month, I found missing information in a customer’s file. I contacted the customer and explained which document was necessary. I updated the contract and checked it again. The file was completed before the deadline. This shows that I am organised and attentive to detail. These skills could help me work accurately in Australia.

B1.1 · 90 seconds

In my current position, I have gained considerable experience handling detailed contracts and communicating with business customers. Last month, I noticed that an essential document was missing from an urgent file. I contacted the customer, explained what was required and coordinated with the relevant department. I then updated and checked the contract. As a result, the file was processed accurately and on time. This demonstrated my ability to analyse information, solve problems and remain calm under pressure. Although agriculture would be new to me, these transferable skills would help me follow procedures and support an Australian team.
