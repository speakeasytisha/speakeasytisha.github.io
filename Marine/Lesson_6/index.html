RESET THE MEANING OF YESTERDAY

A mock gives information. It does not give a verdict.

You are not here to prove anything today. You are here to notice what helps you communicate when pressure appears.

Un examen blanc donne des informations. Ce n'est pas un verdict. Aujourd'hui, l'objectif est de retrouver les stratégies qui vous aident à communiquer sous pression.

PRESSURE THOUGHT

“If one question is difficult, the exam is going badly.”

Better thought: One question is one question. Your job is to answer what you can, then start the next task fresh.
PRESSURE THOUGHT

“I need a complicated answer to show my level.”

Better thought: A clear, relevant answer is stronger than a complicated answer that becomes confused.
PRESSURE THOUGHT

“If I miss a word, I cannot answer.”

Better thought: Find the situation, the important information and the action you need to take. You do not need every word.
02

YOUR EVIDENCE

Start from what is already working.

Your earlier assessment showed that understanding the essential information is already one of your strengths. Today, you will use that strength deliberately.

Votre évaluation initiale a montré que repérer les informations essentielles est déjà un point fort. Aujourd'hui, vous allez utiliser ce point fort comme stratégie.

Choose one strength above.
Your priority is not “learn everything.”It is: retrieve the English you already know, keep answers structured, and add useful vocabulary.
03

THE EXAM AS FOUR SMALL JOBS

Do not carry the whole exam in your head.

The LILATE IA evaluates four professional communication actions. Click each one to see your simple job.

Le LILATE IA évalue quatre actions de communication professionnelle. Cliquez sur chaque partie pour voir votre mission simple.

Current LILATE IA format: four parts of roughly seven minutes, with reading, listening, writing and speaking tasks. Individual questions are timed on screen. The whole test generally lasts about 30–45 minutes and is adaptive. Le test actuel comporte quatre parties d'environ sept minutes, avec des questions chronométrées individuellement.

05

RESCUE LANGUAGE

Words and phrases that keep you moving.

You do not need to memorise long speeches. Build a small set of phrases that help you think, clarify, reformulate, organise and solve.

Vous n'avez pas besoin de mémoriser de longs discours. Apprenez plutôt quelques phrases qui vous permettent de réfléchir, clarifier, reformuler et structurer.

06

LISTEN FOR THE JOB, NOT EVERY WORD

Keyword listening lab

Your method: situation → key facts → required action. Listen once for meaning. Listen again only if you need specific detail.

Méthode : situation → informations clés → action demandée. Première écoute pour le sens global, deuxième écoute pour les détails utiles.

07

REFORMULATE WITHOUT PANICKING

Keep the meaning. Simplify the language.

You are not translating word for word. You are transmitting the useful information clearly.

Vous ne traduisez pas mot à mot. Vous transmettez l'information utile de façon claire.

08

MICRO-MISSIONS

Short answers can be strong answers.

Complete these without a timer first. Aim for 3 useful points, not perfection. Record yourself if you want to hear the difference between “thinking” and “communicating.”

Faites d'abord ces missions sans chronomètre. Visez trois informations utiles, pas la perfection.
