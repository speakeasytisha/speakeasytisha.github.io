05

A / AN / THE

Small words, big difference

De petits mots, une grande différence.

a

a small island

Before a singular, countable noun mentioned for the first time.

Devant un nom singulier comptable, mentionné pour la première fois.

an

an amazing memory

Use an before a vowel sound.

Utilisez an devant un son voyelle.

the

the island we visited

Use the when the listener already knows which one you mean.

Utilisez the quand l'interlocuteur sait déjà de quoi vous parlez.

—

birds, monkeys

No article for plural nouns used in a general sense.

Pas d'article devant un nom pluriel employé au sens général.

a lot of

a lot of birds

Plural noun after "a lot of" — do not forget the final -s.

Nom pluriel après "a lot of" — n'oubliez pas le -s final.

with

with my boyfriend

Possessive + person, no article needed.

Possessif + personne, pas d'article nécessaire.

About plurals

bird → birds, monkey → monkeys. Almost every English noun needs an -s in the plural — even when the French equivalent does not change.

bird → birds, monkey → monkeys. Presque tous les noms anglais prennent un -s au pluriel — même quand le français ne change pas.

YOUR BEST MEMORY

TRAVEL STORY BUILDER

Tell the story of your island trip

A2

Clear and simple

Last year, I went to a small island in Southeast Asia with my boyfriend. We rented a motorbike and went scuba diving. We saw a lot of birds and monkeys. We felt free. It was my best trip!

A2+

Connected and personal

Last year, I travelled to a small island in Southeast Asia with my boyfriend. First, we rented a motorbike to explore the island, and then we went scuba diving near the reef. We saw a lot of birds and monkeys, which made the landscape feel truly wild. Because we had so much freedom, it became one of my favourite memories.

B1

Detailed and natural

One of my most memorable trips was to a small island in Southeast Asia with my boyfriend. During our stay, we rented a motorbike to discover the island at our own pace, and we also went scuba diving, which was an incredible experience. Although the roads were sometimes difficult, we saw a lot of birds and monkeys along the way, which made every moment feel exceptional. We felt completely free, and it is still, without a doubt, the best trip of my life.
