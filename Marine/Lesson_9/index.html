Your busy period — in clear English

You described a demanding annual period involving company clients, employee information, contributions, accountants, deadlines and corrections. First, make the story easier to follow.

1Receive informationEmployee data comes from the company or its accountant.
2Check the dataYou need accurate information about employees and the contract.
3RecalculateThe annual health/protection contributions are updated.
4Apply a fallbackIf information is missing, the previous year’s figures may be used in the process you described.
5Correct discrepanciesIf incorrect data creates a problem, you follow the required correction procedure.
6Manage reactionsYou explain the situation to the client and coordinate with the accountant.

Natural sentence to describe the period

VOCABULARY HUB

The words you needed yesterday

“Cotisation prévoyance” — what should you say?

insurance contribution is very useful when talking about an employer/employee group plan. insurance premium is the broader insurance term for the amount paid for cover. In your work story, you can often say contribution when explaining the group scheme and premium when speaking more generally about insurance pricing.

« Cotisation » peut se traduire par contribution dans un régime collectif, et par premium dans un contexte d’assurance plus général.

Which verb sounds most natural?

redo ❌For calculations, prefer recalculate, update or review.
fill out ✅Use for a form: “The accountant fills out the form.”
send us ✅Natural. More formal: “provide us with / submit the information.”
play the game ⚠Idiomatic but informal. At work: “follow the process”, “cooperate”, “provide the required information”.

REAL-WORLD ERROR CASE

When incorrect data creates a huge discrepancy

CASE STUDY · ANONYMISED

A small hair salon receives an amount of approximately €300,000 because incorrect figures were entered. The client is understandably shocked. You need to identify the source of the error, contact the accountant, follow the correction procedure and keep the client informed.

ERROR LANGUAGE · 5 POINTS

Say it professionally

LISTENING · CLIENT CALL · 4 POINTS

What exactly is the problem?

Listen once for the situation, then again for the details. Audio never starts automatically.

Hello, I’m calling because we have just received the annual contribution notice and the amount is completely different from what we expected. It says nearly three hundred thousand euros, which cannot be correct for a salon of our size. Could you please check the employee information used for the calculation and let us know what needs to be corrected? We need to understand what happened before the payment deadline.

Listen for 4 things: why the client is calling · the amount · what they want checked · the deadline concern.

LILATE TRANSFER

Turn your real work into a strong speaking answer

90-SECOND PROFESSIONAL RESPONSE

“Could you explain a particularly busy period at work and how you handle problems when information is incorrect?”

  1. Introduce your role and clients.
  2. Explain why this period is busy.
  3. Describe the normal process.
  4. Explain what can go wrong.
  5. Give the anonymised hair-salon example.
  6. Finish with what you do to solve the problem.
Speaking timer01:30
I work at Swiss Life with company clients. We work with health and protection insurance. This is a very busy period because we have to update the annual contributions. We receive information about the employees and check it. If the information is not correct, the amount can be wrong. For example, one small company received a very high amount because the figures were incorrect. In this situation, I check the information, contact the accountant and explain the next steps to the client.
I work at Swiss Life with corporate clients, mainly on group health and protection insurance. At the moment, we are in one of our busiest periods because annual contributions have to be recalculated using updated employee information. Normally, we receive the data, check the figures and update the contribution. If the required information is missing, the calculation may be based on the previous year’s figures. Problems can arise when incorrect data is submitted. For example, a small hair salon once received an amount of nearly €300,000 because the figures entered were wrong. In that situation, my role is to identify the discrepancy, contact the accountant, follow the correction procedure and keep the client informed until the issue is resolved.
