GRAMMAR REPAIR LAB

Small changes, stronger English

Focus on the grammar that will make your English immediately clearer.

Cibler la grammaire qui rendra votre anglais immédiatement plus clair.

he / she / itverb + sMy father likes cooking.

Use: routines, facts and regular situations.

Negative: he doesn’t work · they don’t work

Question: Does she work? · Do they work?

Attention : après does/doesn't, le verbe revient à la base : Does she work?

finished pastverb + ed / irregularWe rented a motorbike.

Use: a finished action at a finished time.

Negative: We didn’t rent a car.

Question: Did you enjoy the trip?

Attention : après did/didn't, utilisez la base verbale : Did you go?

for + durationa/an + singular nounfor three weeks · a small island

for: for two days / for three weeks

to + verb: to discover, to work, to meet

plural: many birds, two monkeys, nice memories

Astuce : après a/an, le nom est singulier. Après many/a lot of, vérifiez le pluriel.

Thanks for your message. The room is still available from 3 September. The flat is in West End, about fifteen minutes from the city centre by bus. You would share the kitchen and living room with me and another flatmate, Josh.

We both work during the week, so the flat is usually quiet in the evenings. At weekends, we sometimes cook together or go to the market. We are looking for someone friendly, tidy and independent. The rent includes water, but electricity and internet are extra.

Hi, I’m Marine. I’m from France, and I currently live near the Atlantic coast. I’m preparing to move to Australia because I’m looking for a new personal and professional experience. I’m quite open-minded, and I usually adapt quickly to new situations, although I know the first few weeks may be challenging. One of my favourite travel memories is a three-week trip I took with my boyfriend. We rented a motorbike to explore a small island, went scuba diving and saw a lot of wildlife. What I loved most was the feeling of freedom. When I arrive in Australia, I would like to settle in, find work and build a new social circle. I’m especially excited about the outdoor lifestyle and the opportunity to use English every day.
