What is a Working Holiday Visa?

Learn the key facts and the English you need to describe your project clearly.

Apprendre les informations essentielles et le vocabulaire pour expliquer clairement le projet.

PVT in English

Current information

PVT means Programme Vacances-Travail. In Australia, you should say Working Holiday visa or Working Holiday programme.

Pour l’Australie, dites Working Holiday visa, et non “work vacation permit”.

VisaSubclass 417for eligible French passport holders
First stayUp to 12 monthstravel with short-term work
Second visa3 monthsof eligible specified work
Third visa6 monthsof eligible specified work
Important: the “88 days”

They are commonly used to describe the three-month specified-work requirement for a second visa. They are not the duration of the first visa, and the work must meet official sector, location and evidence rules.

Les « 88 jours » correspondent couramment aux trois mois de travail éligible pour demander une deuxième année. Il faut vérifier le secteur, la zone et les justificatifs.

Immigration rules can change before 2027. Verify all conditions on the Australian Department of Home Affairs website before applying.

In 2027, I am planning to go to Australia with a Working Holiday visa. I would like to travel, improve my English and find a job. I may work in agriculture or in the mining industry. I would also like to complete eligible work for a possible second visa. My goal is to gain experience and become more independent.

I am planning to move to Australia in 2027 on a Working Holiday visa. This visa will allow me to travel and work in the country for up to one year. I am considering working in agriculture or the mining sector, especially if the position qualifies as specified work for a second-year visa. My goals are to improve my English, gain international experience and build a new life abroad.

Build the sentence before you speak

Your priority structure: subject + time adverb + verb + useful detail + place + time + reason.

Structure prioritaire : sujet + verbe + complément utile + lieu/temps + raison.

1

Where does currently go?

Put currently before the main verb: I currently work at a bank.

With the verb be, put it after am/is/are: I am currently preparing my project.

2

Where does the “why” go?

Give the reason near the end with because + subject + verb: …because I want to gain international experience.

Use so for a result: I want to work abroad, so I am improving my English.

Complete pattern: I + currently + handle + insurance contracts + at a bank + every day + because accuracy is important.

Currently se place avant le verbe principal, mais après be. La raison se place généralement à la fin avec because.

The B1 sentence ladder

Say every line aloud
  1. I work.
  2. I work at a bank.
  3. I work at a bank in France.
  4. I work at a bank and I handle contracts.
  5. I currently work at a bank in France, where I handle banking and insurance contracts.
  6. I currently work at a bank in France, where I handle banking and insurance contracts, so I am used to checking details and following procedures carefully.

GRAMMAR BASICS

Present, past and future — kept separate

Review one system at a time, then use it in your real project.

Réviser un système à la fois, puis l’utiliser dans votre projet réel.

routine / factI work · she worksDo you work? · She doesn’t work.

Affirmative: I/you/we/they work · he/she/it works.

Question: Do you work? · Does she work?

Negative: I don’t work · She doesn’t work.

Après does ou doesn’t, le verbe reste à la base : Does she work?

finished actionverb + EDI visited · we travelled · she decided

Regular: work → worked · visit → visited · decide → decided.

Question: Did you visit Mexico?

Negative: I didn’t visit Mexico.

Après did ou didn’t, utilisez la base : Did you visit?

/t/worked · helped · packed
/d/travelled · planned · enjoyed
/ɪd/visited · decided · needed

Key: only verbs ending in a /t/ or /d/ sound add a new syllable: visit-ed, need-ed.

be going to

a decided plan

I’m going to move in 2027.
plan to

an organised intention

I plan to apply for a visa.
would like to

a wish

I’d like to work outdoors.
may / might

a possibility

I may work in mining.
hope to

a goal

I hope to improve my English.
if + present, will

a real future condition

If it works out, I’ll stay longer.

A

FARM SUPERVISOR

Tomorrow’s first shift

Listen twice. Write only key information during the first listening.

We start at six tomorrow morning. Please meet outside the packing shed at five forty-five. Wear closed shoes, bring water and speak to Emma if you need gloves.
The supervisor said that we have to meet outside the packing shed at 5:45 tomorrow morning. We need to wear closed shoes and bring water. If we need gloves, we should speak to Emma.
B

MINING SITE

Safety induction

Listen for obligation words: must, have to, before, immediately.

Before entering the work area, you must put on your helmet, safety glasses and high-visibility vest. Check your equipment before every shift and report any damage immediately to the site supervisor.
Before we enter the work area, we must wear a helmet, safety glasses and a high-visibility vest. We also have to check our equipment before each shift and report any damage immediately.

Four professional communication missions

With examinerUp to 60 minutes · guided professional role plays
AI modalityApproximately 30 minutes · timed QCU/QCM and recorded/written open answers

PART 1Welcome & interact

Introduce yourself to an Australian recruiter

Situation: A recruiter asks about your current position, experience, availability and motivation.

Could you tell me about your current job? Why would you like to work in Australia? What transferable skills can you bring to this position?
I currently work in banking and insurance, where I handle contracts, check documents and communicate with customers. I am planning to move to Australia in 2027 because I would like to gain international experience and improve my English. I am reliable, organised and used to following detailed procedures, which would be useful in a new workplace.
PART 2Collect & transcribe

Turn a voicemail into structured notes

Mission: Use the recruiter voicemail from the listening lab and complete the form.

Caller: Olivia · Company: Red Earth Staffing · Interview: next Tuesday at 10:30 · Location: King Street, opposite the train station · Documents: photo ID and résumé · Action: call before Friday to confirm.
PART 3Collect & transmit

Explain safety instructions to a colleague

Mission: Give clear instructions using sequence words and obligation.

First, you must…Before you…, make sure…You also have to…If there is a problem…Finally,…
First, you must put on your helmet, safety glasses and high-visibility vest before entering the work area. Then, check that your equipment is in good condition. If you notice any damage, report it to the site supervisor immediately. These rules are important because they protect everyone on the site.
PART 4Analyse & decide

Compare two possible jobs

Job A · Packing shed
  • Day shifts
  • Regional Queensland
  • Training provided
  • Physical repetitive work
Job B · Mine-site admin support
  • Remote roster
  • Computer and document tasks
  • Safety induction required
  • Previous admin experience preferred
Job B may be more suitable for me because I already have experience handling contracts and administrative documents. I am comfortable checking information and following procedures. However, I would need to adapt to the remote roster and learn the site’s safety rules. Job A could also be useful for specified work, but it is less closely connected to my current skills.
