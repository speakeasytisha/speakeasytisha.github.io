01 · Flight plan

What this lesson is designed to do

Your self-presentation is already strong. Today, the objective is to make it easier to deliver naturally without notes, while keeping your tone precise, calm and credible under pressure.

FR: Votre présentation est déjà très solide. L’objectif est maintenant de pouvoir la retrouver naturellement sans notes, tout en gardant un ton précis, calme et crédible sous pression.

1

Recover the structure

Use cue words instead of memorising every sentence.

2

Control the delivery

Use pauses, tone, posture and energy intentionally.

3

Speak under pressure

Practise openings, attention recovery and senior-management responses.

Key principle for today

A strong delivery does not sound memorised. It sounds structured. Small differences from the model are acceptable if the logic remains clear.

03 · Step 1

Your corrected self-presentation

This version keeps your tone: technical, precise, structured and credible. It should sound like you, not like a generic speech.

Pause Slow down Energy up Calm Firm Emphasise Look up

Final corrected version

I am a Project Leader in the Validation and Verification team, which is composed of 24 engineers. Our role is to ensure that validation and verification activities are properly performed for landing systems on helicopter, aircraft and business jet projects. We need to ensure compliance with requirements while making sure that customer satisfaction and safety standards are fully achieved. As a Project Leader, communication is one of the most important aspects of my role. I need to communicate clearly and precisely in order to provide accurate information to the executive team and enable them to make the right decisions. My goal is to transform a complex technical situation into a message that is easy to understand, easy to trust and easy to act on.

Cued delivery version

Notes for delivery
  • Before speaking, take one breath and look at the listener or camera.
  • Say “Project Leader” and “Validation and Verification” clearly because these words define your authority.
  • Slow down on “landing systems” and “safety standards”. These are important anchors.
  • Make the communication part calmer; it shows control, not hesitation.
  • Pause before “easy to understand, easy to trust and easy to act on”. This is the sentence to remember.

04 · Step 2

From full text to cue words

The difficulty is not writing a strong version. The difficulty is finding it again when speaking without notes. Cue words create a mental runway.

Pause Slow down Energy up Calm Firm Emphasise Look up
1

Full text

Read the complete version once. Focus on meaning, not perfection.

2

Cue words

Keep only the words that help recover the structure.

3

Free delivery

Use different words if necessary, but keep the same logic.

1. Role

Project Leader · V&V team · 24 engineers

Model text: I am a Project Leader in the Validation and Verification team, which is composed of 24 engineers. Pause

Look up This sentence establishes professional authority. Do not rush the job title.

2. Responsibility

validation · verification · landing systems · aircraft projects

Model text: Slow down Our role is to ensure that validation and verification activities are properly performed for landing systems on helicopter, aircraft and business jet projects.

The list is technical. Keep the rhythm steady and avoid accelerating at the end.

3. Standards

compliance · requirements · customer satisfaction · safety standards

Model text: Calm We need to ensure compliance with requirements while making sure that customer satisfaction and safety standards are fully achieved. Pause

This is where calmness builds trust. The sentence should sound controlled, not dramatic.

4. Communication

clear · precise · accurate information · right decisions

Model text: As a Project Leader, communication is one of the most important aspects of my role. Pause Firm I need to communicate clearly and precisely in order to provide accurate information to the executive team and enable them to make the right decisions.

The first sentence is reflective. The second sentence is firmer because it explains your value.

5. Final goal

complex situation · easy to understand · easy to trust · easy to act on

Model text: Pause My goal is to transform a complex technical situation into a message that is easy to understand, easy to trust and easy to act on.

This is the sentence to remember. It should sound calm, slow and complete.

Important delivery reminder

A strong delivery does not sound memorised. It sounds structured. Small differences from the model are acceptable if the logic remains clear.

06 · Step 4

Tone, posture and energy

The same sentence can capture attention, build trust or guide a decision depending on how it is delivered.

Pause Slow down Energy up Calm Firm Emphasise Look up

Capture attention

Use when: opening, refocusing or signalling urgency.

How: slightly more energy, clear first word, direct eye contact.

Energy up Look up Let me start with the key point: the decision we need today concerns validation risk.

Build trust

Use when: explaining risk, safety, evidence or compliance.

How: slower pace, calmer tone, steady posture, one pause.

Calm Slow down The main risk is a compliance gap before the next milestone.

Recommend

Use when: asking for a decision or proposing a direction.

How: firm voice, calm energy, clear end of sentence.

Firm My recommendation is to secure the validation now before moving to the next milestone.

What to listen for

  • Attention the sentence sounds more alive, but not rushed.
  • Trust the sentence sounds calm and controlled.
  • Recommendation the sentence sounds firm, with a clear end.

Practice

Use the same sentence three times. Do not change the words. Change only the intention.

The main risk is a compliance gap before the next milestone.

07 · Step 5

High-impact openings

A strong opening tells the audience where to place attention. Start with the issue, risk, impact or decision, not with too much background.

Pause Slow down Energy up Calm Firm Emphasise Look up

Direct opening

Energy up Let me start with the main issue: we have a validation point to secure before the next milestone.

Risk-focused opening

Calm The key risk is that we move forward before the compliance evidence is complete.

Decision-focused opening

Firm The decision we need today is whether to secure additional validation before moving forward.

Your turn

Create one opening for a real or realistic project situation. Use one of these starts:

  • The key point today is…
  • The main risk is…
  • The decision we need today is…
  • Before going into the details, let me clarify the impact.

CLOE transfer

How this lesson prepares your oral exam

The goal is to sound clear and spontaneous while keeping professional structure.

CLOE-style question

Can you describe your role and explain why communication is important in your job?

I am a Project Leader in Validation and Verification. I coordinate technical activities for landing systems and make sure that requirements and safety standards are properly addressed. Communication is important because I need to transform complex technical information into clear messages that help the executive team make the right decisions.

Follow-up question

What is difficult when you communicate technical information?

The difficulty is to give enough information to be accurate, but not too much information. In my role, I need to select the key point, explain the risk clearly and help the listener understand what decision is needed.
