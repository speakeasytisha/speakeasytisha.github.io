11 · FINAL MISSION · 2 MINUTES

One story that shows where you have come from and where you are now.

Tell one meaningful past event or experience. Use a specific Past Simple story, then connect it to what you have learned, changed or prepared since.

EXPERIENCE→SPECIFIC EVENT→RESULT→IMPACT NOW→NEXT STEP
02:00

Strong B1

I’ve had several experiences that have helped me understand what I want next. One important example was the end of my previous role. I worked in process analysis for six years, and my position ended after a reorganisation. At first, the change was unexpected, but I decided to use the time positively. Since then, I’ve improved my English, prepared more seriously for Australia and thought about the type of role I want. The experience has made me more flexible and more confident about making a change.

B2 / VTest target

Several experiences have shaped the way I’m approaching my next step, but one of the most significant was the end of my previous role. I spent six years in process analysis before my position ended as part of a wider reorganisation. Rather than rushing into another role, I chose to use the transition productively. Since then, I’ve strengthened my English, clarified the value I can bring to a team and prepared more actively for opportunities in Australia. The experience has taught me that an unexpected change can also create space for a more deliberate next move.
