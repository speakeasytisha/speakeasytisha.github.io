01 · THE BIG MAP

Before choosing a verb, find the time.

English tenses become easier when you ask one question first: Where is the action on the timeline?

NOW / ROUTINE

Present Simple

facts, habits, permanent situations

I work well with different teams.usually · often · every day · generally
FINISHED PAST

Past Simple

finished action + finished time

I worked there for six years.yesterday · last… · ago · in 2024
PAST → NOW

Present Perfect

past action connected to now

I have improved my English.since · for · already · yet · ever · never
FUTURE

Future forms

plans, decisions, possibilities

I’m going to move to Australia.next… · soon · in the future
🚦 Not yet: Past Perfect and Past Perfect Continuous are deliberately outside today's lesson. Present Perfect Continuous will only be introduced after the simple forms below are secure.
Aide FR : Cherchez d'abord le repère de temps. Ne choisissez pas un temps parce qu'il « sonne bien ».
02 · VERB LAB 1

Present Simple — facts, routines and professional strengths

When do you use it?

  • A fact: I speak French and English.
  • A routine: I train several times a week.
  • A skill or characteristic: I adapt quickly.
  • A general preference: I prefer practical solutions.
PositiveI/you/we/they workhe/she/it works
NegativeI/you/we/they don't workhe/she/it doesn't work
QuestionDo you work…?Does it work…?
KEY RULE: After do / does / don't / doesn't, use the BASE VERB. ✓ Does he work? ✗ Does he works?

03 · VERB LAB 2

Past Simple — tell a finished professional story

Use it when the action AND the time are finished.

I worked at Decathlon for six years. That six-year chapter is finished.

My role ended after a reorganisation. The event is finished.

Positiveregular: work → workedirregular: go → went
Negativedidn't + base verbI didn't leave immediately.
QuestionDid + subject + base verb?Why did you leave?
KEY RULE: DID carries the past. ✓ Did you work? ✗ Did you worked?

High-value irregular verbs for your story

be → was/werehave → hadgo → wentmake → madetake → tookfind → foundleave → leftlearn → learnt/learnedbecome → becamebuild → builtchoose → chosemeet → met

04 · VERB LAB 3

Present Perfect Simple — the bridge from past to now

SUBJECT+HAVE / HAS+PAST PARTICIPLE

Three uses you need now

  1. Experience, no finished date: I have worked with different teams.
  2. Change/result now: I have improved my English.
  3. Situation from past to now: I have had more time to prepare since leaving my role.
PositiveI/you/we/they have workedhe/she/it has worked
NegativeI haven't finished.He hasn't decided.
QuestionHave you worked abroad?Has it changed?

Past participles you need

work → workedimprove → improvedbe → beendo → donego → gonehave → hadmake → madetake → takenlearn → learnt/learnedchoose → chosenbuild → builtfind → found
SINCE

starting point

since June / since leaving my role
FOR

duration

for three months / for six years
ALREADY

earlier than expected

I have already started.
YET

questions / negatives

I haven't decided yet.
EVER / NEVER

life experience

Have you ever worked abroad?

Past Simple vs Present Perfect

I worked there for six years. → finished job.

I have developed strong problem-solving skills. → the result matters now.

Finished time word? yesterday / last year / in 2024 → normally Past Simple.

Since leaving my role, I ___ more time to work on my English.

I ___ Decathlon in 2026.

05 · VERB LAB 4

Used to — show how life was different before

Use used to + base verb for a repeated past habit or a past situation that is no longer true.

PositiveI used to work in process analysis.
NegativeI didn't use to work outdoors.
QuestionDid you use to travel for work?
WATCH THE SPELLING: after DID / DIDN'T → use to, not “used to”.

Which is correct?

Compare your old routine with the kind of experience you are open to in Australia.

06 · VERB LAB 5

Question Builder — know which helper to use

BE

Move BE before the subject.

You are ready. → Are you ready?
DO / DOES

Present Simple ordinary verb.

You work here. → Do you work here?
DID

Past Simple ordinary verb.

You worked there. → Did you work there?
HAVE / HAS

Present Perfect.

You have applied. → Have you applied?

What kind of role ___ you looking for?

What ___ you learn in your previous role?

Write 4 useful questions for an Australian recruiter or employer.

07 · VERB LAB 6

Future choices — going to, will, might, Present Continuous

PLAN / INTENTION

be going to

I’m going to spend time in Australia.

decision already made
DECISION / PROMISE

will

I’ll adapt quickly.

instant decision, promise, prediction
POSSIBILITY

might

I might try a hands-on role.

possible, not certain
ARRANGEMENT

Present Continuous

I’m meeting a recruiter on Friday.

fixed arrangement
VERB FORMS: am/is/are going to + base verb · will + base verb · might + base verb · am/is/are + verb-ing.

You have a real intention to go to Australia. Which is best?

You are open to practical work, but it is not certain.

Write one plan, one possibility and one promise about Australia.

08 · KEYWORD DECODER

Spot the clue before you touch the verb.

usually / often / every…→ Present Simple
yesterday / last… / …ago / in + finished year→ Past Simple
since / for / already / yet / ever / never→ often Present Perfect
next… / soon / plan / intention→ future form
