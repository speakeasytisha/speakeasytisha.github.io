01 · PAST SIMPLE · RULES FIRST

Finished time = Past Simple.

Use the Past Simple when the event belongs to a finished past period. The length of the action does not matter. The key is that the time is closed.

PAST SIMPLEfinished action + finished time

yesterday · last week · two years ago · in 2022 · when I was at university · from 2018 to 2024

AFFIRMATIVE

Subject + past form

worked · studied · planned

went · made · chose · took

The team changed the process last year.
NEGATIVE

Subject + did not + base verb

didn't work, not “didn't worked”

The company didn't replace the tool in 2022.
QUESTION

Did + subject + base verb?

Did they move…?

Did your responsibilities change over time?
BE

was / were — no “did”

I/he/she/it was · you/we/they were

Were the expectations clear?

Affirmative markers

yesterday · last… · …ago · in 2022 · then · after that

Negative markers

didn't … yesterday · didn't … last week · wasn't / weren't

Question markers

When did…? · Why did…? · Where did…? · Was…? · Were…?

High-value irregular verbs

Rule check · 8 questions

Before the larger bank, make sure the structure itself is clear.

Aide FR : Did porte déjà le passé. Après did / didn't, revenez à la base verbale. Avec be, utilisez directement was / were.

03 · PRESENT PERFECT · RULES FIRST

Past + connection to now = Present Perfect.

Use the Present Perfect for experience with no finished date, a result that matters now, an unfinished period, or a situation that began before now and still connects to the present.

PRESENT PERFECThave / has + past participle

since · for · already · yet · ever · never · just · recently · so far · this week

AFFIRMATIVE

have/has + participle

I/you/we/they have worked

he/she/it has worked

The market has changed recently.
NEGATIVE

haven't / hasn't + participle

The recruiter hasn't replied yet.
QUESTION

Have/Has + subject + participle?

Have they discussed Melbourne yet?
EXPERIENCE

ever / never

Has she ever worked abroad?
He has never lived in Australia.

Affirmative markers

already · just · recently · so far · since · for · this week

Negative markers

not … yet · never · hasn't / haven't … since

Question markers

Have…ever? · Has…yet? · How long have…? · What have…so far?

SINCEstarting point

since Monday · since 2024 · since the reorganisation

FORduration

for two months · for six years · for a long time

ALREADYearlier than expected

has already replied

YETuntil now

hasn't replied yet / Has she replied yet?

Do not combine Present Perfect with a finished past time: ✗ “I have gone there last year.” → ✓ “I went there last year.”

Rule check · 8 questions

Secure have/has, participles and the signal words before the larger bank.

Aide FR : Le Present Perfect regarde le passé depuis le présent. Il ne donne normalement pas une date passée terminée.

09 · PUT IT TO USE · SPEAK + WRITE

Move from grammar accuracy to your real story.

SPEAKING 1 · PAST SIMPLE

Your studies + early career

Speak about where you studied, what you studied, how long it lasted, what you learned, and how you entered your professional life.

Must use: at least 5 Past Simple verbs + one did question you could ask someone with a similar background.
01:30

Strong B1

I studied for several years before I started working full time. During my studies, I learned how to analyse information and organise my ideas. After I graduated, I looked for a role where I could use those skills. My first professional experiences helped me understand what kind of work I enjoyed.

B2 / VTest target

I completed my studies before moving into full-time work. The course developed my analytical approach and taught me to structure information clearly. Once I graduated, I started building practical experience and gradually became more interested in roles where I could understand how organisations worked and improve the way teams operated.

SPEAKING 2 · MIXED

Your previous job → your transition now

Explain what you did during your six years in process analysis, how the role ended, and what you have done since then to prepare for your next chapter in Australia.

Switch deliberately: finished job period = Past Simple; since then / recently / so far = Present Perfect.
01:30

Strong B1

I worked as a process analyst for six years. I analysed workflows and worked with different teams. My role ended after a reorganisation. Since then, I’ve improved my English, prepared more seriously for Australia and thought carefully about the kind of role I want next.

B2 / VTest target

I spent six years in process analysis, where I focused on understanding workflows and improving how teams worked together. My role ended as part of a wider reorganisation. Since then, I’ve used the transition productively: I’ve strengthened my English, refined the way I present my experience and prepared more actively for the Australian job market.

WRITING · 110–140 WORDS

An Australian recruiter asks:

“Could you briefly explain what you did in your previous role and what you have done since it ended?”
0 words

Strong B1

I worked as a process analyst for six years. I analysed workflows, spoke with different teams and looked for practical ways to improve processes. My role ended because of a reorganisation. Since then, I’ve focused on improving my English and preparing for Australia. I’ve also thought more carefully about the type of role I want and the skills I can bring to a new team. So far, the transition has helped me clarify my goals and become more confident about the next step.

B2 / VTest target

I spent six years working in process analysis, where I examined how workflows operated in practice and collaborated with different teams to make them clearer and more efficient. My position ended following a wider reorganisation, which gave me an opportunity to reconsider my next step. Since then, I’ve used the transition productively: I’ve strengthened my English, refined the way I present my experience and prepared more actively for opportunities in Australia.

Recruiter-facing Australia story: keep the focus on finding a role, contributing, adapting and gaining international experience. Your longer-term private project does not need to be part of the recruitment story.
