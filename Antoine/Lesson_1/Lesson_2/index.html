03 · PRECISION SPRINT

Past, present or future?

Your recurring errors are not about ideas. They happen when the tense has to be chosen quickly.

Choisissez le temps selon le repère temporel, puis automatisez la structure.

PAST SIMPLEfinished timeI worked in finance for two years.
PRESENT PERFECTexperience / link to nowI have worked with international teams.
PRESENTnow / routineI train twice a day.
FUTUREplan / arrangementI’m going to look for work in Australia.
Say it again. After each correction, repeat the complete sentence aloud. Accuracy becomes useful only when it becomes automatic.

08 · TRANSFERABLE SKILLS

Sell the value, not the job title

Your next employer may not care what a process analyst is. Make the value obvious in ordinary English.

analyse a process→ spot problems quickly
work with business + tech teams→ communicate with different people
automation→ look for faster, safer ways to work
finance responsibility→ be accurate and reliable
international teams→ adapt to different communication styles
high-level sport→ show discipline, stamina and consistency

45-second job pitch

Situation: You walk into a business that needs temporary staff. The manager asks: “So, what kind of work are you looking for?”

1. flexible target2. previous value3. physical readiness4. availability5. question back
Reveal two model answers

B1 model

I’m quite flexible about the type of job. I worked for several years in a large international company, where I analysed problems and worked with different teams. I’m reliable, organised and used to learning new things quickly. I also train a lot, so physical work doesn’t worry me. I’m available to start quickly. What kind of person are you looking for?

B2 model

I’m deliberately keeping my options open because I’d like to experience a different kind of work while I’m in Australia. My background is in process analysis, so I’m used to identifying problems, following procedures and working with people from very different teams. Outside work, I train twice a day, which has taught me consistency and resilience. I’m happy to start in a hands-on role and learn on the job. What would success look like in the first few weeks?

Although my previous work was office-based, I’m highly active outside work and used to demanding physical training. I would therefore be comfortable considering farm, logistics, site-support or other operational positions. I’m reliable, adaptable and keen to learn on the job rather than limit myself to my previous field. I’ll be available to start shortly after arriving.
