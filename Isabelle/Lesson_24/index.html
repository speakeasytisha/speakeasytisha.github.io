Netherlands interview reality. EURES notes that Dutch interviews commonly focus on motivation, strengths and development areas supported by examples, and candidates are normally given time to ask questions. Dutch government business guidance also describes workplace communication as direct. This lesson trains that exact balance: direct + evidence-based + warm.

FR : Les entretiens néerlandais mettent souvent l’accent sur votre motivation, vos qualités et vos axes de progression avec des exemples concrets. La communication professionnelle est généralement directe : votre objectif est donc d’être claire, factuelle et chaleureuse.

Part 1 · Professional positioning

Your 90-second value story

Do not recite your CV. Give the interviewer a map: who you are → what makes you different → proof → where you want to contribute next.

1 · Identity+2 · Dual value+3 · Evidence+4 · Direction
1

Identity

I am a real-estate legal and commercial professional with over 24 years’ experience in the French property sector.

Je suis une professionnelle de l’immobilier avec une double expertise juridique et commerciale et plus de 24 ans d’expérience.

2

Distinctive value

What makes my profile distinctive is that I combine legal judgement, transaction experience and commercial awareness.

Ce qui distingue mon profil, c’est la combinaison du jugement juridique, de l’expérience transactionnelle et d’une vision commerciale.

3

Evidence

I have negotiated development contracts, supported operational teams, managed property sales and coordinated with notaries, lawyers, investors and other stakeholders.

J’ai négocié des contrats de promotion, accompagné les équipes opérationnelles, piloté des ventes et coordonné de nombreux interlocuteurs.

4

Direction

I am now looking to bring this cross-functional experience to an international real-estate environment in the Netherlands.

Je souhaite aujourd’hui mettre cette expérience transversale au service d’un environnement immobilier international aux Pays-Bas.

B1+ model — clear and safe

I am a real estate legal and commercial professional with over 24 years of experience in France. I worked for major property developers in legal roles and I also spent ten years in sales management. I have experience in contracts, negotiations, property development, risk prevention and client relations. I am now looking for a new challenge in the Netherlands, where I would like to use both my legal and commercial skills in an international environment.

B2 model — stronger positioning

I am a real-estate legal and commercial professional with more than 24 years’ experience across property development, legal advisory work and sales management. What makes my profile distinctive is the ability to look at a project from both a legal-risk and a commercial perspective. In my previous roles, I negotiated development contracts, supported operational teams, managed property disposals and worked closely with notaries, lawyers, investors and technical partners. I am now looking to bring that cross-functional experience to a Dutch or international property organisation where I can contribute quickly while continuing to develop my knowledge of the local market.

Part 4 · Grammar that changes how you sound

Three B2 tools for interviews

How to use these exercises. First try the sentence on your own. If you need support, click Hint: the page will show you the base verb or useful clue only — never the conjugated answer. Your job is to choose the correct form.

FR : Essayez d’abord seule. Si besoin, cliquez sur Hint : vous verrez uniquement le verbe à l’infinitif ou un indice, jamais la réponse conjuguée.

01

Career overview vs finished experience

Present perfect for career experience with relevance now; past simple for finished jobs, dates and specific projects.

Present perfecthave/has + past participle

I have negotiated many complex real-estate agreements.

I have developed strong stakeholder-management skills.

Past simpleverb-ed / irregular past

I worked at Eiffage from 2017 to 2025.

I managed a sales team for ten years.

FR : Present perfect = bilan/expérience encore pertinente aujourd’hui. Past simple = période terminée, date précise, projet précis.

02

Explain how you achieved something: by + -ing

Use by + verb-ing to explain your method. It makes an answer more precise and professional.

Action

I reduced legal uncertainty...

Method

...by clarifying responsibilities early.

Impact

This helped the team move forward with a clearer risk position.

FR : by + -ing = « en faisant… ». Très utile pour montrer votre méthode, et pas seulement le résultat.

03

Turn a possible weakness into credible transferability

Use while / although + reality, then strength + action. Never deny the gap; show how you manage it.

While my legal experience is rooted in the French market, the core skills are transferable: contract analysis, negotiation, risk assessment and stakeholder coordination. I would combine that experience with focused learning of Dutch practice.

FR : Reconnaître la réalité → montrer ce qui est transférable → expliquer votre plan d’adaptation.

Part 5 · Evidence storytelling

STARR — the story behind your expertise

A strong interviewer does not only want adjectives such as rigorous or adaptable. They want evidence. Use one concrete situation to prove the quality.

SSituationContext — 1–2 sentences
TTaskYour responsibility
AActionWhat YOU did — longest part
RResultWhat changed / improved
RReflectionWhat it proves / what you learned

Build your real story

B1+ model

In one property-development project, there was a disagreement about contractual responsibilities. My role was to analyse the contract and help the operational team find a safe solution. I reviewed the documents, discussed the issue with the different parties and proposed clearer wording. We reached an agreement and the project could continue. This experience showed me that I can stay calm and practical when legal and commercial interests are different.

B2 model

On a property-development project, a contractual disagreement created uncertainty about the allocation of responsibilities between several parties. I was asked to secure the legal position without blocking operational progress. I reviewed the relevant agreements, identified the key exposure, clarified the points that were genuinely negotiable and coordinated discussions with the operational team and external stakeholders. By reframing the discussion around the project risk rather than individual positions, we were able to reach workable wording and move forward with greater contractual clarity. The experience reinforced the importance of combining legal rigour with a pragmatic understanding of the business objective.

Part 8 · Two-way interview

Ask questions that make you sound senior

“Do you have any questions for us?” is not the end of the interview. It is another opportunity to show judgement, curiosity and professional maturity.

ROLE

Success

What would success look like in the first six months in this role?

À quoi ressemblerait une prise de poste réussie au cours des six premiers mois ?

IF THE INTERVIEWER ASKS YOU THIS QUESTION

B1+ model
For me, success in the first six months would mean understanding the team, the main processes and the Dutch property context, while also becoming useful as quickly as possible. I would like to build good relationships with the people I work with, understand the priorities of the role and gradually take responsibility for my own projects.

B2 model
I would define success in the first six months in three stages. First, I would want to understand the team, the internal decision-making process and the Dutch market context. Second, I would aim to become a reliable contributor on live matters by using my existing legal, commercial and stakeholder-management experience. By the end of that period, I would hope to be handling my responsibilities with increasing autonomy, while having built strong working relationships and a clear plan for the local knowledge I still need to deepen.

Coach: Cette question peut surprendre parce qu’elle semble demander de « prédire l’avenir ». Isabelle n’a pas besoin de promettre des résultats précis : elle doit montrer qu’elle sait observer → apprendre → contribuer → devenir autonome.

TEAM

Collaboration

Which internal teams and external stakeholders would I work with most closely?

Avec quelles équipes internes et quels interlocuteurs externes travaillerais-je le plus étroitement ?

MARKET

Dutch context

Which aspects of the Dutch market would be most important for someone with my international background to learn first?

Quels aspects du marché néerlandais seraient prioritaires à maîtriser pour une personne venant d’un parcours international ?

PRIORITIES

Challenges

What are the main challenges the team is trying to solve at the moment?

Quels sont les principaux défis que l’équipe cherche à résoudre actuellement ?

CULTURE

Decision-making

How are decisions typically made across legal, commercial and operational teams?

Comment les décisions sont-elles généralement prises entre les équipes juridiques, commerciales et opérationnelles ?

NEXT STEP

Process

What would the next stage of the recruitment process involve?

En quoi consisterait la prochaine étape du processus de recrutement ?

CHOOSE 3

Select the three questions that best fit the interview. Then ask them aloud without reading word-for-word.

Part 10 · Written follow-up

Leave the interview professionally

Scenario

You have just completed a first interview for a property-related role. Write a short message that thanks the interviewer, confirms your interest and refers to one relevant point from the conversation.

Target

70–100 words · clear subject · warm but concise · no repetition of your entire CV.

B1+ model

Subject: Thank you — Real Estate Legal Role

Dear [Name],

Thank you for taking the time to speak with me today. I enjoyed learning more about the role and the way your team works with different property stakeholders. Our discussion confirmed my interest in the position, particularly because it combines legal analysis, negotiation and business support. I would be very pleased to continue the recruitment process and provide any additional information you may need.

Kind regards,
Isabelle Davion

B2 model

Subject: Thank you — Legal & Business Affairs interview

Dear [Name],

Thank you for the conversation today. I particularly appreciated learning more about the way the team balances legal risk, transaction priorities and stakeholder coordination. That combination strongly reflects the cross-functional approach I have developed throughout my career in property development and sales. Our discussion reinforced my interest in the opportunity, and I would be pleased to continue the process. Please do not hesitate to contact me if any further information would be helpful.

Kind regards,
Isabelle Davion
