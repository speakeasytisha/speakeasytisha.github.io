03 · Competency questions

STAR without sounding rehearsed

Target: 75–90 sec
SSituation

Give only the context needed.

TTask

What was your responsibility?

AAction

Spend most time here: what did you do?

RResult

What changed, improved or was learned?

FR : Pour éviter une réponse trop longue : 15 % situation, 10 % tâche, 55 % actions, 20 % résultat / apprentissage.

04 · Listening + follow-up pressure

When the recruiter pushes for precision

Pause. Confirm the question → give one precise example → finish with the result or lesson.
05 · Development area

Position your Dutch positively and credibly

Development, not weakness

Acknowledge

Do not pretend Dutch is irrelevant.

Show action

Explain how you are actively improving it.

Protect credibility

Do not claim a level you do not have.

Return to value

Explain what you can contribute while learning.

FR : Il ne faut ni minimiser le néerlandais ni se dévaloriser. Reconnaître le besoin, montrer un plan d’apprentissage réel et expliquer ce que vous pouvez déjà apporter professionnellement en anglais.
06 · Grammar repair

Three B2 tools that change how you sound

Immediate feedback
A

Present perfect for relevant experience

have/has + past participle for experience connected to now.

I have worked with many stakeholders throughout my career.

B

Conditional for diplomatic answers

would + base verb to sound measured and professional.

I would approach the issue by clarifying the priorities first.

C

Contrast without sounding negative

Although / While / However to acknowledge a limitation and pivot.

Although my Dutch is still developing, I am actively studying it.

FR : Chaque exercice contient un bouton indice et le verbe / mot à utiliser. Le verbe n’est volontairement pas conjugué dans l’indice.
07 · Listening under pressure

Hear it, reformulate it, answer it

No autoplay

Listen first. Then say what the recruiter is really asking before you answer. Transcript and French help are available only when you choose to reveal them.

08 · Unexpected questions

Questions that can throw you off guard

1Do not rush

A two-second pause is professional.

2Name your angle

“The first thing that comes to mind is…”

3Give evidence

Use one real example, not three vague claims.

4Close the loop

Connect the answer to how you work today.

Click the button for a surprise question.
FR : Prenez deux secondes avant de répondre. Commencez par une idée principale, donnez un exemple, puis terminez en reliant l’exemple à votre façon de travailler aujourd’hui.
09 · Reverse interview

Ask intelligent questions at the end

Prepare 4–5

A strong candidate does not end with “No, I think you answered everything.” Choose questions that reveal expectations, priorities, team culture and the first months in the role.

FR : Vos questions doivent vous aider à comprendre le poste et montrer votre réflexion : priorités, réussite, équipe, difficultés actuelles et prise de décision.
10 · Closing

Finish with motivation, value and confidence

Target: 30–45 sec
Motivation+Your value+Fit with the environment+Positive next step
FR : La conclusion ne doit pas répéter tout l’entretien. Gardez un message final : motivation + valeur + intégration + prochaine étape positive.
