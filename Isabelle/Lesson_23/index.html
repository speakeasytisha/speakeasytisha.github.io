🌟 Part A — Your professional identity in 30 seconds

Before an interview, you need one clear professional identity. Do not explain everything. Start with a strong, simple frame.

Core version

I am a real estate legal and commercial professional with over 24 years of experience in the French property sector. My background combines legal advisory work, property development, contract negotiation, risk analysis, sales management and stakeholder coordination.

One-line networking version

I combine legal real-estate expertise with commercial experience and strong stakeholder coordination skills.

⭐ Part G — STAR achievement simulator

Use one strong achievement to answer several interview questions: complex transaction, time pressure, stakeholders, negotiation and risk prevention.

Hero story: senior serviced-residence transaction

Situation: A complex senior serviced-residence project with multiple stakeholders and a very short timeline.

Task: Finalise key off-plan sale and lease documentation before the deadline.

Action: Coordinate stakeholders, negotiate actively, prioritise legal risks and follow up closely.

Result: The transaction moved forward on time and supported the business unit’s objectives.
