01 Numbers — the four situations

04 Ready phrases

  • Let me read that back to make sure I have everything correctly. Je relis pour vérifier.
  • Could you spell that for me, please? Pourriez-vous épeler, s'il vous plaît ?
  • Is that B for Bravo or D for Delta? C'est B comme Bravo ou D comme Delta ?
  • The line isn't very clear — could you repeat that, please? La ligne n'est pas très claire — pourriez-vous répéter ?
  • Could you speak a little more slowly, please? Pourriez-vous parler un peu plus lentement ?
Before every call: pick one phone number, one postcode, one email and one price from the lesson and say them out loud once. Thirty seconds of warm-up is usually enough.
