Dutch professional culture

Clear does not mean cold

🇫🇷 French habit

More context, more formal distance, more indirect nuance, longer explanations before the main point.

🇳🇱 Useful Dutch habit

Be polite, but get to the point quickly. Say what you want, why it is relevant and what the next step could be.

✅ Your best balance

Warm opening + clear professional objective + concrete request + easy next step.

Golden formula: Thank you → clear purpose → one relevant value → one clear next step.

FR : Merci → objectif clair → une valeur professionnelle pertinente → une prochaine étape claire.
