From First Reply to First Interview

Your quick-reference sheet for recruiter replies, phone calls and follow-ups in your Netherlands real-estate job search.
Lesson date: 29/07/2026 Trainer: Tisha Douty-Dosiere Focus: Reply · Call · Follow-up

Your golden formula

Thank you → clear purpose → one relevant value → one clear next step.

FR : Merci → objectif clair → une valeur professionnelle pertinente → une prochaine étape claire.

Recognise the reply — reconnaître le type de message

TypeWhat it usually looks likeYour quick reaction
Positive They ask a question or propose a next step. Thank them, answer directly, confirm the next step.
Neutral They acknowledge receipt without commitment. Keep it short, add one relevant detail, stay available.
Refusal Polite decline, sometimes vague reasons. Thank them, ask to stay in touch for future roles.
Request for CV They ask you to resend or update documents. Send promptly, confirm format, add one sentence of context.
Call invitation They propose a day/time for a call. Confirm the slot, or propose an alternative clearly.
Dutch-language concern They ask about your Dutch level. State your real level honestly, add your learning plan.

Clarify safely on the phone — clarifier sans stress

Ask for repetition

  • Could you repeat that, please?Pourriez-vous répéter, s'il vous plaît ?
  • Sorry, could you say that more slowly?Pardon, pourriez-vous parler plus lentement ?
  • Could you spell that for me?Pourriez-vous épeler, s'il vous plaît ?

Confirm & recover

  • Just to confirm, you said Tuesday at 2pm?Pour confirmer, vous avez dit mardi à 14h ?
  • Could you send that in writing / by email?Pourriez-vous me l'envoyer par écrit / par e-mail ?
  • Let me make sure I understood correctly.Laissez-moi vérifier que j'ai bien compris.

Handle the call: your 60–90 second pitch — votre présentation orale

1 · Profile

Your real-estate background, ~24 years, plus your legal experience.

2 · Target role

The type of role you're looking for in the Netherlands.

3 · Dutch

Your honest current level and your learning plan.

4 · Salary

A realistic range, stated with confidence.

5 · Availability

Your real start date or notice period.

Follow up — relancer

After a call

Thank you for the call today. As discussed, I'm sending my updated CV — I look forward to hearing about the next step.

Merci pour l'appel. Comme convenu, je vous envoie mon CV à jour — j'attends avec intérêt les prochaines étapes.

After sending your CV

I wanted to follow up on the CV I sent last week. I remain very interested in this opportunity.

Je me permets de relancer au sujet du CV envoyé la semaine dernière. Je reste très intéressée.

After no reply

I hope this finds you well. I wanted to check whether there's any update on my application.

J'espère que vous allez bien. Je me permets de demander s'il y a du nouveau concernant ma candidature.

After a refusal

Thank you for letting me know, and for your time. I'd welcome staying in touch for future opportunities.

Merci de m'avoir informée, et pour votre temps. Je serais ravie de rester en contact pour de futures opportunités.
