Part 1

🔁 Tense mirror — one subject, different times

The vocabulary stays similar. The structure changes because the meaning changes.

Important correction

I like travelling is not present continuous. It is present simple because like describes a general preference. The -ing word is a noun idea after like.

« I like travelling » n’est pas du présent continu. C’est du présent simple, car like exprime une préférence générale.

MeaningStructureSame subject: travelSame subject: work
Routine / factPresent simpleI travel for work every month.I prepare orders every week.
PreferencePresent simple + like + -ingI like travelling.I like working with clear instructions.
Happening now / temporaryPresent continuousI am travelling to Lisbon today.I am preparing an order now.
Finished pastPast simpleI travelled to Lisbon last month.I prepared the order yesterday.
Experience / result nowPresent perfectI have travelled to Portugal several times.I have prepared many airline orders.
Action in progress in the pastPast continuousI was travelling when the client called.I was preparing the order when my wife called.

Part 2

🧭 A / An / The / Ø — the article compass

Before a noun, ask: Is it one? Is it specific? Is it general?

A / AN

one, general, first mention

There is a hotel in the city centre.

I received an order this morning.

On utilise a/an quand c’est une chose, pas encore précise.

THE

specific, known, second mention

The hotel is excellent.

The order is for three passengers.

On utilise the quand on sait de quoi on parle.

Ø NOTHING

general plural / general uncountable

Airlines need clear labels.

Food safety is important.

On ne met rien avec les pluriels généraux et les noms indénombrables généraux.

Rule 1 · A or An?

Use a before a consonant sound: a hotel, a client, a meal.

Use an before a vowel sound: an order, an aircraft, an email.

The sound is important, not only the letter.

Rule 2 · First mention → second mention

First mention: a / an

Second mention: the

There is a hotel near the airport. The hotel is excellent.

Rule 3 · The = specific

Use the when both people know the thing.

The delivery is at 7:45. The client confirmed the order.

Rule 4 · Nothing = general

Use no article with general plural nouns and general uncountable nouns.

Airlines need labels. English is important. Food safety is essential.

Useful exceptions and exam traps

  • Meals: I have breakfast / lunch / dinner. But: The breakfast was excellent.
  • Languages: I speak English. But: The English in this document is clear.
  • Cities and countries: most single cities and countries take no article: Lisbon, Portugal, France. Use the with names that include a group/common word or a plural idea: the United States, the United Kingdom, the United Arab Emirates, the Netherlands.
  • Work: I am at work. I go to work. But: The work is difficult today.
  • Superlatives and ordinals: the best option, the most important detail, the first order.

Rule 5 · Cities and countries

Most cities and single-name countries use no article.

I live in France. We are moving to Portugal. The client is in Lisbon.

Use THE when the country name contains a group word or a common noun, for example: states, kingdom, republic, emirates, or when the name is plural.

the United States · the United Kingdom · the United Arab Emirates · the Netherlands · the Czech Republic

Part 3

👤 I / Me / Myself — who does what?

Use the pronoun according to its job in the sentence.

Quick translation and job in the sentence

The form changes because the job changes: subject, object or reflexive/emphasis.

La forme change parce que le rôle change : sujet, complément ou réfléchi / insistance.
EnglishFrench ideaJobSimple testExample
Ijesubject: the person does the actionI + verbI prepared the order.
meme / moiobject: the action goes to the personverb / preposition + meThe client called me. / They sent it to me.
myselfme / moi-mêmesame person or emphasisI ... myselfI introduced myself. / I checked it myself.

1 · I = subject

Use I when the person does the action.

I prepared the order.

My daughter and I are moving to Lisbon.

I = je. C’est le sujet : la personne qui fait l’action.

2 · Me = object

Use me after a verb or after a preposition.

The client called me.

They sent the order to me.

The client called my daughter and me.

Me = me / moi. C’est le complément : l’action arrive à cette personne.

3 · Myself = me / moi-même

Use myself when the subject and object are the same person, or to say “alone / without help”.

I introduced myself.

I prepared the order myself.

Do not use myself instead of I or me.

Myself = me / moi-même. Même personne, ou pour insister : je l’ai fait moi-même.

My daughter and I OR my daughter and me?

My daughter and I = subject. The two people do the action.

My daughter and I are going to Lisbon. → I am going to Lisbon.

My daughter and me = object. The action goes to the two people.

The client called my daughter and me. → The client called me.

Professional rule: put the other person first. Use my daughter and I / my daughter and me, not me and my daughter in professional writing.

Astuce : enlevez “my daughter and”. Si la phrase devient “I am...”, utilisez my daughter and I. Si la phrase devient “called me”, utilisez my daughter and me.

Easy test

Remove the other person and check what sounds correct.

My daughter and ___ are moving. → I am moving. → My daughter and I are moving.

The client called my daughter and ___. → The client called me. → The client called my daughter and me.

Part 4

⏳ Past continuous — a gentle introduction

Use the past continuous for an action that was in progress at a moment in the past.

long action in progressshort action interrupts

I was preparing the order when my wife called.

Affirmative

Subject + was / were + verb-ing

I was preparing an order.

We were checking the labels.

Negative

Subject + was / were + not + verb-ing

I was not driving.

We were not working at the restaurant.

Questions

Was / Were + subject + verb-ing?

Were you preparing the order?

Was the client waiting?

With when / while

When often introduces the short action.

While often introduces the long action.

I was driving when the client called.

While I was driving, the client called.
