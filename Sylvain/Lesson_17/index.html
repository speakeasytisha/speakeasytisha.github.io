04

When / While — not only “short” and “long”

This is the key clarification from the last lesson.

WHEN = at the moment / at the time / event

Use when to introduce the moment something happened, or a period in your life.

  • I was preparing an order when the client called.
  • When I lived in France, I worked in catering.

Important: when can introduce a long period too. So it is not only “short action”.

WHILE = during the same time

Use while to show that two actions or situations happened at the same time.

  • The client called while I was preparing the order.
  • While I was looking for a house, I was also preparing for interviews.

Useful extra meaning: while can also show contrast: While I like the house, it is too expensive.

when + past simple
when the client called
while + past continuous
while I was preparing
when + life period
when I lived in France
while + contrast
while I understand, I disagree
FR : when répond souvent à “à quel moment ? / quand ?”. while insiste sur “pendant que / en même temps que”. Ce n’est donc pas seulement court contre long.

05

Narrative grammar bridge

Use the tenses together to explain a real situation clearly.

Past continuous · action in progress in the past

Use: for the background action at a specific past moment.

Subject + was / were + verb-ing

Affirmative

I was preparing the order.
We were checking the labels.

Negative

I was not driving.
We were not speaking Portuguese yet.

Questions

Were you working?
What were you doing?

Avoid: ❌ Did you were working? → ✅ Were you working?

Past simple + past continuous

Past continuous = background. Past simple = event / interruption / next action.

I was preparing the order when the client called.

Meaning: first, you were in the middle of preparing the order. Then, the client called.

Past perfect · before another past action

Use: when you need to show that one past action happened before another past action.

Subject + had + past participle

Affirmative

I had checked the labels before the driver arrived.

Negative

I had not received the address before I left.

Question

Had you confirmed the time before the delivery?

Use it only when the order of events is important. If the order is clear, past simple can be enough.

Past perfect continuous · duration before a past moment

Use: when an action continued for a period of time before a past event or result.

Subject + had been + verb-ing

Affirmative

I had been looking for a house for several weeks before we found this rental.

Negative

I had not been working in Portugal before we moved.

Question

How long had you been looking for a job before the interview?

This tense is useful, but not urgent to master today. Today, the objective is to recognise it and understand the meaning.

Speaking 1 · Explain a problem

Tell the story of a last-minute change at work. Use past continuous + past simple.

A2+ model

Yesterday, I was preparing an order when the client called. The client changed the delivery time. First, I checked the new information. Then, I changed the labels. Finally, I sent the confirmation.

B1 model

Yesterday, I was preparing an airline order when the client called to change the delivery time. I had already checked the quantities, but I needed to verify the labels again because one passenger had an allergy. Finally, I updated the order and sent a clear confirmation to the client.

Speaking 2 · Job interview answer

Answer: What kind of job are you looking for near Lisbon, and why?

A2+ model

I am looking for a job near Lisbon. I have experience in catering, organisation and client service. I would like to work in a professional company because I like quality and clear communication.

B1 model

I am looking for a position near Lisbon where I can use my experience in catering, logistics and client service. I have managed orders, deadlines and last-minute changes, so I can work carefully under pressure. I would like to join a professional team where quality, organisation and communication are important.

Writing lab · professional and personal

Use grammar + connectors + useful vocabulary in realistic writing tasks.

Task 1 · Professional email after a last-minute change

Situation: A client changed an order while you were preparing it. Write a short professional email to explain what happened, what you had already done, what you are checking now and what you will do next.

A2+ model

Hello,

Thank you for your message. I was preparing the order when I received your update. I had already checked the quantities, but I am checking the labels again now because there are new dietary requirements. I will send the final confirmation this afternoon.

Could you confirm the exact delivery point?

Best regards

B1 model

Hello,

Thank you for sending the updated order. I was preparing the meals when I received your message, so I immediately checked the new information. I had already confirmed the quantities, but I am now verifying each label carefully because some passengers have specific dietary requirements.

I will send the final confirmation this afternoon. Could you please confirm the exact delivery point and the name of the contact person at the airport?

Best regards

Task 2 · Personal message about settling near Lisbon

Situation: Write a message to a friend. Explain that you moved, what you have already done, what you were doing this week and what you are looking for now.

A2+ model

Hi,

We have moved near Lisbon and we are settling in. We have found a temporary rental home with a pool. This week, we were organising the house and the girls started school. Now, we are looking for a new home to buy, and I am also looking for a job.

It is a big change, but it is exciting.

B1 model

Hi,

We have moved to a town near Lisbon, and we are slowly settling into our new life. We have already found a temporary rental home with a pool, which is comfortable while we look for a house to buy. This week, we were organising the house, helping the girls start school and discovering the area.

At the moment, I am also looking for a new job. It is a big change, but I think it is a good opportunity for the family.
