01

Tense timeline — same subject, different meaning

Le même sujet (« I », « the client »…) change de sens selon le temps utilisé. Regarde d’abord le sens : routine, maintenant, passé fini, résultat, action en cours dans le passé, ou futur.

Six meanings, six forms

RoutineI usually check each order.Present simple
NowI am checking the labels now.Present continuous
Finished pastThe client changed the order yesterday.Past simple
Experience / resultI have already checked the quantities.Present perfect
Background past actionI was preparing the meals when the client called.Past continuous
Future actionI will send the confirmation today.Future with will

The problem-solving formula (used in every exercise)

1. Situation 2. Problem 3. Action 4. Result now 5. Next step

Example: Yesterday, I was preparing an order when the client called. The delivery time changed, so I checked the labels again. I have already confirmed the quantities, and I will send the final confirmation today.

03

Past continuous — full grammar

Le past continuous sert à dire ce qui était en train de se passer à un moment précis du passé. Idée en français : « j’étais en train de… ».

Use

Use the past continuous for an action that was in progress at a specific moment in the past.

French idea: “j’étais en train de…”

✅ Affirmative

Subject + was / were + verb-ing

I was preparing the order.

We were checking the labels.

❌ Negative

Subject + was / were + not + verb-ing

I was not driving.

We were not waiting at Gate 2.

❓ Questions

Was / Were + subject + verb-ing?

Were you working?

Was the driver waiting?

Was or were?

SubjectPast continuousExample
I / he / she / itwas + verb-ingI was preparing. The driver was waiting.
you / we / theywere + verb-ingYou were working. We were checking.

Important mistake to avoid

With was / were, do not use did for the question.

✅ Were you working when the client called?

❌ Did you were working when the client called?

Past continuous = background action

It was happening at that moment.

I was preparing the order...

Past simple = short event / interruption

It happened and interrupted the action.

...when the client called.

I was preparing the order when the client called.

We were checking the labels when the delivery time changed.

The driver was waiting at the airport when I sent the update.

04

When / while

WHEN introduit souvent l’action courte. WHILE introduit souvent l’action longue en cours.

WHEN + short action

I was preparing the order when the client called.

We were checking the labels when the delivery time changed.

WHILE + long action

The client called while I was preparing the order.

The delivery time changed while we were checking the labels.

05

Problem-solving language

Ces mots permettent d’expliquer les obligations, les règles, les conseils et les demandes polies.

have toobligation / necessary

You have to check the labels.

muststrong rule

You must respect food safety rules.

shouldadvice / good idea

You should confirm the delivery point.

couldpolite request / possibility

Could you confirm the time?

Light first conditional: If this happens, I will...

Use this structure for a real possible situation in the future.

If + present simple, will + base verb

If the flight is delayed, I will call the client.

If there is an allergy, I will check the ingredients.

06

Writing models — explain a problem professionally

Trois niveaux du même e-mail. Compare-les pour voir comment ajouter des connecteurs et des détails à mesure que le niveau monte.

Exam-style writing task

Situation: An airline client changed an order at the last minute. Write an email to confirm the change, explain what you were doing when the client called, what you have already checked, and what you will do next.

  • Write 100–150 words.
  • Use at least 4 connectors.
  • Use at least 3 tenses.
  • Ask one polite question.

A2 model

Hello,

Thank you for your message. I received the update for the order. I was preparing the meals when you called. I have already checked the quantities and the labels.

The delivery point is now Gate 4, so I will update the driver. I will also check the nut-free meal again before delivery.

Could you confirm the final delivery time?

Best regards

A2+ model

Hello,

Thank you for sending the updated order. I was preparing the meals when you called, so I could not answer immediately. I have already checked the quantities, the delivery point and the labels.

First, I will update the driver. Then, I will check the nut-free meal again because one passenger has an allergy. After that, I will send the final confirmation.

Could you please confirm the exact delivery time at Gate 4?

Best regards

B1 model

Hello,

Thank you for your update regarding tomorrow’s flight. I was preparing the order when you called, so I could not answer immediately. However, I have already checked the quantities, the new delivery point and the main dietary requirements.

First, I will inform the driver that the delivery point is now Gate 4. Then, I will verify the nut-free meal carefully to avoid any problem with the allergy. Finally, I will send you the final confirmation before delivery.

Could you please confirm whether the flight is now scheduled to leave 30 minutes later?

Best regards

07

Speaking models — explain what happened

Structure à suivre à l’oral : situation → problème → action → résultat maintenant → prochaine étape.

Oral prompt

Explain a problem at work and how you solved it.

Use this structure: situation → problem → action → result now → next step.

A2 oral model

Yesterday, I was preparing an order when the client called. The delivery point changed. I checked the labels and the quantities. I have already updated the driver. I will send the confirmation today.

A2+ oral model

Yesterday, I was preparing an order when the client called with an urgent update. The delivery point changed, and one passenger needed a nut-free meal. First, I checked the labels. Then, I contacted the driver. I have already checked the quantities, and I will confirm the delivery time this afternoon.

B1 oral model

Yesterday, I was preparing an airline order when the client called to change some details. The flight was delayed, the delivery point changed, and one passenger had a nut allergy. First, I checked the order again. Then, I verified every label and contacted the driver. I have already confirmed the quantities, and I will send the final confirmation today. If the flight time changes again, I will call the client immediately.

08

Final mission subjects — model answers

Quatre sujets possibles pour la mission finale. Utilise-les pour t’entraîner à l’oral et à l’écrit.

Subject 1 · Urgent order update

Write or say a message to confirm a last-minute change in an airline order.

Model answer

I was checking the order when the client sent an urgent update. The delivery point changed, and one passenger needed a special meal. First, I checked each label. Then, I contacted the driver. I have already confirmed the quantities, and I will send the final confirmation today.

Subject 2 · Missed call

Explain why you missed a call and what you will do next.

Model answer

I am sorry I missed your call. I was preparing an order when you called, and I could not answer immediately. I have already finished the urgent part. I will call you back in 20 minutes because I need to confirm one delivery detail first.

Subject 3 · Delivery problem

Explain a delivery delay and propose a solution.

Model answer

The driver was waiting at the airport when the flight time changed. First, I checked the new schedule. Then, I contacted the client to confirm the delivery point. If the flight is delayed again, I will update the driver immediately.

Subject 4 · Food safety issue

Explain how you managed an allergy or special meal requirement.

Model answer

We were preparing the meals when we noticed a special dietary requirement. One passenger needed a nut-free meal, so I checked every ingredient carefully. We have already labelled the meal in English. Food safety is essential, so I will verify the order again before delivery.
