Present Perfect Foundations

Life experience · Results now · Already / yet · For / since · Past simple contrast

01

The big idea

Use the present perfect when the past is connected to now.

Past experience

I have worked with airline clients.

FR : J’ai travaillé avec des clients dans l’aérien. L’expérience existe dans ma vie.

Result now

I have sent the confirmation.

FR : J’ai envoyé la confirmation. Le résultat est important maintenant.

Unfinished time

We have prepared three orders this week.

FR : Cette semaine n’est pas terminée. On parle d’un résultat jusqu’à maintenant.

Duration until now

I have worked in catering for many years.

FR : L’action a commencé dans le passé et continue maintenant.

Important: Do not use the present perfect with a finished past time like yesterday, last week or in 2020. Use the past simple.

✅ I travelled last week. · ✅ I have travelled to Portugal. · ❌ I have travelled last week.

02

Same subject · different tense

The example changes, but the subject stays similar so the meaning is easier to see.

Present simple

routine / fact

I work with airline clients.

Present continuous

now / temporary

I am working on an order today.

Past simple

finished past

I worked on an order yesterday.

Present perfect

past connected to now

I have worked with airline clients before.

Past continuous

past action in progress

I was working when the client called.

03

How to make the present perfect

Affirmative · negative · questions · short answers

✅ Affirmative

Subject + have / has + past participle

  • I have prepared the order.
  • You have sent the confirmation.
  • We have worked with airline clients.
  • The client has changed the order.

FR : sujet + have/has + participe passé. Ex. “J’ai préparé…”

❌ Negative

Subject + have / has + not + past participle

  • I have not checked the labels yet.
  • We haven’t received the final details.
  • The client hasn’t confirmed the time.

FR : haven’t / hasn’t = n’ai pas / n’a pas encore, selon le contexte.

❓ Questions

Have / Has + subject + past participle?

  • Have you prepared the order?
  • Have you ever worked with an airline?
  • Has the client confirmed the delivery time?

FR : on met have/has devant le sujet pour poser une question.

SubjectAuxiliaryExample
I / you / we / theyhaveI have prepared the order.
he / she / ithasThe client has confirmed the time.

Key rule

After have / has, use the past participle, not the base verb.

✅ I have prepared the order. · ❌ I have prepare the order.

05

When to use it

Four clear situations.

1 · Experience

ever · never · before

Have you ever worked with international clients?

I have worked with airline clients before.

FR : expérience dans la vie, sans date précise.

2 · Result now

just · already · yet

I have already checked the labels.

The client hasn’t confirmed the time yet.

FR : le résultat compte maintenant.

3 · Unfinished time

today · this week · this month · this year

We have prepared three orders this week.

I have spoken to two clients today.

FR : la période continue encore.

4 · Duration until now

for + duration · since + start point

I have worked in catering for many years.

I have lived in France since 2004.

FR : commencé dans le passé, vrai maintenant.

FOR

for + period / duration

for two years · for many years · for three months

SINCE

since + starting point

since 2024 · since Monday · since January

06

Practice from easy to harder

Each exercise gives feedback immediately. Use the hint if needed.

Exercise 1 · Choose have or has

Use have with I / you / we / they. Use has with he / she / it.

1. I ___ prepared the order.

2. The client ___ changed the delivery time.

3. We ___ received the final details.

4. The aircraft ___ had a technical problem.

Exercise 2 · Choose the past participle

After have / has, use the past participle: prepared, sent, written, spoken, been.

1. I have ___ the confirmation.

2. You have ___ a professional message.

3. We have ___ the meals.

4. I have ___ to the client.

Exercise 3 · Make the sentence negative

Use haven’t / hasn’t + past participle. Do not use don’t / doesn’t here.

1. I have checked the labels yet. →

2. The client has confirmed the time yet. →

3. We have received the final order yet. →

Exercise 4 · Make questions

Move have / has before the subject. Have you...? Has the client...?

1. You have checked the labels. →

2. The client has confirmed the time. →

3. They have sent the order. →

Exercise 5 · Ever and never

Ever is common in questions. Never means “not in my life”.

1. Have you ___ worked with a British client?

2. I have ___ visited the United Kingdom.

3. Has the airline ___ changed an order at the last minute?

4. We have ___ had a problem with this client.

Exercise 6 · Just, already or yet

Just = a moment ago. Already = done before now. Yet = questions and negatives.

1. I have ___ received the order. It arrived five minutes ago.

2. We have ___ checked the labels, so they are ready.

3. Have you confirmed the delivery point ___?

4. The client hasn’t answered ___.

Exercise 7 · For or since

For + duration. Since + starting point.

1. I have worked in catering ___ many years.

2. We have lived in France ___ 2004.

3. I have known this client ___ six months.

4. The order has been ready ___ 7 a.m.

Exercise 8 · Past simple or present perfect?

Finished time = past simple. No exact finished time / result now / experience = present perfect.

1. I ___ to Portugal last year.

2. I ___ to Portugal several times.

3. I ___ the confirmation yesterday.

4. I ___ the confirmation, so you can check it now.

Exercise 9 · Been or gone?

Been to = went and came back. Gone to = went and is there now.

1. I have ___ to Lisbon twice. I know the city.

2. The driver has ___ to the airport. He is there now.

3. Have you ever ___ to the United States?

Exercise 10 · Integrated paragraph

Use present perfect for results now, past simple for yesterday, and connectors to organise the paragraph.

Complete the paragraph with the best words: received · have checked · hasn’t confirmed · sent · yet · already · yesterday

07

Oral practice · VTest style

Speak in short, clear answers first. Then add details.

Round 1 · Experience questions

Have you ever worked with international clients?

Yes, I have. I have worked with airline clients, and I have prepared food for international passengers.

Round 2 · Results now

Have you checked the labels yet?

Yes, I have. I have already checked the labels, but I haven’t sent the final confirmation yet.

Round 3 · Duration

How long have you worked in catering?

I have worked in catering for many years. I have also developed experience with airline clients.

Round 4 · Past simple contrast

When did you start working with airline clients?

I started a few years ago, when a company contacted me for a catering service in France.

08

Writing lab · from simple to professional

Scenario: An airline client asks for an update about an order.

Realistic scenario

You received an updated order from an airline client. You have checked the labels and prepared some of the meals, but the client has not confirmed the exact delivery point yet. Write a short professional update.

  • Say what you have already done.
  • Say what has not been confirmed yet.
  • Ask one clear question.
  • Use at least two present perfect sentences.

A2 model

Hello,

Thank you for your message. I have received the updated order. I have checked the labels and prepared the cold meals. The client has not confirmed the delivery point yet.

Could you confirm the exact place at the airport?

Best regards,

A2+ model

Hello,

Thank you for your updated order. I have already checked each label, and we have prepared the cold meals. However, we have not received the exact delivery point yet.

Could you please confirm the delivery point and the contact person at the airport?

Best regards,

B1 model

Hello,

Thank you for sending the updated order. We have already checked the dietary requirements and prepared the cold meals. I have also reviewed each label carefully, especially for allergies.

However, we haven’t received the exact delivery point yet, so I cannot finalise the delivery instructions. Could you please confirm the delivery point and the name of the contact person at the airport?

Best regards,
