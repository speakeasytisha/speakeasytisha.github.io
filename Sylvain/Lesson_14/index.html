Task 1 — Describe the image

Minimum 40 words. Describe what you see — the people, the place, the actions.

Words:0· minimum 40
Show model answers (A2 / A2+ / B1)
A2In the picture, I can see a kitchen. There are two chefs. One chef is cooking. The other chef is checking the trays. The trays are for a plane.
A2+In this picture, two chefs are working in a professional kitchen. One is preparing food on the stove, and the other is organising meal trays for a flight. The kitchen looks clean and well organised.
B1The image shows two chefs working together in a busy commercial kitchen. While one chef is cooking on the stove, the other is carefully checking the meal trays before they are loaded for an upcoming flight. Everything looks organised, which suggests the team is used to working under time pressure.

Task 2 — Give your opinion

Minimum 150 words. Answer the question below with clear examples.

Writing prompt
"Moving to another country for work is always a positive experience." Do you agree or disagree? Give reasons and examples.
Words:0· minimum 150
Show model answers (A2 / A2+ / B1)
A2I don't agree. Moving to another country is not always easy. It can be difficult for children at school. But it can also be a good chance for the family. My family is moving to Lisbon and I think it will be good, but also a little difficult at the beginning.
A2+In my opinion, moving abroad is not always positive. It depends on the family and the reasons for moving. For example, my family is moving to Lisbon because of my job and because we want a better lifestyle. I think it will be positive for us, but I know it will take time to adapt, especially for my daughters at school.
B1I partly agree with this statement, but I don't think it is always true. Moving abroad can bring many advantages, such as better career opportunities, a different lifestyle, or a safer environment for children. In my own case, my family is relocating to Lisbon because of my work in catering, and we also hope for a fairer tax system as a self-employed worker. However, the experience isn't always easy. Children often need time to adapt to a new school and a new language, and adults can feel isolated before they build a new network. In conclusion, I believe moving abroad is usually a positive experience in the long term, but only if the family prepares carefully and stays patient during the first few months.
