02 · HOW WE WILL WORK

Speak first. Notice. Improve. Reuse.

We will not study grammar for grammar’s sake. We will use grammar and vocabulary to make something you actually want to say clearer and stronger.

Nous partons d’une situation concrète. Une difficulté devient ensuite un petit point de langue, puis vous réutilisez immédiatement la nouvelle structure à l’oral ou à l’écrit.

1

Speak

You answer naturally with the English you have today.

2

3

Learn

4

5

Reuse

You use the new language again in a real conversation or task.

Your mistakes are useful information. They show us what to practise next. The goal is progress, not perfection.

05 · YOUR FIRST POWER TOOL

Tell a past story clearly.

Your initial work shows that you can already tell a story. The fastest improvement now is to make the past tense more stable.

A

Finished past → Past Simple

If the action is finished, use the past form.

Subject + past verb + …

go → went

spend → spent

have → had

work → worked

B

Be in the past → was / were

I / he / she / it → was
you / we / they → were

I was there for a year.

The people were very friendly.

They were happy.

Pour raconter une expérience terminée : go → went, spend → spent, et attention à was / were. Avec people et they, on utilise were.

06 · SMALL WORDS, BIG DIFFERENCE

to · in · at

TO

destination / movement

go to Vanuatu
travel to Australia

IN

inside a place / country / city

live in France
stay in Nouméa

AT

workplace / point / event

work at a factory
meet at the airport

to = mouvement vers une destination · in = à l’intérieur d’un pays/ville · at = lieu précis, point ou lieu de travail.

07 · SOUND MORE NATURAL

Say exactly what you mean.

Sometimes the grammar is understandable, but English uses a different everyday phrase. Learning these small “chunks” gives you more fluency.

UnderstandableMore naturalWhy?
“I did it with my husband and my son.”“I went there with my husband and my son.”Use a precise verb.
“I went there for working.”“I went there to work.”Purpose → to + verb.
“factory of water”“a water factory / a water bottling plant”English noun combinations.
“They have no money.”“They had very little money.”Keep the past story consistent.
