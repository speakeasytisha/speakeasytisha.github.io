01 / UNDERSTAND THE EXAM

A clear route, one mission at a time

The aim is not to memorize a speech. It's to respond naturally using short, clear sentences and to keep the conversation moving.

01

Welcome & interact

Introduce yourself, identify a need, offer help.

02

Understand & reformulate

Read or listen, then pass on the essential facts.

03

Transmit & solve

Give instructions, explain changes, suggest solutions.

04

Analyze & give your view

Use information, give an opinion and a reason.

Which LILATE format?

Examiner: a live guided exchange in four parts, up to 60 minutes. AI: online assessment with timed multiple-choice and open oral/written responses; around 30 minutes, potentially longer depending on the adaptive format.

Grammar toolkit · build accurate sentences

Present simple · habits

I work with an association. / I don't work alone. / Do you organize events?

Habitudes et faits : sujet + verbe ; don't + base verbale ; Do + sujet + verbe ?

Past simple · completed actions

We organized an event last year. / We didn't cancel it. / Did you attend?

Passé terminé : -ed ou verbe irrégulier ; après did/didn't, verbe à la base.

Future · plans & arrangements

We're going to prepare a meeting. / We're meeting on Friday. / I'll send the information.

Going to : intention ; présent continu : arrangement fixé ; will : décision / offre.

Polite questions · requests

Could you tell me the date? / Would you like more details?

Could you + verbe de base ? Would you like + nom / to + verbe ?

Connectors · longer answers

because = parce que · so = donc · however = cependant · although = bien que · first = d'abord · finally = enfin

Because introduit une raison ; so introduit un résultat ; however marque un contraste.

Pronunciation · clarity

worked /t/ · played /d/ · wanted /ɪd/; clubs /z/ · projects /s/ · changes /ɪz/.

Prononcer les sons finaux : ils portent parfois une information grammaticale essentielle.
