01 · WATERWAYS

The Canal de l’Àghjara

Built in 1875 near Bastelica. Used to irrigate agricultural land and supply fountains. Selected for heritage support in 2025. The term sluice gate fits its water-control structures.

The Canal de la Gravona

Near Ajaccio and linked to Napoleon III, this nineteenth-century canal supplied the city with water. It is a different project, supported by the 2018 heritage lottery.

The communal bread oven
