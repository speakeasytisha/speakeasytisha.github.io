YOUR FIRST 30-SECOND STORY

Say it in four steps

1 · Place: I spent a year in…

2 · Morning: I worked from… until…

3 · Afternoon: Afterwards, I…

4 · Evening: In the evening, I…

I spent a year in Vanuatu with my son. I worked from seven until one, then I went swimming. In the evening, I helped in the snack bar my husband and I had created.
