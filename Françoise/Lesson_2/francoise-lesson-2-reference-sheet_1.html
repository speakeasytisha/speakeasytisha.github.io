BUILD IT AGAIN

Your 5 building blocks

  1. Who you areI’m… / I live…
  2. Your present lifeI’m involved in… / I’m part of…
  3. Your experienceI lived… / I worked…
  4. Why English mattersI want to… / I’d like to… because…
  5. Your learning preferenceI learn best when… / I learn best by + -ing

I’d like to = « j’aimerais ». Après to, verbe à la base : I’d like to speak (jamais to speaking). Learn best by + -ing = « j’apprends mieux en… » (watching, reading, listening, doing).

LIFE NOW

Present simple vs present continuous

PRESENT SIMPLE

Routine · fact · regular situation

I / you / we / they work
he / she / it works

  • I live in Corsica.
  • I belong to a volleyball association.
  • We sometimes use English.

Signals: usually, often, sometimes, every week

PRESENT CONTINUOUS

Now · temporary · changing situation

am / is / are + verb-ing

  • I am taking English lessons now.
  • We are preparing an event.
  • My English is improving.

Signals: now, at the moment, currently, this week

⚠️ Do not overuse the continuous.

State verbs stay in the present simple: know, like, want, need, understand, believe.

✅ I want to improve.   ❌ I am wanting to improve.

Même si l’action semble « actuelle », certains verbes d’état restent au présent simple.

YOUR EXPERIENCE

Past simple — key verbs

go → wentI went to Australia. spend → spentI spent a year there. live → livedI lived in Australia. work → workedI worked in Germany. be → was / wereThe people were friendly. feel → feltI felt frustrated sometimes.

🔧 Precision upgrades from Lesson 2

❌ My trip was in Vanuatu.→✅ My trip was to Vanuatu.
❌ I go there to work.→✅ I went there to work.
❌ The people was friendly.→✅ The people were friendly.
❌ I spend one year there.→✅ I spent one year there.

Pour un événement terminé (« l’année en Australie », « le travail en Allemagne ») : toujours le prétérit, pas de -ing.

VOCABULARY

Words to keep active

Reread these once a day and use three in a spoken sentence about your life.

Introductions

👋

confident

à l’aise / confiant(e)

able to speak or act without too much doubt

Example: I’d like to feel more confident when I speak English.

💬

to introduce yourself

se présenter

to tell someone who you are

Example: Let me introduce myself. I live in Corsica.

🎯

goal

objectif

something you want to achieve

Example: My main goal is to speak more spontaneously.

🧩

background

parcours / contexte

your experience, education or history

Example: I have an international background.

🤝

to be part of

faire partie de

to belong to a group or organisation

Example: I’m part of an international volleyball association.

Place & Corsica

🏝️

island

île

land completely surrounded by water

Example: Corsica is an island in the Mediterranean.

🌊

coast

côte / littoral

the land next to the sea

Example: Many towns are located on the coast.

⛰️

mountainous

montagneux / montagneuse

having many mountains

Example: Corsica is a very mountainous island.

🧭

located

situé(e)

in a particular position or place

Example: Corsica is located south-east of mainland France.

🌿

inland

à l’intérieur des terres

away from the coast

Example: The landscape becomes more mountainous inland.

Travel & experience

🇦🇺

abroad

à l’étranger

in or to another country

Example: Living abroad taught me to adapt.

🧳

to get by

se débrouiller

to manage with what you have

Example: I could get by in English, but I wanted to say more.

😣

frustrated

frustré(e)

annoyed because you cannot do what you want

Example: I felt frustrated when I could not express my ideas.

🌱

experience

expérience / vécu

knowledge gained by living through something

Example: Living in Australia was an important experience.

Association & volleyball

🏐

member

membre

a person who belongs to a group

Example: Our association has members from different countries.

🌐

international

international(e)

involving more than one country

Example: It is an international sports association.

✉️

correspondence

correspondance

letters or emails exchanged with someone

Example: Sometimes I have to write correspondence in English.

🤝

to coordinate

coordonner

to organise people so they work well together

Example: English helps us coordinate with international members.
