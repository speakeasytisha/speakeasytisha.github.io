03 · GRAMMAR TOOL 1

Build a natural introduction

12 min

Use 5 building blocks

  1. Who you areI’m… / I live…
  2. Your present lifeI’m involved in… / I’m part of…
  3. Your experienceI lived… / I worked…
  4. Why English mattersI want to… because…
  5. Your learning preferenceI learn best when…

Useful grammar

PurposeStructure
IdentityI am / I’m
PlaceI live in Corsica.
MembershipI’m part of an association.
Past experienceI lived / worked in…
GoalI’d like to + verb

I’d like to = « j’aimerais ». Après to, utilisez toujours le verbe à la base : I’d like to speak, pas to speaking.

04 · GRAMMAR TOOL 2

Where are you? in · on · at · to · from

12 min
NESW

Think: type of place → choose the preposition

in a country, city, region, area · on an island / coast · at a precise point · to a destination · from an origin

in = dans une zone ; on = sur une île / côte / surface ; at = à un point précis ; to = vers une destination ; from = provenance.

🏝️ onI live on an island.
📍 inI live in Corsica.
🌊 onAjaccio is on the west coast.
🧭 inIt is in the south-west of the island.
✈️ toI travelled to Australia.

🧭 Useful direction phrases

in the north ofin the south ofin the east ofin the west ofon the coastinlandnearbetween

Say “Corsica is in the Mediterranean” but “I live on an island.” Both are correct because the noun changes.

05 · GRAMMAR TOOL 3

What you usually do vs what is happening now

15 min
PRESENT SIMPLE

Routine · fact · regular situation

I / you / we / they work
he / she / it works

Examples

  • I live in Corsica.
  • I belong to a volleyball association.
  • We sometimes use English.
usuallyoftensometimesevery week
PRESENT CONTINUOUS

Now · temporary · changing situation

am / is / are + verb-ing

Examples

  • I am taking English lessons now.
  • We are preparing an international event.
  • My English is improving.
nowat the momentcurrentlythis week
⚠️ Do not overuse the continuous.

Some verbs normally describe a state, not an action: know, like, want, need, understand, believe.

✅ I want to improve.   ❌ I am wanting to improve.

Même si l’action semble « actuelle », certains verbes d’état restent au présent simple.

06 · GRAMMAR BRIDGE

Talk about what you’ve done without getting lost in the past

10–12 min

Finished past → Past simple

When the time or period is finished, use the past simple.

subject + past verb

Pour un événement terminé : prétérit. Gardez la structure courte et claire.

go → wentI went to Australia. spend → spentI spent a year there. live → livedI lived in Australia. work → workedI worked in Germany. be → was/wereThe people were friendly.

🔧 Precision upgrades

❌ My trip was in Vanuatu.→✅ My trip was to Vanuatu.
❌ I go there to work.→✅ I went there to work.
❌ The people was friendly.→✅ The people were friendly.
❌ I spend one year there.→✅ I spent one year there.
Practice

Quick past-tense check

2. Why did you go there?
