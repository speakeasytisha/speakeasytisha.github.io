Your homework: telling a travel story

Lesson 3 will build on your Australia mission and go further into the past simple, so you can tell a full travel story with confidence. Do this at your own pace — everything you type here is saved automatically in this browser.

01 · VOCABULARY PREVIEW

New words for your travel story

5 min

Read each word, listen to the example, then try to remember it without looking before Lesson 3.

🧳

journey

voyage / trajet

an act of travelling from one place to another

Example: It was a long journey, but it was worth it.

✈️

flight

vol

a journey made by plane

Example: My flight to Australia took almost 24 hours.

📍

to arrive

arriver

to reach a place at the end of a journey

Example: I arrived late in the evening, so I went straight to bed.

🏠

to stay

rester / séjourner

to live somewhere temporarily

Example: I stayed with a host family for the first two months.

🌄

adventure

aventure

an exciting or unusual experience

Example: The whole year felt like a real adventure.

✨

unforgettable

inoubliable

so good or special that you will always remember it

Example: Meeting people from so many countries was unforgettable.

💭

homesick

avoir le mal du pays

sad because you miss home while you are away

Example: I sometimes felt homesick during my first month.

🎁

souvenir

objet-souvenir (pas « un souvenir » en anglais)

a small object you keep to remember a place or trip

Example: I bought a small souvenir to remember my trip.

Faux ami : en anglais, souvenir = l’objet que vous rapportez. Pour « un souvenir » au sens du français (une mémoire), dites a memory.
