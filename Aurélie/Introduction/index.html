Meet your trainer · Tisha

I am a French–American English trainer with professional experience in training, communication and technology. I have worked in IT support, web design, and educational settings, where explaining things clearly and helping people solve real problems are essential. I also work with adults preparing for professional English certifications and real workplace conversations.

I came to France as a sixteen-year-old exchange student, so I know firsthand what it means to communicate in a new language. I teach with clear step-by-step explanations, French support when useful, examples, immediate feedback and plenty of opportunities to practise without pressure. My goal is to help you make English your own.

A simple 4-step reflex

  1. Notice the problem · “I can’t hear you.”
  2. Describe it · “Your image is frozen.”
  3. Ask for help · “Could you write it in the chat?”
  4. Try a solution · “I’ll leave and rejoin.”

More detail · English voices and browser compatibility

Windows 11: Settings → Time & language → Language & region → Add a language → English (United Kingdom) or English (United States). Install speech features if available. In some versions, additional voices can be managed through Accessibility or Narrator settings. Then restart the browser.

macOS: System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System Voice → Manage Voices (the wording may vary by version). Download an English UK or US voice, then restart the browser.

Your available voices depend on your browser and operating system. If the requested accent is not installed, the page will use another available English voice.
