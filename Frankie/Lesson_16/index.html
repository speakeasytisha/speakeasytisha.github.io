Sound clear when you speak

Past endings · Listen for /t/ in checked, /d/ in called and an extra syllable /ɪd/ in needed.

Final -s · Listen for /s/ in checks, /z/ in calls and an extra syllable /ɪz/ in changes.

Stress the important words · In “Could you send the signed form by Thursday at noon?”, stress the item and the deadline. Pause briefly before confirming the next step.

06

WRITING STUDIO · MESSAGE WITH PURPOSE

Writing coaching

Read the situation, identify who will read your message and plan what they need to do next. These original tasks practise workplace writing described in the CLOE framework.

How to structure a professional email
  1. Subject · Name the action and the item. Updated form needed by Thursday.
  2. Greeting · Use Hi Maya, for a familiar colleague, Dear Ms Carter, for a formal named recipient, and Dear Support Team, for a team.
  3. Purpose · I’m writing to confirm… Je vous écris pour confirmer…
  4. Context · Say what happened and include only the details the reader needs. Use On Tuesday… for a finished event and We’re still waiting for… for a current situation.
  5. Action and deadline · Could you send the form by noon on Thursday? Pourriez-vous envoyer le formulaire d’ici jeudi midi ? By gives a deadline. Until says how long something continues.
  6. Close · Thank you for your help. Then Kind regards, or Best regards, and your name. In traditional UK formal correspondence, Yours sincerely follows a named greeting, and Yours faithfully follows Dear Sir or Madam.

REFLECTION · CELEBRATE THE SMALL WINS

Your progress

Notice what is becoming easier and choose one useful thing to practise again.

How to review your speaking and writing
  • Task completion · Did I respond to each instruction, including the follow-up?
  • Development · Did I add a reason, example or consequence where it helped?
  • Accuracy · Did I use the right time frame and build questions correctly?
  • Interaction and clarity · Did I ask for clarification, confirm details and pronounce key words clearly?
  • Writing · Did I use an appropriate tone and make the next action easy to find?

Choose one point to mark as secure, one that still needs practice and one sentence to say or write again.
