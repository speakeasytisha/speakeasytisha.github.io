Step 3

Grammar guide: which tenses can you use?

For a travel story, the most important tense is the Past Simple. You can also use a few other structures to describe places, background actions, life experience and future plans.

🕰️

1 · Past Simple: finished actions

Use it for: what you did during the trip.

Positive: subject + past verb
  • I travelled around France.
  • I visited small villages.
  • I parked the campervan near the campsite.
  • I rented a house with my family.
  • I ate local food at a market.
Rules and exceptions
  • Most verbs add -ed: visit → visited, park → parked.
  • If the verb ends in e, add d: arrive → arrived.
  • If the verb ends in consonant + y, change y to ied: carry → carried.
  • Short verb ending consonant-vowel-consonant: double the final consonant: stop → stopped.
  • Some verbs are irregular: go → went, have → had, see → saw, eat → ate, spend → spent, take → took.
❌

2 · Past Simple questions and negatives

Use did + base verb for questions and negatives.

Question: Did + subject + base verb?
Negative: subject + didn’t + base verb
  • Did you sleep in the campervan?
  • Did you rent a house?
  • I didn’t stay in a hotel.
  • I didn’t drive every day.
⚠️ Say Did you go? not Did you went?
🏕️

3 · There was / There were: describe a place

Use it for: what existed in a place.

Singular: There was + one thing
Plural: There were + many things
  • There was a small campsite near the river.
  • There was a parking area for campervans.
  • There were clean showers.
  • There were many families at the campsite.
🎬

4 · Past Continuous: background action

Use it for: what was happening at a moment in the past.

Structure: was / were + verb-ing
  • I was driving when it started to rain.
  • We were looking for a place to park.
  • The children were playing outside.
  • While I was cooking, my family was relaxing.
When or while?

When often introduces a short action: I was driving when it started to rain.

While often introduces a longer background action: While we were driving, we listened to music.

🧭

5 · Present Perfect: life experience or result

Use it carefully. It is useful to say that you have the experience now.

Structure: have / has + past participle
  • I have travelled around France.
  • I have visited many beautiful places.
  • I have never slept in that campsite.
⚠️ With a finished time, use Past Simple: Last month, I visited Brittany. Not: Last month, I have visited Brittany.
➡️

6 · Future review: going to / will

Use going to for future plans. Use will for promises, offers or quick decisions.

  • I’m going to travel again next year.
  • I’m going to rent a house with my family.
  • I’ll send you the address.
  • Don’t worry, I’ll ask at reception.

Step 4

Tense detective: hear the key words

Before answering, listen for the question signal and the time marker.

Did…?Past Simplelast month, yesterday, during the trip, when you rented the house
Do / does…?Present Simpleusually, every year, in general, often
Are you + -ing?Present Continuousnow, today, this week, at the moment
Are you going to…?Future Planstomorrow, next week, next year, soon
Have you ever…?Present Perfectlife experience, no finished time

Step 5

Grammar exercises with hints

These exercises recycle the grammar you need to tell your road trip story.

🚐 Exercise A · Past Simple verb forms

Regular verbs often take -ed. Important irregular verbs: go → went, eat → ate, spend → spent, take → took.

🏕️ Exercise B · There was / There were

There was = one thing. There were = many things.

🎬 Exercise C · Past Simple or Past Continuous?

Use was/were + -ing for the background action. Use past simple for the short finished action.

🧭 Exercise D · Present Perfect or Past Simple?

Finished time words like last month need Past Simple. Have you ever…? and never before can use Present Perfect.

🧩 Exercise E · Put the words in order

Question order: Where + did + subject + base verb? Future order: I am going to + base verb.

Step 7

Listening: a campervan trip in France

Listen first without the transcript. Then answer the questions. Finally, reveal the transcript and listen again.

Transcript: Last month, I travelled around France in a campervan. I visited small towns, quiet campsites and beautiful places near the sea. Sometimes, I parked in a special campervan area, and sometimes I stayed at a campsite with showers and electricity. At one point, I rented a house because my children came on holiday with me. We cooked together, went to a market and spent time outside. While we were driving, we saw beautiful landscapes. It was a simple but very special trip.

1. Where did I travel?

Listen for the place after “travelled”.

2. Where did I sometimes park?

Listen for “special campervan…”

3. Why did I rent a house?

Listen for “because”.

4. What did we see while we were driving?

The question uses the past continuous phrase “while we were driving”.

Question bank for conversation

After your answer, ask a question back. This keeps the conversation going and prepares you for CLOE interaction.

🕰️ Past trip questions

  • Where did you go on your last holiday?
  • Did you travel by car?
  • Where did you stay?
  • What did you visit?
  • Did you enjoy it?

🏕️ Campsite questions

  • Do you like campsites?
  • Did you stay at a campsite?
  • Was there electricity?
  • Were there clean showers?
  • Would you like to go again?

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family holiday questions

  • Do you often travel with your family?
  • Did your children come on holiday?
  • What did you do together?
  • Are you going to plan another family holiday?
  • Why is family time important?

🚐 Campervan questions

  • Have you ever travelled in a campervan?
  • Do you prefer a campervan or a hotel?
  • Where can you park a campervan?
  • Was it easy to find a place to park?
  • What do you need in a campervan?
